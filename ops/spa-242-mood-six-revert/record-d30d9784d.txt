## Why #170 could not voice these
Popty's render route refused them with **EXISTS_IN_LANGUAGE** (`services/shared/exists-in-language-guard.cjs`, checked in `services/shared/audio-render-entry.cjs` renderClip). Recordings are kept per language, not per course: if the same Spanish words are already recorded in *any* voice, a new take in this course's own voice counts as a second performance and is refused. Here the only existing takes were off-cast Azure voices (spa_for_jpn, and old spa_for_eng rows). The intros were refused by the re-record gate (`services/shared/rerecord-gate.cjs`, 403 RERECORD_NOT_PERMITTED) for the same reason. The one sanctioned way past both is a re-record grant: Tom's permission row, or Kai's `kai:YYYY-MM-DD:<reason>` approval token on the request (`rerecordGranted`, rerecord-gate.cjs). #170 had neither; this job carried Kai's token, and every ask passed.
