## 3. "X wants Y to…" cut a different way in every seed

**(a)** The same construction (want / would like / hope / wish / insist + a different subject) is cut differently from seed to seed, so the learner never meets it as one pattern.

**(b)** Finnish before: 11 seeds cut 6 ways — whole chunk (S0015), clause only (S0171), head+että (`he wanted that | se halusi että`, S0238), että+clause (`you to believe | että sä uskot`, S0427), no LEGO at all (S0169). After (Kai, 21 Oct): first one or two occurrences present the whole chunk with *"which literally means…"*; thereafter head (`he wanted | se halusi`), `that | että` (not new), clause (`you tell me | sä kerrot mulle`). Italian (Kai, 21 Oct): che stays with the clause because it binds the subjunctive — `that you ask | che tu chieda`, never "you to ask".
