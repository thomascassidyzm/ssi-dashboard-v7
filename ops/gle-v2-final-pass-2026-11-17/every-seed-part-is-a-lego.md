Kai 12 Nov 2026: every part of a seed must be covered by a LEGO. If a piece was taught earlier, it STILL appears as a LEGO in this seed, marked not-new. Never accept a LEGO with a gap in it (e.g. "nuair atá … anseo"). Small pieces first, then the combined chunk. Present fix items hardest-first, one at a time, with the full seed and its current LEGOs.
**Why:** learner must be able to build the whole seed; gaps are undrillable.
**How to apply:** in every LEGO proposal/brief, any gle/spa/etc course.
Refined 12 Nov 16:40Z: a LEGO itself never has a gap, BUT wrap-round target constructions are fine: teach the frame contiguous first (tá … orm → 'tá áthas orm' / 'cuireann sé sceitimíní orm'), teach middle bits alone, then phrases slot middles into the frame. Prefer keeping the native reviewer's wording over rewording to avoid wraps.

NEVER use disambiguating labels on LEGOs (e.g. 'when? (asking)') — Kai 12 Nov: unacceptable. A would-be clash stays inside the bigger LEGO instead.
