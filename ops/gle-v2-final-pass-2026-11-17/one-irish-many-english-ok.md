One target-language word carrying several English meanings is fine: the learner hears the English and has only one answer to give. The problem is only the reverse — one English meaning with two target answers.

**Why:** prompts go English → target; only multiple valid answers confuse.
**How to apply:** never argue a split on "one word, many meanings"; split only when accurate and consistent (e.g. gle go leor → dóthain/a lán/mórán, ruled 12 Nov). Related: [[new-verb-for-known-concept-clash]].
