REMEMBER: RECALL IS WITTE, ÛNTHÂLDE IS MEMORISE (Kai 16 Nov 2026 on Auke's answers; jobs #108, #117; replaces the 11 Nov 'REMEMBER IS NEVER RECALL' rule, which no longer matches the live course). Recall, when something will not come back to mind, = witte + net mear, and its English cue is 'I can't remember' / 'I couldn't remember' (ik wit net mear wat ik sizze woe | I can't remember what I wanted to say; ik wit syn namme net mear | I can't remember his name). When nothing follows, the Frisian needs 'it': ik wit it net mear | I can't remember. Whether the English cue should also carry 'it' is open with Kai: do not add it on your own. A few lines already live say 'still know' / 'don't know ... any more' over witte (S0464L03 se wist it net mear | she didn't know it any more); keep a line consistent with its own seed. ûnthâlde = memorise, keep in your head, and its English is 'memorise' (ik sil it net maklik ûnthâlde kinne | I'm not going to be able to memorise it easily; it antwurd net ûnthâlde | not memorise the answer). ûnthâlde is NEVER recall and never carries 'can't remember'. Four lines still say 'remember' over ûnthâlde and are held for Auke (S0024L02B01, S0024L02B02, S0024L03U06, S0166L01U06): do not copy them. Known by heart = noch + kenne or witte: ik ken it noch (ik wit it noch is also fine). Stories and things learned by heart that a person knows = kenne (sy ken hûndert ferhalen). Searching for a word that won't come = op ... komme, English 'think of' (op in wurd komme | think of a word); ik tink oan in wurd means having a word in mind. Never write heuge or betinke. 'want to know' = wol witte and 'I still have to hear' = moat noch hearre: never write 'find out' over either. If a seed's English says 'remember', decide which it is, recall (witte ... net mear) or memorise (ûnthâlde), and post one line in chat naming the seed.
