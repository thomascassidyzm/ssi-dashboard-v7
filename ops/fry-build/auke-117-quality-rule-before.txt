REMEMBER IS NEVER RECALL (Kai 11 Nov 2026, on Auke's facts; jobs #166, #169): the English word 'remember' is written ONLY over ûnthâlde, and only in its keep-in-your-head sense: memorise, retain, not forget (ik kin nije wurden ûnthâlde | I can remember new words; sadat ik ûnthâlde kin | so I can remember; ik fyn it noflik as minsken myn namme ûnthâlde | I like it when people remember my name). NEVER write 'remember' for recalling something in the moment, and never write ûnthâlde for it: recall is witte, and its English is 'still know' (oft ik de hiele sin noch wit | if I still know the whole sentence) or 'don't know ... any more' (ik wit net mear wat ik sizze woe | I don't know what I wanted to say any more; se wist it net mear | she didn't know it any more). Searching for a word that won't come = op ... komme, English 'think of' (op in wurd komme | think of a word), never 'remember a word'. Never write betinke or heuge. 'want to know' = wol witte and 'I still have to hear' = moat noch hearre: never write 'find out' over either. Stories and things learned by heart that a person knows = kenne (sy ken hûndert ferhalen). If a seed's English says 'remember' and means recall, build it with witte and the English 'still know' / 'don't know ... any more', and post one line in chat naming the seed.
