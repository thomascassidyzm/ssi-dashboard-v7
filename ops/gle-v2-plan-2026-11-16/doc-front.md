## How to read the plan

- Part 1 is the plan, seed by seed, in the order it should be applied. Apply it front to back and nothing is ever used before it is taught: each seed's block adds its new pieces before it touches phrases, and a later copy only turns not-new in the same pass that makes the earlier copy new.
- "NEW" means this is where the piece is taught. "not-new" means it was taught earlier and is listed here so the whole sentence is covered. Not-new pieces never play and need no phrases.
- "(frame)" marks a wrap frame: the frame is taught whole, the middle is taught alone, and phrases slot the middle in (Kai, 12 November).
- Syllables are counted by vowel groups, the same way the final check counted them.
- Tags in the Change column: "ruled 12 Nov" is one of Kai's decisions; A1 to A8 and C2, C4, C5 are the final check's families; "every part is a LEGO" is the coverage rule.
- Part 1 holds the three families Kai has not ruled yet (A5 labels, A6 special forms, A7 clashes) only where the fix is a re-cut inside one seed. They are tagged, so any of them can be dropped without disturbing the rest. The summary document asks about each family.

## What is not in the plan

- **Seeds 301 to 668 have no LEGOs.** Only their sentences change here (15 of them). They are built later.
- **Fuller baskets.** Where a new piece ends up with fewer than 4 practice phrases (129 pieces), the plan does not invent Irish. It adds the seed's own sentence where needed so the piece can be introduced, and leaves the rest to be written at apply time. See open question 4: these are best written by the builder once it knows the Irish rules.
- **383 phrases are left in baskets that stop playing**, because their piece is now taught earlier and they use words the earlier seed does not have yet. They are harmless (not-new baskets never play). They can be deleted or re-homed later.
- **Phrase quality was not audited**, as in the final check. Eoghan's phrase proofread (delete-only) covers it.
- **A5, A6 and C families beyond the local re-cuts**: 11 label fixes, about 10 special-form LEGOs and the bare little words (C2) are listed in the summary for a ruling and are not cut here.
