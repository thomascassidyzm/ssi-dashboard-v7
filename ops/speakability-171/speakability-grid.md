# Speakability layers × Q/R/S × the metagraph — what a learner preparing for a conversation would hit

**This tags every canonical seed (668) and every line of the two canonical pods: the live pod-1 (Sarah's day, 231 lines) and the environment conversation (545 lines). Each one gets layer 0–6 and Q/R/S. Each pod line also gets its move in the flow and its metagraph shape and response move.** Found, not invented: every number comes from a tag on a real line, and every example is verbatim.

## The verdict in five lines

1. **The seeds are a statement bank.** Of the 668 seeds, 484 are S, 121 are Q and only 63 are R. Seeds 1–30 have no R at all. No seed answers anything at Meta, Sophisticated or Register level, and only 5 are responses that carry the speaker's own view (L3 × R). A learner can say what they think 111 ways but can barely agree, disagree or answer with a view.
2. **Repair is one-way.** Learners can ask for repair: 10 Meta questions, such as "Could you say that again a little more slowly?". But the seeds hold **zero Meta responses**. Nothing answers a comprehension check or a repair. Pod-1 carries the only examples, such as "Yes, I said it's over there." (g214).
3. **The flow moves are almost absent, even in the pods.** Live pod-1 has 4 reciprocal returns, all "…And you?" in one scene (g26, g29, g32, g34). The environment conversation has none. Both pods have no sympathy reactions, no invitations and no contact exchange, and only one how-are-you. The richer returns quoted in the archive ("And what about you?", "Enough about me, though — how's your mum doing?") come from the July 2026 16-scene draft `pod-1-english-canonical.md`. **That draft is not the live pod-1.**
4. **The metagraph is the service layer.** Every transactional shape sits ≥80% in L1: N2 Transaction, N3 Availability, N4 Instructions, P1 Option-choice, N8, N9 and N12. N10 Compliment sits in L2. For these shapes the layer is just a coordinate. Only the relational shapes cross layers: N1 Ritual (L1/L2), N5 Acquaintance, N11 Mutual assessment, N6 Repair and P3 Read-back. Above L2 the layers have almost no shape: L3 is 69/81 lines unshaped, L4 28/31, L5 336/350, and L6 has no pod lines at all. **13 store shapes appear in neither pod.** These include Premise audit, Digression-and-return, Story → matched story and The misreading corrected. Parked disagreement shows up once, and without the park.
5. **The missing shapes are named below, found in the pods.** They are: plain information question → answer ("What time is breakfast served?"), small-talk assessment ("It's hot today, again." / "It's not bad."), feeling report, own-fault trouble ("my son lost his ticket"), monologue + continuer, topic-offer and take-up, reframe-and-ratify, and session-closing without a farewell ritual.

So for conversation preparation, Tom's hunch holds. The six layers sit inside the metagraph as a coordinate on its shapes, but only for L1–L2. L0 repair is partly shaped (N6, N1201). L3–L5 are where the conversations Tom means happen, and there the store has almost nothing to walk.

## 6. How this was made, and the honest gaps

- **Sources (live, 2026-10-20):** seeds come from Popty `GET /api/canonical-seeds`; pods from `GET /api/pods/<course>/pod-1` for every `_for_eng` course and from `ita_for_eng/environment-conversation`; the shapes from the store at `services/shared/metagraph/` (origin/main 9331c78b7). For pod-1, the shapes and response moves come straight from the store's own pod-1 walk set and attestations. The store marks 93 rows as unplaced, and I tagged those against the store by hand. The other lines were tagged by three Sonnet workers (#175·U, #176·U, #177·U) under one codebook. Each line was tagged once, with no second tagger, so treat single-digit cells as ±a few.
- **Pod-1 exists in two editions.** 23 courses serve the 231-line Sarah's day; that is the slate the metagraph indexes and the one counted here. 17 courses (bul, cat, dan, ell, est, fas, heb, hye, lav, lit, nep, nor, pol, swa, tha, tur, ukr) still serve a **142-line older cut of the same story**. It was not tagged separately. deu_at (266 lines) and ara_sy and fin (232 each) were not tagged either.
- **Per-language variants were not judged one by one.** Each course carries small English edits to pod-1, 5–18 lines in the 231 edition. The worker gave those variant lines their modal line's tags without reading each one, so §4 of the builder's variant check is not shown.
- **The environment conversation was tagged one turn at a time.** All lines in one turn share a layer, Q/R/S, move and shape, so its move sequences are coarser than pod-1's.
- **Pod-1 drill scenes 15–21 and the 16 number/colour codas** are counted in the grid, but they are left out of the flows because they are not conversations.
- **Seed R/Q/S is judged by form**, because a seed has no context. A seed counts as R only if it starts like a reply (yes, no, thanks or agreement). That rule is strict, so R is undercounted for lines that could answer in context.
- The tag files and the builder (`build.py`, which regenerates this document) are on branch `cs/171-speakability-layers-x-q-r-s-tag-` under `ops/speakability-171/`.
