## 2. What the learner now expects
- Statements: dw i'n / dw i 'di / o'n i'n + verbnoun; short pasts wnes i / wnest ti / ges i / weles i / es i, and ddudodd o/hi/fy mam.
- Negatives: dw i ddim, wnes i ddim (gwneud), do'n i ddim, fedra i ddim, ddylwn i ddim, dydy X ddim yn, does neb.
- Questions: wyt ti …? / wnest ti …? / be' wnei di?, plus the tag tydy?. Yes/no answers met only as do + chunk; naddo is unannounced (S0115L02U14).
- "that" + clause = bo' fi / bo' ti / bod hi / fod o (the course never explains fod vs bod). Negative "that" = na (untaught, used about 10 times). y byddai once or twice.
- Time adverbs soft-mutate when "on": ddydd Gwener, fore dydd Sul, bnawn dydd Sul (always b-); nos Sadwrn/nos Wener; bore 'fory unmutated. S0114L02 drills the bore/fore contrast.
- yn + adjective mutates (yn dda, yn ddiddorol, yn gyflym), but not after eitha' (yn eitha' cyflym).
- Possessives: fy + nasal (mam, mrawd, nhad), dy + soft (fam, frawd, lyfr, Gymraeg). Never explained.
- "it" as an object is a lottery: ei fwynhau / ei mwynhau / ei gwylio hi / wylio hi / wylio fo / licio fo.
- Contractions are heard freely: be'/beth, 'di/wedi, 'fory/yfory, 'r after vowels.

## 5. New kinds of problem
- **known-form-new-sense**: a target word whose FORM was taught, now used in a MEANING never taught, so every untaught-word scan passes it. Examples: am = about (S0100L02B05, seed 103); and, possibly, faint for degree (S0100L02B01).
- **known-form-new-function**: a known little word reappears as a grammatical particle. Examples: y = "that" (S0115L02B03, S0116L02B04); a = relative "who" (S0115L02U16).
- **head-word-gloss**: the LEGO's English names one word and the target is the whole chunk. Examples: S0101L02 short | mewn amser byr; S0103L01 you feel | sut wyt ti'n teimlo?; S0106L02 a bit | am dipyn.
- **orphan-phrase**: a basket phrase that contains none of its LEGO. Examples: S0115L01 (15/15), S0112L02U01-U16, S0113L01.
- **gloss-bites-later**: a wrong pre-batch gloss that is harmless until a later seed needs the bare word. Examples: S0094L01 at seed 103; S0088L01 at S0103L01B06; S0097L01 do, ges i against bare ges i (S0101L02U06).
- **seed-part-untaught**: the SEED itself needs a form no LEGO covers, so the hint fires only later. Example: seed 114 wrth fy mam (the hint first fires at S0116L02B05).
