## 1. Seed 5: the one real decision

**Live seed:** I'm going to practise speaking with someone else = *voy a practicar hablando con otra persona*
**Its LEGOs:** L01 to practise = *practicar* · L02 I'm going to = *voy a* · L03 speaking = *hablando* · L04 with someone else = *con otra persona*. No intro line quotes the seed; they all have the plain shape "The Spanish for 'X', is…".

**The knock-on.** S0005L03 *hablando* is the only standalone teaching of the gerund anywhere in the course. **69 phrases outside this census are tiled on it**, across seeds 26 to 591. Examples: *disfruto hablando español* (S0073), *seguir hablando* (S0092, four rows), *estaba hablando con…* (S0083, S0232, S0233), *mejorar hablando* (S0044) and *como si estuviera hablando* (S0026). Canon K15 forbids adding a LEGO to rescue them. So if seed 5 loses *hablando*, all 69 rows have to be rewritten or deleted.

**"Para" at seed 5.** "Para" isn't taught before **S0026L04** *listo para irme*. It is also in live **S0027L03** *para contestar*, where the component *para* is glossed "in order to". #441·AI is changing this right now: it removes *para contestar* from seed 27 and, if you said Y to its item 2, adds *para* = "to" as a component of S0026L04. **This draft assumes "para" is usable from seed 27 on, whichever way #441 lands.** It first uses "para" at seed 29.

**Option B (recommended): keep the gerund and drop the bare frame.**
I'm going to practise Spanish by speaking with someone else = **voy a practicar español hablando con otra persona**
- LEGOs: L01–L04 unchanged; *español* gets listed as not-new (S0001L03), since every seed part is a LEGO. Zero changes to intros or LEGO text, and **0 of the 69 rows break**.
- With an object, the gerund means "by speaking", the ordinary instrumental use: *practicar un idioma hablando con…* is a normal way to say this ([MosaLingua](https://www.mosalingua.com/es/hablar-con-uno-mismo-para-ganar-fluidez/), [MyLanguageExchange](https://www.mylanguageexchange.com/Learn_spn/Spanish.asp); RAE on the gerund of manner: [DPD "gerundio"](https://www.rae.es/dpd/gerundio)). Deborah's "speech therapy" reading comes from the bare *practicar hablando*, where nothing is being practised except the act of speaking.
- On the English side, "by" is a glue word (free class, K1).

**Option A: your frame at seed 5.**
I'm going to speak with someone else to practise = **voy a hablar con otra persona para practicar**
- LEGOs: *voy a*, *hablar* (not-new, S0001L02), *con otra persona*, plus a **new M-LEGO "in order to practise" = *para practicar*** (components *para* "in order to" + *practicar*). This copies the S0027L03 precedent and avoids clashing with L01 "to practise" = *practicar*. It needs a new intro line, about 45 Charlotte chars.
- S0005L03 *hablando* is deleted. **The 69 tiled rows would need a second drafting pass that isn't done here.** On the plus side, every phrase in seeds 5–25 could then use *hablar para practicar* too.

Each one keeps the LEGO of its basket.

**Where the frame goes in C.** A time or frequency adverb comes before *para practicar* (*hablar lo más a menudo posible para practicar*), so that the purpose clause is last, as Spanish prefers. Longer clauses stay where they were (*…para practicar cuando hay otras personas aquí*). If you want "in order to practise" in the English (the S0027L03 gloss), it can be swapped in throughout. "To practise" is the simpler English and maps one-to-one.
