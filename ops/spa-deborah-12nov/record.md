## 6 · S0027L02 "taking too much time" → pasar demasiado tiempo (seed 27, plus seed 65)
**One verb, not three.** A phrase in this basket must contain the LEGO, so I can't give each phrase its own verb (estar / pasar / perder). I take **pasar**: it is Deborah's verb for "take too long doing something", which is what every row here means. Her *estar* reading ("stay too long somewhere") fits none of them; build 1 is rewritten to the doing sense. *pasar* is already a taught word (S0012L03 *lo que va a pasar*). Later, "to spend | pasar" (S0333L02) and "spending time | pasar tiempo" (S0567L02) agree with it. The LEGO English stays "taking too much time". The odd builds Deborah named (B03, B04) and the "-ing" rows move to "spending too much time … speaking", which is natural English. One Spanish for two English is fine under your rule. The reuses carrying the same wording are rewritten as well: S0027L03 ×6, S0028L01U08, S0071L03U14, S0084L01U09 and S0225L02U10.
**Ripple to seed 65:** once L02 leaves *tomar*, *tomar* is next taught at S0095L02 (take the bus). Seed 65 "Es importante tomar tiempo para ponerte a prueba", its draft copy S0065L02U09 and S0078L01U11 would then use an untaught word. So: the seed loses the glue and becomes "Es importante ponerte a prueba" (both LEGOs kept; the same text as B04), U09 is deleted, and U11 drops "take time".
**⚠ Flag, the weakest join:** the seed and 9 rows put *pasar demasiado tiempo* before **para contestar** (L03). Natural Spanish here would be a gerund (*pasar demasiado tiempo contestando*) or *tardar demasiado en contestar*. But re-cutting L03 to *contestando* would break the infinitive *contestar*, which about 30 later phrases in 22 LEGOs rely on. So *para contestar* is kept: it is the taste-safe default, and the one line worth asking Deborah before the apply: "¿suena natural *no me gusta pasar demasiado tiempo para contestar*?".
**Left alone, listed:** other "take time / a long time" calques with *tomar* at seeds ≥95 are a separate family, and Deborah's answer doesn't cover *llevar* or *tardar*: S0134L01U10, S0136L02U04, S0188L02U09 (*tomar demasiado de tu tiempo*), S0189L01U03, S0211L02U05, S0272L02U14, S0291L02U11.
