PER-LANGUAGE RULES (from the course's own seeds 1-300):

zho_for_eng (pinyin): lowercase; tone marks (ā á ǎ à; ü/ǘ…); neutral tone has no mark (le, de, ma, ne,
  men, zi in context, péngyou, xuésheng, dōngxi, rènshi, xǐhuan, zhīdào…), as the examples do.
  Words are JOINED (wǒmen, xǐhuan, zhīdào, yīqǐ); separate words take spaces. Measure words stand alone
  (yī gè, nà zhī). Tone SANDHI IS NOT WRITTEN: 一 = yī, 不 = bù always (bù shì, yī gè), 3-3 unchanged.
  The validator accepts sandhi spellings, but do NOT use them. Question tags: shì bu shì.
  Apostrophe before a/o/e syllable inside a word: nǚ'ér, Xī'ān.
  Polyphones decided in context: 得 de (complement) / děi (must) / dé (obtain); 了 le / liǎo (了解 liǎojiě, 受不了);
  行 xíng / háng; 还 hái / huán; 都 dōu / dū; 长 cháng / zhǎng; 重 zhòng / chóng; 觉 jué (觉得) / jiào (睡觉);
  着 zhe / zháo / zhuó; 地 de (adverbial) / dì; 便 biàn / pián; 只 zhǐ / zhī (measure); 为 wèi / wéi;
  少 shǎo / shào; 种 zhǒng / zhòng; 数 shù / shǔ; 好 hǎo / hào; 要 yào / yāo; 差 chà / chā / chāi; 教 jiāo / jiào;
  干 gàn / gān; 会 huì / kuài; 和 hé / huo (暖和); 发 fā / fà; 难 nán / nàn; 相 xiāng / xiàng; 应 yīng / yìng.
  Punctuation: SEEDS end with "." (statement) or "?" (question), ASCII. LEGOs carry no final punctuation.
  PHRASES: no final punctuation unless the target has a ？ (then "?"). Internal ，→ ", ".
  Chinese characters never appear in the roman. Latin letters in the target are copied as they are.

jpn_for_eng (romaji, the course's own Hepburn variant): lowercase. Particles: は = wa, を = o, へ = e.
  Long vowels DOUBLED, no macrons: おう/おお → oo (doo, kyoo, hoohoo, arigatoo), うう → uu (raishuu, juu),
  ええ → ee, katakana ー doubles the vowel (paatii, koohii); えい stays ei (sensei, eiga). But a verb's
  own -ou ending is o+u (omou, iu for 言う, kau): the doubling is for long vowels only.
  ん before a vowel or y: n' (hon'ya, kin'en). Small っ doubles the consonant (itte, motto); っち = tchi.
  Spacing: particles stand alone (kanojo wa, kuruma o, ni, de, to, ga); a verb keeps its endings and
  auxiliaries joined (yomitakunai, wakatteru, shitenai, ikitai). Contracted -te iru is written JOINED
  (tteru, shiteru); uncontracted 〜ている = -te iru. Kanji readings decided by the English and context
  (今日 kyoo, 明日 ashita, 一人 hitori, 何 nani / nan). SEEDS end with "." or "?"; LEGOs none; PHRASES none
  unless the target has ？ (→ "?"). 、 → ", ".

kor_for_eng (the course's PRONOUNCED, syllable-hyphenated Revised Romanization): lowercase; every
  Hangul syllable is one hyphen-joined unit inside its word, words separated by spaces, written AS
  PRONOUNCED: liaison (있어요 = i-sseo-yo, 생각이 = saeng-ga-gi, 않았어요 = a-na-sseo-yo), nasal and
  other assimilation (있는 = in-neun, 합니다 = ham-ni-da), aspiration (축하 = chu-ka), tensing as the
  examples do (안타깝게도 = an-ta-kkap-ge-do). One unit per Hangul syllable: the validator counts.
  Punctuation: copy a target "?" only; nothing else.

ara_for_eng (MSA): follow examples.tsv. Plain ASCII-ish chat transliteration: long vowels doubled
  (aa, ii/ee, uu/oo as the examples do), ' for ع and for hamza where the examples write it, th/dh/kh/gh/sh,
  the article written al- (with the sun-letter assimilation only where the examples do).
  Follow the vowelling of the target: if the target carries diacritics, romanise what they say;
  if it has none, romanise the natural MSA pausal reading (no case endings). ؟ → "?"; no other punctuation.
ara_eg_for_eng (Egyptian) / ara_lb_for_eng (Lebanese): follow examples.tsv exactly. Romanise the
  DIALECT as pronounced (Egyptian ج = g, ق = ' (glottal); Levantine ق = ', ة = -e/-a as the examples do).
  Copy the target's final "." and "?" (؟ → ?). One spelling per word across the chunk.

Other courses (hye, mkd, fas, heb, hin, rus, ukr, bul, ell, tha): follow examples.tsv exactly. The scheme
  is whatever those rows use (e.g. rus: kh/zh/ya/yu/y, soft sign dropped; mkd: č š ž ḱ ǵ). Copy punctuation as the examples do.
