## 2. P2 — Default controlled-queue reads erase recorded counts

**Location:** `services/voice-engine/booth-actor-queue.cjs:60`–`:68`; `services/voice-engine/recordist-router.cjs:669` passes the request's `includeRecorded` into the builder.

**Failing input:** a controlled queue contains two assigned lines, one recorded and one outstanding. Request `GET /api/recording/voice/human_ana_spa` without `includeRecorded` and without a narrower scope.

**Actual:** `{ total: 1, recorded: 0, remaining: 1 }`, rather than `{ total: 2, recorded: 1, remaining: 1 }`. `finishQueue` deliberately omits recorded lines from `lines` by default while returning whole-queue counts. The new filter recalculates all counts from that incomplete list. With every line recorded it reports total zero. The existing contract keeps recorded lines off the wire without erasing their tally.
