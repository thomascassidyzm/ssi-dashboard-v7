In the first case `plan()` omits the expansion altogether; in the second it produces a partly masculine female reading. Its expansion-writing/rendering callers therefore miss or retain the wrong speaker form.

That still separates the existing regression case `मैं थका था और वह सो रहा था।` before वह, while retaining मैं for modifiers and shared-subject verb groups.
