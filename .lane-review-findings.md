## 1. P2 — Failed checks cannot bring standalone clip re-records back

**Location:** `services/voice-engine/booth-rerecord.cjs:270–271`; newly connected caller: `services/voice-engine/recordist-router.cjs:851–855`.

**Failing input:** an authorised reader re-records a standalone `course_audio` narration/instruction clip with `rerecord_wanted` set, whose words do not also belong to a pod/seed line. The upload succeeds, then its deferred check returns `{checked:true, pass:false}`.

`recordRerecordClip` now queues that check against the clip UUID, but still clears `rerecord_wanted` immediately at recordist-router.cjs:879 and invalidates the language queue. The standalone line exists only through `fetchRerecordWanted` (recordist-queue.cjs:1939, 2000–2014, 2730–2737). Once the queue rebuilds, the clip UUID is absent from the pool, and the new `withTextCheckFailures` silently drops its failure here. It never returns on the Re-record card. **Smallest fix:** include still-authorised standalone clips named by live failed checks in the queue/pool, and permit their re-record through the same ownership checks when a live failure supplies the reason. Merely retaining the old cached line or displaying the poll result does not fix the 409.

## 2. P2 — Pod revoice flush exceeds the receiving route's link limit

**Location:** `tools/audio/revoice-pod-to-cast.cjs:99–102`.

**Failing input:** a pod with 251 lines sharing the same off-cast TTS greeting clip in both `target_audio_id` and `sentence_audio_ids`, with the same speaker gender. `podAsks` groups this into one render ask holding 502 slots. A successful render/proxy check appends all 502 links; the final flush sends them in one request.

`linkClips` rejects any request over 500 links before processing anything (`services/shared/link-clip.cjs:149–150`, code `TOO_MANY`). Counting 25 render asks between flushes does not bound their slot count. The driver already removed every link with `pending.splice(0)`, logs the rejection and finishes without attaching any of those clips. This can also occur across several smaller asks in one flush. The seed driver correctly chunks its links to 500; the pod driver does not.

**Smallest fix:** split `links` into requests of at most 500 in this flush, as `revoice-seeds-to-cast.cjs` does, and count/report each batch's result.
