# Translate encouragement messages to Malay

## Context

These are spoken encouragement messages for a language learning app (SaySomethingin / SSi). A friendly coach (Aran Jones) speaks directly to the learner during breaks in their practice session. The tone is warm, conversational, supportive — like a pep talk from a friend.

**These will be read aloud by a TTS voice**, so they must sound natural when spoken.

## Rules

1. **Use informal/familiar "you"** throughout (tu/tú/du/ty etc., NEVER formal vous/usted/Sie)
2. **"your new language"** — translate naturally as the equivalent phrase in Malay (e.g., "tu nueva lengua" in Spanish). Do NOT use the word "Malay" or "English".
3. **Keep the warm, conversational tone** — contractions, dashes, casual phrasing
4. **"The Zone"** — translate the concept naturally (e.g., "la Zone" in French). It's the sports concept of peak focus.
5. **"The Noticing Mind"** / **"noticing mind"** — translate this coined term naturally. It means the part of your mind that observes without reacting. E.g., in French you might say "l'esprit observateur" or "l'esprit qui remarque".
6. **"press-ups"** — use whatever term is natural in Malay (push-ups, etc.)
7. **Preserve the meaning and encouragement, not word-for-word** — natural Malay is more important than literal accuracy
