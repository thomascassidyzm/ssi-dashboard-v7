┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ACTION PRIORITIES                                                            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  PRIORITY 1: Fix "I want to" Repetition (ara_for_eng, eng_for_ara)
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Current: 11-12% of phrases
  Target:  <4% per opening
  Impact:  HIGH - learner boredom risk

  Actions:
  • Replace 60-70% with alternatives: "I'd like to", "I need to", "I'm planning to"
  • Convert to questions: "Do you want to...?", "Would you like to...?"
  • Add conditionals: "If I could...", "I wish I could..."


  PRIORITY 2: Reduce First-Person Dominance (All courses except Welsh)
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Current: 73-77% first person
  Target:  40-50% first person
  Impact:  MEDIUM - limits conversational fluency

  Actions:
  • Convert 200-300 phrases to second person ("You want...", "Can you...?")
  • Add 100-150 third-person phrases ("He/she wants...", "They think...")
  • Include observational phrases ("The teacher says...", "People often...")


  PRIORITY 3: Increase Second-Person Usage (All courses except Welsh)
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Current: 8-12% second person
  Target:  20-25% second person
  Impact:  MEDIUM - improves dialogue readiness

  Actions:
  • Add direct address: "You should try...", "Can you help...?"
  • Include advice: "You might want to...", "You'd better..."
  • Add questions: "What about you?", "Do you agree?"


  PRIORITY 4: Add Negation (cym_s_for_eng)
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Current: 0% negations detected
  Target:  10-15% negations
  Impact:  LOW - may be Welsh-specific

  Actions:
  • Review if negations exist but weren't detected
  • If missing, add: "I don't want...", "She can't...", "We won't..."
