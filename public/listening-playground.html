Spec (Aran 2026-04-29)

  • PS = pod sentence at normal speed (1×, 3 s). PS×2 = pod sentence at double speed (2×, 1.5 s). trans = English translation (2 s). Only two speeds — no 0.8×.
  • Layer 2 (pods) activates at main round 6 (start of Seed 2). Fires every round at round-end. One new sentence enters per round. Stages 1–6 each last 3 rounds; stage 7 is the eternal holding bay.
  • Layer 1 (graduated seeds) activates at main round 56 (offset+lastRound for first seed). Fires active-10 every 3 rounds + reserve every 13 rounds. Sliding window: when seed 11 graduates, oldest moves to reserve. 3 and 13 are coprime, so clashes only every 39 rounds.
  • Round: assumed 5 min (≈ 30 prompt-response cycles) for the "% of round" reading.
  • L1 simulation in this visualiser uses 1 graduation per round from R56, capped at 80 seeds — the real implementation will follow course-specific timing.

What you're looking at

  • The arc chart shows the combined L1 + L2 round-end lap time. Watch for the spikes at every-3-round (active-10) and every-13-round (reserve) intersections post-R56.
  • Each chip in the stage grid is one Pod 0 sentence at its current stage. Stages 1–6 show three iteration rows; stage 7 is the holding bay.
  • L1 status panel (right side of stats) shows how many seeds are in the active-10 sliding window vs the reserve, and which fires this round.
