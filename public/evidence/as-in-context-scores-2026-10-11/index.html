Presentation intros: how much does the "as in" context do?

Read-only scoring of every presentation intro in deu_for_eng and spa_for_eng, seeds 1-100. Text is read from the live course_audio row each intro points to (lego_introductions). Scale: 5 = the context is needed to disambiguate X (X alone has several senses, a homograph, or a word-choice the sentence settles); 1 = X is unambiguous on its own and the context is unnecessary. 2–4 between.

Coverage. 480 intros in scope. 337 carry an "as in" context and are scored. 143 have no context to score (Frame A, bare "The X for: …"): 68 German, 75 Spanish, counting three exceptions — German S0083L01 has a long preamble, and Spanish S0062L01 and S0098L02 have empty intro text in the audio row.

Judgement call to check. Where X is ambiguous only in register or gender (du/Sie, dein/Ihr, amigo/amiga), the context sentence cannot settle it, so those score 2, not 4–5. 31 of the 80 items at score 2 are of this kind. If register should count as "needed", those rows move up.
