Phone — both states in one screen

With “show the lines I've already recorded” turned on. Recorded rows are solid and filled. No tick, no badge, no red or green — the only colour on the page is the level meter while a line is being read.

Desktop — same list, wider gutters

1440 × 900. Nothing changes shape between the two.

What a tap does

  • Tap a line — the mic opens on that line and a level meter appears under it.
  • Tap it again — the take is filed through the existing upload seam, the row redraws solid, and the count at the top drops.
  • While one line is live every other line is inert. One degree of freedom.
  • Upload a file is the same seam for anyone who records elsewhere; Listen back plays the clip stored on the server, never a local blob.
