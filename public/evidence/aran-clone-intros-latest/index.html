Aran’s clone: course intros, latest

Six real intros from the Spanish course, English side only. Each plays three ways: the intro the course uses today, Aran’s clone reading the whole line, and Aran’s clone spliced the way intros will be made from now on.

Spliced means three short pieces in Aran’s clone, “The Spanish for”, “is” and “as in”, joined around his reading of the word or phrase, which is lifted slightly so it stands out. The last three carry the example sentence.

All of it is the clone Aran made on 6 October. Nothing here is in the course.
