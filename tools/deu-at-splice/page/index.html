Four phrases, spliced out of Sascha's own recordings

Sascha uses they/them. Nothing here is synthesised — every candidate is made of audio they already recorded, cut and glued. Tap the triangle to hear it. Tap PICK on anything you like — as many as you want. Picking is how you tell us what "good" sounds like. Your picks survive a reload; Send my picks at the bottom gets them to us.

Each candidate says what it was made from and how, so your picks can become a rule. Three things vary on purpose: which line the words were cut out of, how much silence is left around the cut (tight / medium / wide), and how long a gap sits between glued pieces (none, a crossfade, 50 / 110 / 190 ms).

The long phrase, in two halves. Glued whole it buries the join inside seven words, so it is split: pick one first half and one second half that say they pair with each other, and we glue your two choices. There are two ways to divide it because the recordings are lopsided — 16 lines begin “i wü iatz” but only 2 end “mit dir Deitsch reden”, and 17 end “Deitsch reden” but only 2 begin “i wü iatz mit dir”. Either pair completes the phrase.
