English narrator voice — ten voices, blind

What this is. The presentation slot — the voice that says “The German for: … is:” and reads the explanation lines. Nine Cartesia female voices plus the voice cast today, all reading the same three real lines from deu_for_eng. Letters are shuffled; the names are behind the reveal at the bottom.

How to use it. Section by section: tap A to J on one line, compare like with like, then mark each letter Yes / Maybe / No in the verdicts box at the bottom. Verdicts and notes save on this phone and survive a reload. Tap the same button again to stop.

Read before judging.

• All ten clips per line are loudness-matched to −18 LUFS (measured: every clip within 0.06 LU of target, widest spread within a line 0.08 LU, true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP; re-levelled 2026-09-21 after the first build was found to drift by up to 2 LU) so a louder voice does not sound “better”. Rendered on Cartesia sonic-3.6 at speed 1.0, 24 kHz mp3 — the same model, speed and format production uses. Nothing in the course was touched: these are standalone files.

• One of the ten is the voice cast today for German and French presentation (Tom's own clone). It is in the blind set on purpose.

• The known-side (English prompt) voice on both courses is also a Tom clone right now, so the whole English side is currently male; only the target voices are female.

• One take per clip. Cartesia has no seed, so a re-render of the same voice will differ a little in pace and stress. Judge the voice, not the take.

• Line 1 is a live template row; lines 2 and 3 are the seed 83 and seed 92 explanation rows, verbatim from course_audio.
