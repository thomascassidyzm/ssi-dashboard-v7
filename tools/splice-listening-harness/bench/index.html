The two ways of cutting, in one sentence each

Way A — cut the slow take at its pauses. You read the line slowly with a stop between each piece. The machine listens for the gaps and cuts there. The pieces are in your slow voice.

Way B — cut the clear take, guided by the slow one. You read the line twice. The slow read is only used to work out how long each piece takes. Those proportions are then stretched across the clear read and the cuts are made there. The pieces are in your normal voice.

Both are in the code today, in the same function. Way B is what runs whenever a clear take exists; Way A is what runs when there isn't one. Either way, the slow read is where the timings come from — so the stopping is not optional for Way B either. That is the thing worth checking with your own ears below.
