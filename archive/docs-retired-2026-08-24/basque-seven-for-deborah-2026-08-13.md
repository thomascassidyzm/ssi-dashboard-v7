# Seven Basque phrases that need your eye

Seven phrases in the Basque course have been set aside because we're not sure they're doing
what they're meant to do. Three of them need a real Basque judgement from you; the other four
are quick yes/no calls. It should take about twenty minutes.

You don't need to log into anything or change anything. Just reply to Tom with the numbers and
your answers — a word or a line each is plenty.

## 1. Needs your Basque ear

**Round 152, sentence 55**

> **esna egotea ez zait gustatzen**
> "I don't enjoy being awake"

This phrase is meant to be teaching **izatea** — "being", from *izan*.

But the phrase doesn't use *izan* at all. It uses **egotea**, from *egon*. Two different verbs
for "to be", and the phrase has picked the other one.

Your options:

- **egotea is right here** — and the phrase should be teaching *egotea*, not *izatea*.
- **izatea is right here** — and the Basque should be rewritten to use it.
- **Something else entirely** — tell us what it should say.

## 3. Needs your Basque ear

**Round 299, sentence 115**

> **euskaraz hitz egiteko prest sentitzen naiz**
> "I feel that I'm ready to speak Basque"

This phrase is meant to be teaching **nagoenik** — "that I am".

**nagoenik** isn't in the phrase anywhere. The phrase says **sentitzen naiz** — "I feel" —
instead.

Your options:

- **Rebuild the Basque** so it actually uses *nagoenik*, and tell us how it should read.
- **The Basque is fine** — it's the pairing that's wrong, and this phrase shouldn't be sitting
  under *nagoenik* at all.
- **Neither** — say what you'd do.
