## Overall: **DO-NOT-SHIP AS SCOPED (18 rows)** — but for a narrower reason than #870's

The mechanics that made #870 refuse the 5-seed version are now sound: the shared-clip collapse
(point 2) is real and correctly computed, the seed-230 numbers (point 3) hold at exact-token
granularity, the trigger risk (point 7) is real but manageable with correct sequencing, and — the
thing #870 actually blocked on — **the self-contradiction inside seeds 246/426/431/456/464 is
genuinely cured** by bundling the phrase edits (point 4).

But **point 1 is a hard blocker on its own terms**: this plan calls itself the fix for "seeds
before their only LEGO," and two more seeds carrying the identical, unambiguous defect — **165
and 178, live in the database right now** — are entirely absent from it. Shipping 18 rows while
knowingly leaving 165 and 178 uncorrected doesn't just under-deliver; it ships a *second* incomplete
pass on the same named defect class, the exact failure mode the first proposal was refuted for
(incompleteness), one level up.

Point 6 is a real, if pre-existing, correction: seed 426 will share one Sinhala clip between two
officially different English glosses ("would like to" vs "want to") — worth a decision (pick one
English, or accept the divergence explicitly) before render, not silence.

**Recommendation:** hold the 18-row plan; extend it to 165 and 178 (both are seed-only, no
phrase-level compounding, so cheaper than the five main pairs) before shipping anything under the
"orphaned හැබැයි seeds" banner. Separately, resolve the 426 English-divergence and note the s257
ලෙයිකයි co-located defect in the ship record even if left unfixed.
