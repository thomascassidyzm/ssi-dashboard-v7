## The question, in one line

In Syrian Arabic the Learner speaks with a **male** voice. Nine of the Learner's lines were
made masculine on 14 August to agree with it. Should the Learner be male?

## What is actually cast, right now

The Syrian Arabic pod casts the Learner as **Laith** — Azure's male Syrian voice. The pod's
own metadata also declares the Learner male, so voice and metadata agree. There is no
contradiction inside Syrian Arabic; the nine edits were correct against the cast in front of them.

The nine lines are all still masculine, and **none of them has any audio**. They are unrecorded
draft lines. Changing them back is a text edit and costs nothing.

## The fact that changes the decision

The Learner is female nearly everywhere else. Counted across all 66 courses that have a pod:

- **47** courses cast the Learner as an explicitly female voice
- **16** courses leave the gender blank, but every one of them is cast on a female voice
  (Eve, Ara or Aleksandra)
- **3** courses cast the Learner male: **Syrian Arabic (Laith)**, Catalan (Enric), Hebrew (Avri)

So Syrian Arabic is one of three exceptions out of sixty-six. Its own two Arabic siblings —
Arabic and Egyptian Arabic — both give the Learner a female voice. Syrian Arabic is the odd
one out inside its own family.

Worth knowing: on the English side the Learner is your own cloned voice in every course,
Syrian Arabic included. The estate norm is already your English against a female target voice.
Laith is the anomaly on the target side only.

## What it would cost to change

Nothing but the edit. Azure's Syrian Arabic has exactly two voices, and the female one —
**Amany** — is *already cast in this same pod*, on Anna, Barista, Sarah, the Receptionist and
two Customers. No new voice, no pool change, no audio to re-render, nothing to delete.
Syrian Arabic has no voice approval on record, so nothing can bulk-render there in any case
until you have listened and approved.

## My recommendation

**Recast female.** It matches 63 of 66 courses and both Arabic siblings; the female voice is
already in the pod so there is nothing to add; and it costs nothing because no line has been
recorded yet. Doing it now, before you approve a cast for Syrian Arabic, also avoids
invalidating that approval later.

This inverts what the card said before. The old recommendation ("male stands") was correct
about the cast and blind to the estate.

It is still your call — it is a persona choice, not a data fix. Say **female** and I recast
Laith to Amany and revert the nine lines; say **male** and I leave everything exactly as it is
and note Syrian Arabic as a deliberate exception.
