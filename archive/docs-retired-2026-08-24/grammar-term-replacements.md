# Grammar Term Replacements for TTS

## Guiding Principle
Replace jargon with **functional explanations** that tell learners what the word DOES, not what grammar category it belongs to.

## Summary: Replacement Rules

1. **Question particles (吗, 呢):** → `makes it a question`
2. **Completion/aspect (了):** → `indicates completion` or `shows change`
3. **Progressive (在, 正在):** → `shows ongoing action` or use English equivalent
4. **Experiential (过):** → `shows past experience`
5. **Possessive (的):** → `indicates possession`
6. **Relative clause (的):** → `who` or `that` or `links to what follows`
7. **Complement (得):** → `links to how well` or `shows result`
8. **Adverbial (地):** → `shows how it's done` or `-ly`
9. **Measure words:** → Usually REMOVE, the phrase demonstrates usage
10. **BA construction (把):** → `marks the object` or REMOVE
11. **Pronouns:** → Keep (plural), (formal), (polite)
12. **Action clarifications:** → Integrate: `park a car`, `move house`
