## Why the candidates click, and the live take does not

Full measurement and the bisect: **[A-131 end click — what it is and which step causes it](./a131-tail-click-diagnosis-2026-08-17.md)**.

Short version: the xAI provider returns a tail whose noise floor **stops dead** ~50 ms before EOF
with digital silence after it. Our `PRE_COMPRESS` stage lifts that floor by **12–15 dB**, which turns
an inaudible cut into an audible switch-off — literally a compressor's make-up gain being switched
off, which is what Tom's ear reported. The fix is subtraction: drop the compressor from the render
chain. Nothing is repaired, trimmed, padded or de-clicked.
