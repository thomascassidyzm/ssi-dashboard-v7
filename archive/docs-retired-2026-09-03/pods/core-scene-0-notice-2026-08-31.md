## The ruling this renders

The **notice** — "X isn't my first language" — is the primitive. It declares the asymmetry and nothing else: no request, no grant, and no branch. The **offer** is the notice plus a request for a grant, mintable only where a shared fallback exists. The survivability branch belongs to the request — which is exactly why the notice has no decline family: with no shared fallback there is nothing to decline *into*.

## The staging, and why it is this one

Pod-0 W13's world: a tourist stops a local in the street for directions. Cast inherited, nobody invented. This is the world where the offer is unmintable **as a fact of the scene, not an assertion**: the tourist did not choose their interlocutor, the local has one language and says so, and the directions have to come across anyway — the tourist is lost either way. No turn anywhere could offer, grant, decline, or retreat. The deixis error that sank v1 is structurally impossible here, and that impossibility is what the scene teaches.

The walk then makes the fused repair licence **earn its keep** in the same minute it was issued: one stumble corrected openly — where the corpus's own directions walk (g123) had to leave its correction silent — one continuer taken freely, and the whole route read back as the receipt.

## The dialogue

> **Tourist:** Excuse me — could you tell me the way to the railway station? Your language isn't my first, so go slowly with me — if I get a word wrong, just tell me, and I'll ask if I lose you.
>
> **Local:** Of course. Mine's the only one I've got, so between us we'll manage. Right — straight down this road, all the way to the lights.
>
> **Tourist:** Straight on, down to the… the lamps?
>
> **Local:** The lights — that's it. Straight to the lights, then left.
>
> **Tourist:** Left at the lights — and then?
>
> **Local:** Then you're nearly there — it's just across the square. You'll hear the trains before you see them.
>
> **Tourist:** Straight to the lights, left, and across the square. Thank you — I understood every word.
>
> **Local:** Every word right. You'll be fine.
