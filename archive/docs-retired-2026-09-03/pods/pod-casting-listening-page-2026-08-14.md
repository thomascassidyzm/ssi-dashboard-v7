# Pod casting: every language, one listen

*One sample line per candidate voice, across 41 languages. Nothing here is linked to any course — these are throwaway clips for your ear only. Tap to play.*

**How to use this.** For each language, pick one male and one female. Say the names in a voice note and I'll cast, render and report per language from there. Where a language shows both an **official pool** voice and an **in production** voice, those genuinely disagree: the pool is what the config says, production is what learners hear today. I have deliberately not chosen for you.

English is not on this page — Olivia and your clone are already approved and unaffected.

**One extra ask, in the Thai section only.** Thai has five speaker roles with nobody cast, 43 lines that were about to render on one male default voice — 18 of them written female. It is at the end of `tha`, it needs a word rather than an ear, and the Thai render is blocked in code until it gets one.
