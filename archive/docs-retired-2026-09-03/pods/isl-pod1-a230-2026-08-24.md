## A recommendation, not a change: the render gate and the pod verifier disagree

Not implemented, and deliberately so.

The STT-advisory rule landed on 2026-08-24 in `verify-pod-clips.cjs` — the
*post-hoc* verifier. Phase 8's *render-time* gate
(`services/audio-veracity.cjs`) is still hard STT: it throws and quarantines on
CER alone, with no voice or VAD escape. So a language whisper cannot referee
can pass the verifier and still be unable to get through the renderer. That is
exactly what happened here, and it is the same cause named in this morning's
report for `ara_sy` 4, `fas` 3, `ell` 2, `cat` 1.

`services/audio-veracity.cjs` already names Icelandic in its own header as a
language whose decoder *works* but whose comparison is mismatched — *"is —
genuinely sloppy but still separating at 0.37"* — and says such languages
"want a threshold or normalisation fix, not a capability guard." The evidence
here supports that: measured across 9 archived clips plus 3 fresh renders,
Icelandic sits at CER 0.30–0.53 on small and 0.08–0.91 on medium against a 0.30
threshold, while the same clips carry the right voice and real speech.

**Recommendation: give the render gate the same three-outcome rule the pod
verifier now has** — voice and VAD hard, STT advisory — rather than raising a
per-language CER number, which only moves the arbitrary line. That is a real
change to a shared gate across the whole estate and it is not this job's to
make unilaterally.

**Still explicitly out of scope and not done:** switching the global
`WHISPER_MODEL` to medium. It is one process-wide constant and would move every
decode across the estate. The evidence above strengthens the case (medium
clears 3 of 9 that small fails outright) but does not come close to justifying
an estate-wide swap on one course.
