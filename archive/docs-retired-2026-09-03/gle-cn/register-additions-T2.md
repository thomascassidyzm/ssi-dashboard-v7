## Objections filed, per §6 rule 3 — used anyway, recorded here

**1. `mar sin` now carries two English senses.** The register fixes #82 *so (= therefore)* → `mar sin`.
Seed 102 needs *"it's not like that"*, whose natural Irish is also `mar sin`. **ZUT is not broken** —
each English string still maps to exactly one Irish — but a learner meets one Irish shape for two
English jobs. The alternative, `amhlaidh`, is bookish. I used `mar sin` and am flagging it rather
than smoothing it.

**2. Seed 106 drops the English "just".** *"we just need to work hard"* — I wrote `is gá dúinn obair
go crua`, preserving ZUT with seed 104's *"we need to"*. The faithful alternative,
`níl le déanamh againn ach obair go crua`, renders *just* honestly but abandons the `is gá dúinn`
frame in the same sentence that has just used it for *"we don't need to"*. **I chose ZUT over the
discourse particle.** If the coordinator prefers the other reading, seed 106 is a one-line change.

**3. Seeds 122 and 132 use unrelated Irish for *excited* and *exciting*.** `ar bís` (122) and
`corraitheach` (132) are not the same root, so the learner gets no pattern. Both words are at or
near zero in the corpus. **If a speaker rejects `corraitheach`, the paired repair is to move both
onto `ar bís`** — seed 132 becomes `Ní chuireann sé sin ar bís mé chomh mór leis an rud a bhí sí a
rá`, which changes the English reading from *less exciting than* to *doesn't excite me as much as*.
That is a coordinator's call, not mine.
