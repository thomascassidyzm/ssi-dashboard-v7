## Show a Connemara speaker these first, in this order

1. **Seed 300** — *she doesn't want to seem unfriendly* → `Níl sí ag iarraidh a bheith cosúil le duine míchairdiúil`. The whole *cairdiúil* family is **0** in Ó Curnáin — `míchairdiúil` 0, `neamhchairdiúil` 0, `cairdiúil` 0. I am translating a word the corpus does not have. **Ask: how do you say someone is being unfriendly?**
2. **Seed 251** — *I don't want to find out until after we finish* → `Níl mé ag iarraidh fáil amach go dtí tar éis dúinn críochnú`. I stacked two register prepositions (`go dtí go` + `tar éis`) into a collocation I could not attest. It is heavy and I do not believe a speaker would say it. **Ask: would you just say `go dtí go gcríochnóidh muid`?** — which drops the English *after*.
3. **Seed 272** — *yes that sounds like a great idea* → `Is cosúil le smaoineamh iontach é sin`. *Sounds like* has no Irish equivalent that keeps the hearing metaphor; I used *is similar to*. The register already fixed the neighbouring *that's a good idea* → `Is smaoineamh maith é sin`, so a speaker may simply collapse the two.
4. **Seed 288** — *most people I know like watching television*. This is the register's own #148, which it labels **genuinely uncertain** and says it would delete if it could (`formhór` is 4 in 2,700 pages). **Ask: `formhór na ndaoine`, or `an chuid is mó de na daoine`?**
5. **Seed 284** — *do you know my sister's friend?* This is one of the two seeds the register flagged for its unsettled `deirfiúr` (#162). I made it worse-proof by writing `cara le mo dheirfiúr` instead of the genitive `cara mo dheirféar` — because **`deirféar` is 0** in the corpus — but the headword itself is still the register's open question. **Ask: brother and sister, how do you say the pair?**
6. **Seed 277** — *an important meeting early next week*. `cruinniú` is 27 in the corpus and every readable instance is a **crowd or a gathering**, not a diary appointment. **Ask: what do you call an appointment you have to attend next week?**
7. **Seed 255** — *when do you think you'll be ready to leave?* → `Cén uair a cheapann tú a bheas tú réidh le himeacht?` Uses the finite present of `ceap`, which nothing bans, but which the register's progressive `ag cheapadh` frame does not authorise.

## What surprised me

**1. A literal apostrophe in a corpus probe returns a false near-zero.** Probing `b'fhéidir` returns
**1**; probing `b.{0,2}fhéidir` returns **191**. The apostrophe in the extracted volumes is not
U+0027. I was one step from recording *b'fhéidir* as unattested and restructuring seed 261 around a
form the dialect uses constantly. This is the same class of trap as §D of the addendum and it is
**not** documented there — every apostrophe-bearing form probed tonight by any worker
(`b'éigean`, `níorbh`, `arbh`, `m'athair`) is suspect unless re-run with `.{0,2}`.

**2. `i gceann` is not the Connemara "in [a time span]" — `faoi cheann` is.** `i gceann` scores 13,
but Ó Curnáin glosses it *"in addition to"*, and `i gceann` + a time noun is **0**. `faoi cheann` is
14 and every instance is temporal: *"Beidh mé ann faoi cheann seachtaine"*, *"faoi cheann dhá mhí
eile"*, *"faoi cheann ceathrú nóiméide"*. Seeds 253 and 274 use `faoi cheann`.

**3. The register's own examples contradict its own rulings twice**, and both landed in my band:
§1 #3 writes `Ar b'éigean duit` (not a well-formed past-copula interrogative — I wrote
**`Arbh éigean duit`** at 278), and §14 #150 writes `Ní raibh orm ach …`, using the `tá orm` that §1
of the same document explicitly forbids for *have to* (I wrote **`Níorbh éigean dom ach …`** at 280).
Both are morphology/ruling fixes, not lexical divergences — the `éigean` ruling is untouched.

**4. `níos faide` is the standard, not Connemara.** `foide` 23, `fuide` 9, `faide` **3**. The
register's worked example for my seed 276 writes `níos faide`, but there is no ruling row for the
comparative of *fada*, and I scanned all 248 then-translated seeds, 368 phrases and 47 legos:
`faide` and `foide` are **both zero** in the live course. Nothing was fixed, so R1 governs. I wrote
**`níos foide`** at 275 and 276 and filed it as an explicit objection.
