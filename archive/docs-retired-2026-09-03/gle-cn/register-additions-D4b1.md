## Three things a Connemara speaker should be shown, in this order

> ### 1. `ar bís` for *excited* — Ó Curnáin calls it non-traditional
> Vol I discusses `bís, ar bís` under speakers **64Me** (labelled *innovative*) and **72A**
> (labelled *nontraditional*), and notes FFG now treats `bís` and `baois` as synonyms. Total across
> four volumes: **1**, and that one hit is the passage saying it is an innovation.
> This is the audited translation of seed 122 and I built it as it stands. But *excited* is a
> recurring English emotion word and the estate will need it again — **ask a speaker before it
> spreads past seed 122.**

> ### 2. `an uair dheireanach` — the lenition is the older-speaker variant
> Vol III, verbatim: *"29C, 43M `an uair deireanach` (common), some older speakers, such as 12S
> (Seán) and 16M (Máire) have frequent `an uair dheireanach`"*. So the **common** Carna form is
> **unlenited** `an uair deireanach`; the course now carries the older-speaker lenited form, because
> that is what the audited seed 117 says. One-line sweep if Kai wants the common form instead.

> ### 3. `úsáid` is a verb of "limited usage" in this dialect
> Ó Curnáin gives `úsáid` its own section (5.326) headed **"Verbs with limited usage"**, and records
> the everyday borrowing `use-áil` / `ghiúsáil` alongside it (vol IV indexes `ghiúsáid, see ÚSÁID2`).
> `do charr a úsáid` is FGB-correct and I built it as audited; a speaker should say whether Carna
> would actually say `do charr a ghiúsáil`.

## Two zeros I am explicitly NOT treating as evidence

- `ná mar a` **0** and `sa phub` **0**. Both are short function-word strings in a phonetically
  transcribed corpus, both are inside the noise floor the addendum names, and both are the audited
  seeds' own Irish. Recorded, not acted on.
- `faoin gcaoi a` **0**, against `an chaoi a` **92** and `chaoi a bhfuil` **20**. The noun +
  particle + finite verb frame is robust; only the preposition-fronted variant is unattested.
  This is **not** a new `cén chaoi` + verbal-noun instance and so is not caught by the B2 hold.
