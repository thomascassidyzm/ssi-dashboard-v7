# Seed 17
Known: Why are you learning her name?
Target: Cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag foghlaim a hainm?

## L1 [M] "why are you" → "cén fáth a bhfuil tú"
Components: why → cén fáth, are you → a bhfuil tú

BUILD:
- why are you talking now → cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag caint anois
- why are you talking → cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag caint
- why are you talking with everyone else → cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag caint le chuile dhuine eile

USE:
- why are you talking all day? → Cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag caint an lá ar fad? [9]
- why are you talking with me this evening? → Cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag caint liom tráthnóna inniu? [9]
- why are you talking quickly? → Cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag caint go tapa? [8]
- why are you talking with someone else at six o'clock? → Cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag caint le duine éigin eile ar a sé a chlog? [9]
- why are you talking and I'm not talking? → Cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag caint agus níl mé ag caint? [9]

## L2 [A] "learning" → "ag foghlaim"

BUILD:
- why are you learning → cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag foghlaim
- learning quickly → ag foghlaim go tapa
- learning all day → ag foghlaim an lá ar fad

USE:
- why are you learning Irish? → Cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag foghlaim Gaeilge? [8]
- why are you learning quickly this evening? → Cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag foghlaim go tapa tráthnóna inniu? [9]
- why are you learning Irish with everyone else? → Cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag foghlaim Gaeilge le chuile dhuine eile? [9]
- why are you learning all day and talking with me? → Cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag foghlaim an lá ar fad agus ag caint liom? [9]
- why are you learning and you want to stop talking? → Cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag foghlaim agus tá tú ag iarraidh stopadh ag caint? [9]

## L3 [A] "her name" → "a hainm"

BUILD:
- learning her name → ag foghlaim a hainm
- to learn her name → a hainm a fhoghlaim
- her name tomorrow → a hainm amárach

USE:
- why are you learning her name? → Cén fáth a bhfuil tú ag foghlaim a hainm? [9]
- I want to learn her name quickly → Tá mé ag iarraidh a hainm a fhoghlaim go tapa [9]
- he wants to learn her name this evening → Tá sé ag iarraidh a hainm a fhoghlaim tráthnóna inniu [9]
- I'm not sure if I can learn her name → Níl mé cinnte an féidir liom a hainm a fhoghlaim [9]
- we want to learn her name and come back later on → Tá muid ag iarraidh a hainm a fhoghlaim agus teacht ar ais ar ball [9]
