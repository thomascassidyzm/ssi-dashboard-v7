# Aran & Tom Talk Bollocks — transcript corpus

**PRIVATE.** These files live in the repo and go nowhere else: never published, never put on a Review shelf, never on any public surface.

## What these are

Three edited Descript transcripts of the *Aran & Tom Talk Bollocks* conversations:

- `part-1.md` — Part 1, with its own front matter, byline, date and audio URL as given
- `part-2.md` — Part 2, likewise
- `part-4.md` — Part 4 (of 4), no audio URL supplied

Tom pasted the edited transcripts into his Watson conversation on 2026-08-29; they were persisted here the same day.

They are **verbatim and unedited**. Speaker attributions are imperfect in places, formatting differs between parts (Parts 1 and 2 carry `- ` bullet prefixes, Part 4 does not), and there are stray artefacts — a lone `- T` line, inline section headings, bracketed interjections, double spaces, a non-breaking space in Part 2's `By Tom Cassidy` line. All of that is deliberate. The transcripts are good enough to work through with an intelligent reader; the reader does the working-through, the file does not do it for them. Do not tidy, normalise, prettify or lint these files.

The one and only edit made: a three-line front-matter block (`title: Aran & Tom Talk Bollocks Part 4 (of 4)`) was added at the top of `part-4.md`, matching the shape Parts 1 and 2 already had, because the pasted Part 4 arrived without one. The pasted text beneath it — including its own bare `Part 4` line — is unchanged. One sentence from Tom reverses this.

## Three recorded facts

**1. Part 3 is lost.** No transcript and no recording of Part 3 is known to exist. Evidence, as reported rather than exhaustively proven: the pasted corpus jumps straight from Part 2 to Part 4, and a text search of the source conversation for Talk Bollocks transcripts turned up nothing beyond what is here.

**2. `/home/tomcassidy/part5.mp3` is misnamed — it is the audio of PART 4.** Verified against the live files, recorded here so nobody redoes it:

- Its whisper log `/home/tomcassidy/part5.whisper.log` opens at `[00:00:00.000 --> 00:00:08.400]` with *"The broad picture, I think, is that it's part of the whole thing, you know, it's part of Plan B, really."* — Aran's first substantive line in **Part 4** of the edited text, there as *"The broad picture, I think is, that it's part of the whole thing, [Game B] it isn't something that happens in isolation."* (`part-4.md` line 21).
- The log's final lines, at 00:39:37–00:39:44, are the *"entertainment layer"* passage — *"natural layer there which becomes your entertainment layer"* — which occurs in the edited corpus only in **Part 4** (`part-4.md` line 377), roughly three-quarters of the way through it.
- The log stops at **00:39:44 of 53:33** and is now **superseded** by the full edited Part 4 text here. **No transcription run is needed and none is to be performed.** `part5.mp3` and its log have not been renamed, moved or deleted — recording the fact is the deliverable.

**3. Part 4 is titled "(of 4)", so there may be no Part 5 at all.** The pasted header reads `Aran & Tom Talk Bollocks Part 4 (of 4)`. Taken with Fact 2 — the only file anyone called "part 5" is in fact Part 4's audio — the working conclusion is a four-part series, of which three survive as text and one is lost. Honestly noted: Part 4 ends mid-thought — *"At this point we got interrupted by the dog. We're going to crack on with this after the next lockdown..."* — so a further session was intended, and whether one was ever recorded is unknown.

It is the estate's only attestation of the summit of conversational competence: joint construction, the reformulation move, anecdote-as-evidence (*"Iost my foot in that river please accept my anecdotal evidence!"*), flagged digression-and-return, callbacks, meta-comment, and the dog interruption that ends Part 4.
