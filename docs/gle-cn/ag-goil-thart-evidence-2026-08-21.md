# `ag dul thart` vs `ag goil thart` — the evidence, and what it costs to act on it

**Raised by worker #613 while decomposing seed 131, 2026-08-21. Probed and sized by the band
coordinator the same night. Needs one ruling from Kai; nothing has been changed.**

## The question

Ruling A1 settled *"I am going to"* as `ag goil a`, never `ag dul a`. That ruling was about the
**future frame**. Seed 131 uses `ag dul thart` for *going around* — literal motion, not the future
frame — so A1 does not reach it. #613 built it exactly as translated and flagged the open question
rather than deciding it, which was right.

The real question underneath: **is Connemara's preference for `goil` lexical, or only frame-specific?**

## The corpus answer

Ó Curnáin, four volumes. Probed with `tools/gle-cn/ocurnain-probe.py`; **controls passed** before
the counts were read (`Gaeilge` 121, `duine` 521, `bhí` 3133 — all at expected). No apostrophe
appears in any probe, so the D2 apostrophe trap does not apply here.

| form | vol I | vol II | vol III | vol IV | **total** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ag goil thart` | 0 | 1 | 4 | 2 | **7** |
| `ag dul thart` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | **0** |
| `ag goil` (all) | 25 | 57 | 132 | 131 | **345** |
| `ag dul` (all) | 5 | 3 | 9 | 1 | **18** |

Attested in running speech, vol IV: *"go mbeadh an fear **ag goil thart** a bhearrthadh dhi é"*.

**The preference is lexical, not frame-specific.** `goil` beats `dul` roughly 19:1 across the whole
corpus, and in the exact collocation the zero is on the `dul` side. This is not a bare zero being
over-read: it sits on top of a 345-hit positive for the form we would be moving *to*.

## What the live course already does

The estate has converged on `goil` by itself. Of the 300 translated seeds, **ten** carry `ag goil`
(82, 179, 201, 223, 227, 236, 243, 270, 289, 293) and only **two** carry `ag dul`:

- **seed 122** — *"…faoin gcaoi a bhfuil sé `ag dul`"* (how it's going). **Not yet decomposed.**
- **seed 131** — *"Tá an iomarca smaointe `ag dul thart` i mo cheann"*. **Decomposed and live.**

So `ag dul` is not a rival convention. It is two stragglers against ten.

## What it would cost

Small, and cheapest if decided before seed 122 is built:

- **seed 122** — translation only, one word. Nothing downstream exists yet.
- **seed 131** — the seed translation, **one teaching unit** (`131.2 "going around" → "ag dul thart"`),
  and **13 practice phrases** that carry the unit.

**Zero audio rows exist for this course**, so nothing has to be re-rendered and no clip is orphaned
by the change. That is the whole blast radius: 2 seed sentences, 1 unit, 13 phrases.

## The recommendation

**Change both to `goil`.** It is what ruling A1 already chose in the frame case, what the corpus
says by 345 to 18, what the collocation says by 7 to 0, and what the course itself has already
converged on 10 to 2. Leaving it is the option that produces an inconsistent course.

**Not done, awaiting Kai.** Seed translations were audited before this band began and are outside a
decomposition worker's remit to rewrite.
