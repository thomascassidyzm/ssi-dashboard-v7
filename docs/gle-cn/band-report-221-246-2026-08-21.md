## 2. Two things that surprised me, both worth a decision

### 2a. Seed 245 was a real ZUT break, and I repaired it

*"in a short time"* is banked as **`i mbeagán ama`** at seed 89. Seed 245's translation said
**`in am gearr`** — the only other instance in the course. Same known prompt, two target forms. I
brought the outlier into line with the earlier banked form and decomposed against that. This is the
one translation edit I made; everything else in the band was decomposed exactly as translated.

### 2b. The word for "help" is split across the course, and it is not a small split

The register says *to help* is `cúnamh do`, **not** `cabhrú le`. That ruling has already lost on the
ground. `cúnamh dhom` holds seeds 25, 74 and 161. `cabhrú`/`cabhródh` holds seeds 62, 63, 142, 168,
171, 176, 203, 204, 226, 246, 249 — and **two of those are already banked as legos** (168 *"to come
and help"* → `teacht agus cabhrú`, 171 *"me to help you"* → `go gcabhróinn leat`), so this is not a
translation-only drift.

My band inherits three `cabhrú` seeds (226, 229, 246). I decomposed them as written, mirroring each
seed's own sentence, and I deliberately avoided minting a lego whose known text is *"to help me"* —
that string is already owned by `cúnamh dhom` and a second one would have been a straight ZUT break.
So seed 226 teaches **"helping me" → `cabhrú liom`** instead.

**That is a workaround, not a fix.** The learner now meets two words for *help*. Someone has to
decide which one the course keeps, and the sweep is bigger than any one band. **This is the first
thing a Connemara speaker should be shown.**

## 3. `ag goil a` and the inverted object — as ruled, annotated

Seeds 223, 227, 236 and 243 all use `ag goil a`, and the object-after-verb order that the ruling
warns about is real in every one of them. I used the attested order and did not front the object
anywhere inside that frame. Seed 243's *"Tá mé ag goil a iarraidh an rud céanna le n-ithe"* is the
clearest case: everywhere else the course fronts (`rud eicínt a rá`), and here it cannot.
