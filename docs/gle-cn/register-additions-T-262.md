## Recurring English this sub-band fixes, for later bands to inherit

- **"a friend **of** X"** takes **`le`**, not `de` and not a genitive: `ba sheanchara le m'athair é`.
  Any later seed with *a friend of …* should use `le`. **confident.**
- **"who was …?"** identifying a person takes the **copula** `cé hé`, matching the C2 ruling that
  identifying *what is …?* takes `Céard é`, not `atá`. **confident.**
- **"who you mean" / "what I mean"** — the whole clause is `cé/céard atá i gceist ag-`. The
  preposition stays inside the unit; there is no tile meaning *mean*. **confident.**

## Defect found, annotated, NOT repaired here

**Seed 231 banked the lego "an old man" → `sheanfhear`** — the *lenited* form as it stands after
`ar` inside `tá aithne agam ar sheanfhear`. The band ZUT sheet fixes seed 264 at the bare
`seanfhear`, and the seed's own audited target text is `seanfhear`, so posting seed 264 with the
gloss *"an old man"* was rejected as a hard LEGO-ZUT violation.

Seed 264's lego is therefore glossed **"old man"** (which is in fact the more literal reading —
Irish has no indefinite article, so the *an* was never part of `seanfhear`). This is annotated in
the seed markdown.

**A later sweep should unlenite the seed 231 lego target to `seanfhear`** and re-merge the two
glosses. The same shape almost certainly exists at seed 232, whose lego is
*"an old woman"* → `sheanbhean`, and at seed 230's *"a young man"* → `fhear óg`. Nothing was
edited outside seeds 262–267.
