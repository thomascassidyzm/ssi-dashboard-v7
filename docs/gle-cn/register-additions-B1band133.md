## One gap this band could not close

Seed 133's known side is *"you get to know someone **very well** when you work together"*. That
*very well* is the only word in the band not carried by a teaching unit:

- the whole tile `cuireann tú aithne an-mhaith ar dhuine` scores **11** syllables against a cap of 8;
- and *very well* is already locked to `go han-mhaith` (S72), so `an-mhaith` could not be glossed
  *very well* without breaking ZUT.

So the seed teaches `cuireann tú aithne ar dhuine` and leaves `an-mhaith` to be recognised from
S125 (*very good*). If a later pass wants the full form taught, it needs either a syllable-cap
exception or a re-cut of S72/S125.
