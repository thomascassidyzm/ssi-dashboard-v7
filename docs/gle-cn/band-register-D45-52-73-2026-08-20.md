## Conflict 3 — seed 47's `I think` tile is structurally unbuildable (CONFIRMED, needs Kai)

The 50-seed checkpoint flags **8 phrases at seed 47** that break R5: they teach
`tá mé ag cheapadh go labhrófá Gaeilge liom` under the English *"I think **you'd** speak Irish with
me"*. `go labhrófá` was introduced at S15 with the known gloss *"you to speak"*. **"you'd" has never
been taught**, so a learner at seed 47 provably cannot produce these from the English prompt — A4,
the governing test, fails on all eight.

This band tried three repairs and **all three are illegal**, which is why it is being reported
rather than patched:

1. **Merge L1+L2** into `"I think that it's a good thing"` → `tá mé ag cheapadh go bhfuil sé go maith`.
   Rejected by the endpoint: **11 syllables against an 8-syllable lego cap**. (Note: neither local
   pre-checker catches the syllable cap — only the live endpoint does. Worth adding.)
2. **Keep three tiles and let L1's phrases use L2's chunk.** Rejected: a lego may only use chunks
   from *earlier* legos of its own seed, never later ones. All eight phrases came back untileable.
3. **Give L1 a different complement.** There is no third option: scanning every chunk taught before
   seed 47, the only things that can grammatically follow `ag cheapadh` are `go labhrófá` (S15) and
   `go bhfuil sé go maith` (S47's own L2). The tile has no legal partner.

So the dead worker's `"you'd"` was not carelessness — it was the only escape from a genuine dead end.

**Seed 47 was unbanked (with a snapshot into `seed_redo_snapshots`) during this investigation and
then RESTORED byte-for-byte to its original 3 legos / 24 phrases.** It is exactly as the fleet left
it. Nothing was lost.

**The repair is a curriculum decision, not a decomposition fix.** Two candidates, both Kai's call:
- teach *"you'd speak"* as a second known gloss for `go labhrófá` at or before S15, or
- relax the 8-syllable lego cap for this one tile so L1+L2 can merge.

Confidence: **confident** that the defect is real and that all three in-band repairs are blocked.
