## Two notes for whoever sweeps this course

**`a bheith agam` is person-locked.** It is the 1sg of the possession idiom, so it cannot appear
under a `muid` or `tú` subject without becoming `againn` / `agat`. One phrase written in this band
paired it with `caithfidh muid` and was corrected before the band closed. Anyone reusing the
`comhrá a bheith agam` tile in a later seed must change the pronoun with the subject.

**`ag cheapadh`, not `ag ceapadh`.** The live course lenites here (S47, S163) and seeds 123, 124 and
135 carry the same spelling in their already-approved translations. Match the course; do not
"correct" it toward the standard.
