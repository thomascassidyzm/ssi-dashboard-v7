BUILD:
- but I speak Irish → ach labhraím Gaeilge
- but I'd like to come back → ach ba mhaith liom teacht ar ais
- but he wants to finish → ach tá sé ag iarraidh críochnú

USE:
- I speak a little Irish but I'd like to speak very well → Labhraím beagán Gaeilge ach ba mhaith liom labhairt go han-mhaith [9]
- she wants to meet but I'm not sure if I can come back → Tá sí ag iarraidh bualadh le chéile ach níl mé cinnte an féidir liom teacht ar ais [9]
- we want to speak Irish but he wants to find out what the answer is → Tá muid ag iarraidh Gaeilge a labhairt ach tá sé ag iarraidh fáil amach céard é an freagra [9]

- you speak Irish very well but I speak a little Irish → Labhraíonn tú Gaeilge go han-mhaith ach labhraím beagán Gaeilge [9]
