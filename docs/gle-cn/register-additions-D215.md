## Caveats a Connemara speaker should be shown

1. **`Dé Sathairn` vs `De Satharainn`.** Ó Curnáin's own vol IV headword lists the local form as
   *De Satharainn*, alongside `Dé Domhnaigh`, `Dé Luain`. `Dé Sathairn` is attested 6 times, so it
   is not wrong, but if the estate wants the Carna spelling this is the one row to change, and it is
   currently in exactly one lego (`S0215L02`) and its phrases.
2. **`gloine nó dhó uisce`** — 0 corpus hits for `gloine uisce` (the corpus is spoken narrative, so
   a zero here is a noise-floor zero, not evidence against). The word order `gloine nó dhó uisce`
   ("a glass or two of water") is the translator's, not mine; a speaker may prefer
   `gloine nó dhó de uisce` or `gloine uisce nó dhó`. Flagging, not changing — the translation is
   audited and out of my scope.
