## Notes for the coordinator

- **`bhíodh`** is the first habitual past in the course as far as I can see. If a later band needs
  *"I used to …"* / *"they used to …"*, the forms are `bhínn` / `bhídís`, and `bhíodh` + noun subject
  as here. Registering it so the next band does not reach for `ba ghnách le`.
- **`a bheith cinnte`** deliberately does **not** reuse the S10 gloss *"sure"* → `cinnte` as a split;
  it is taught whole so no bare adjective+copula frame is invented.
- **`freagra ar an gceist`** is glossed *"an answer to the question"*, not *"the question"*. The `ar`
  is the noun's demanded preposition and stays inside the tile (R7).
