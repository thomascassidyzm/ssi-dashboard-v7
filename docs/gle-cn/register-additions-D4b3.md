## One thing a later sweep may want

`ag dul thart` (S131) is **literal motion**, not the `ag goil a` future frame that ADDENDUM A1
rules on, so A1 does not bite here. But the estate's own evidence for A1 was that Connemara says
`goil` where the standard says `dul`, and if that preference is lexical rather than frame-specific
then `ag goil thart` would be the dialect form. **I did not change it** — the seed's Irish is
already translated and audited and retranslating is out of my scope. Flagging it so a sweep can
decide once, course-wide.
