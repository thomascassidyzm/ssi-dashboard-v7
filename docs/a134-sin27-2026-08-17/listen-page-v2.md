## What changed since the last page you saw

The last page told you 27 Sinhala example sentences needed writing by a speaker. **That was wrong, and in your favour.**

The example sentence in these clips was never authored by anyone. The course *builds* it — it picks a sentence you already approved, from that seed's own practice phrases, and drops it in. The 27 broke because that pick was made against stale data years ago and never redone.

So the fix is not to write Sinhala. It is to **let the course pick again**. Running its own generator against today's content:

- **12 clips get a real example sentence back** — sentences already in your course, with your own English already attached to them
- **15 clips correctly stay short**, with just the chunk. The generator itself suppresses the example on those, because the example barely says more than the chunk does. That is the form **665 of this course's 2,237** presentation clips already ship in.

**No new Sinhala was invented anywhere in this repair.** Every Sinhala word below already existed in your course.

Voice read from the course config, not chosen: **azure si-LK-SameeraNeural**, speed 1. Compressor-free chain. All 12 new clips passed seven checks on the first take, including a new one — *is the example sentence actually spoken* — checked against the voice provider's own word-by-word log rather than against duration, which is blind to short words.
