# eng_for_sin — the 420/432 extension (involuntary-form class)

*2026-08-17. Authored on Tom's ruling to extend the plan. **NOTHING IS APPLIED.** Apply is gated on
#932 reporting the seed triggers live.*

These two cards are their own class within the plan, as ruled. They are not a marking problem: the
stem `ඇහෙන්-` is the **involuntary** form of `අහනවා` — 'be heard / sound' — and it **cannot bear the
ASK meaning at all**. No clitic fixes that. The verb itself is wrong.

But the fix is **smaller than "re-authoring"**, and the reason is worth stating because it vindicates
the canonicality argument from C3.

## Blast radius — measured, with the baseline subtracted

Raw `COURSE-WIDE BREAKAGE` numbers are meaningless without a control, which is the mistake I made
earlier in this plate. Controls run at the **same scope** as each edit:

| edit | reported broken | control (unrelated lego, same scope) | **edit-caused** |
|---|---|---|---|
| `420:3` → "they don't need to ask" | 117 of 3326 | **124** — `420:1` "how long" → "how long then" | **0** |
| `432:1` → "they want you to ask" | 115 of 3114 | **115** — `432:2` "they pretend" → "they pretend so" | **0** |

For 432 the control is **identical** to the edit; for 420 the control is **higher**. So the reported
breakage is entirely the pre-existing pool of phrases that already fail tiling in this course, and
**collateral damage outside each card's own phrases is zero** — matching what #913 found for
381/382. The `CONTAINMENT BROKEN: 8` on each is the card's own phrases, which this plan rewrites.

Note the baseline is **not a constant** (~115–124 here, ~135 at the 380 scope): the tool re-tiles a
scope that varies with the edit, so a control must be run per scope rather than reused.

### Smaller residue this exposes

The Sinhala `ඔහු කොච්චර කාලද` is **elliptical** — literally "he how-much-time", with no verb of
staying. So the English "how long he **stayed**" supplies a verb that the Sinhala does not contain.
The *referent* (duration) is settled and canonical; the specific verb "stayed" is an English-side
choice with no Sinhala attestation behind it. That does not reopen the referent question, but it goes
on the speaker list next to the dative-vs-ablative item.
