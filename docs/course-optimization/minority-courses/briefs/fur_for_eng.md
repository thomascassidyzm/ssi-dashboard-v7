## Orthography

**Standard: grafie uficiâl** (official orthography of the Osservatori Regjonâl de Lenghe e de Culture Furlanis / ARLeF). Reference spellings against **fur.wikipedia.org** and the **DLF / Grant Dizionari Bilengâl Talian-Furlan (GDBtf)**. When two spellings circulate, the GDBtf headword wins.

Hard rules:
- **Circumflex marks long vowels**: â ê î ô û. Never drop it: `fevelâ`, `cîr`, `pôc`, `vuê`, `sigûr`, `cemût`, `dî`, `visâ`. The circumflex is **retained under enclisis**: `visâmi`, `impegnâmi` (not *visami).
- Grafie-uficiâl lexical forms chosen for this course: `cumò` (now), `vuê` (today), `dispès` (often), `plui` (more/most), `pussibil` (possible), `peraule` (word), `cualchidun` (someone).
- `cj` and `gj` are real graphemes (`cjatâ`, `gjat`) — do not "correct" them to Italian `chi/ghi`.
- **REJECT**: Faggin/Pirona-style spellings (`çh`, grave-accent long vowels like `fevelà`), Italianized spellings (`più`, `possibile`, `parola`), and dialect spellings from non-central varieties. If a web attestation uses old orthography, the *construction* may still count as evidence, but normalize the spelling to grafie uficiâl.

## Core grammar the builder needs

**1. Obligatory subject clitics — THE trap of this language.** Every finite verb carries an atonic subject clitic, *even with a full subject present*. Bare `feveli` is WRONG; it is `o feveli`.

| person | clitic | example (fevelâ, pres.) |
|---|---|---|
| 1sg | **o** | o feveli |
| 2sg | **tu** | tu fevelis |
| 3sg m | **al** | al fevele |
| 3sg f | **e** | e fevele |
| 1pl | **o** | o fevelìn |
| 2pl | **o** | o fevelais |
| 3pl | **a** | a fevelin |

- **Negation:** `no` precedes and the **1sg clitic o drops after no**: `no soi sigûr` (NOT *no o soi*). Affirmative keeps it: `o soi`. Verified minimal pair: "No soi dome biel, o soi ancje furlan."
- The clitic is **retained inside embedded clauses**: `se o pues`, `ce che o vuei dî`, `il plui che o pues`.
- **Interrogatives use post-verbal (inverted) clitics** (`fevelistu?` type). None appear in seeds 1–10; when the first question seed arrives, verify each inverted form against GDBtf before use — do not derive by analogy.

**2. Verb forms already in play** (all attested unless noted):

| infinitive | meaning | forms used | model / note |
|---|---|---|---|
| fevelâ | speak | o feveli | 1st conj., clamâ model (1sg -i) |
| cirî | try; seek | o cîr (pres.), o cirarai (fut.) | **non-infix -î verb** (sintî model): o cîr, NOT *o cirìs. Proverb "Cui che nol cîr nol cjate" proves bare-stem 3sg |
| volê | want | o vuei | irregular |
| podê | can | o pues | irregular |
| fâ | do/make | o fasarai (fut.) | future attested in proverb "O fasarài al è fradi di no fâ mai" |
| dî | say | dî (inf. only so far) | short infinitive |
| jessi | be | soi | no soi / o soi (see negation) |
| visâsi (di) | remember | visâmi (inf.+encl.) | reflexive; di-complement (see gotchas) |
| impegnâsi | try hard / commit | impegnâmi | DLF-listed synonym of cirî sense 11 (sfuarçâsi) |
| imparâ | learn | imparâ | takes `a` + inf |
| spiegâ | explain | spiegâ | DLF headword |

**3. Tense/aspect mappings (course-wide):**
- English **progressive → Friulian simple present**: "I'm trying" = `o cîr`.
- English **"going to" → simple future**: `o fasarai`, `o cirarai`. Friulian has **no aller-future**; `lâ a` = physical motion only. (Native-check Q6 may soften this to present tense; until answered, future is the contract.)

**4. Word order & small syntax:**
- `dut` precedes the article: `dute la frase` (not *la dute frase).
- `il plui pussibil` **pre-modifies** in attested Friulian (`il plui pussibil furlan`) — order differs from Italian `il più X possibile`. Only attested before ADJECTIVES; before adverbs it is our unverified projection (seed 3).
- Stressed pronoun after preposition: `cun te` (with you sg).
- Stacked `di ... di` is normal Romance: `o cîr di visâmi di une peraule`.
- Enclitic reflexive attaches to the infinitive: `visâmi`, `impegnâmi`.

## Gotchas (plausible-but-wrong traps, with corrections)

1. **Bare verb without clitic** — *feveli*, *vuei*, *cîr* alone are ungrammatical → always `o feveli`, `o vuei`, `o cîr`. The clitic stays even with a full subject NP.
2. **Keeping the clitic after negation** — *no o soi* → **`no soi`**. But the clitic **returns in embedded clauses**: `se o pues`, `che o pues`.
3. **Italian order for "as X as possible"** — *il plui X pussibil* (Italian calque) → attested Friulian is **`il plui pussibil` + head** (`il plui pussibil furlan`). But only proven before adjectives — see Q1 before extending to adverbs.
4. **`provâ` for "try to"** — rejected; the corpus-native pattern is **`cirî di` + inf** (`o cirarai di vignî` etc.).
5. **`lâ a` as a future** — Friulian has no aller-future; `lâ a` = motion only. "Going to" → **simple future** (`o fasarai`, `o cirarai`).
6. **Dropping the circumflex under enclisis** — *visami* → **`visâmi`**; *impegnami* → **`impegnâmi`**.
7. **`par furlan` after fevelâ when the language is the object** — keeps ZUT: `fevelâ furlan` (speak Friulian) ≠ `dî alc par furlan` (say sth *in* Friulian). Attestation for the bare form: "FURLANS FEVELAIT FURLAN".
8. **cirî conjugated as an -ìs verb** — *o cirìs* → **`o cîr`** (sintî model; proverb "Cui che nol cîr nol cjate" proves bare stem).
9. **Masculine `sigûr` from a female voice** — must be **`sigure`**. Audit every predicate adjective against the recorded voice's gender before TTS.
10. **"more than I can" misreading** — the article in `il plui che o pues` is load-bearing; do not shorten to *plui che o pues*.
11. **Flinching at `di ... di`** — `o cîr di visâmi di...` is correct; do not "fix" the stack.
12. **Italianisms in spelling** — più/possibile/parola/adesso have zero place in output; nearest licensed forms are plui/pussibil/peraule/cumò.

## Instructions to Opus for continuing (seeds 11+)

1. **Reuse before you translate.** For every new canonical seed, first map each known chunk against the LOCKED DECISIONS checklist and seeds 1–10. If a chunk already has a target (`I'm trying to`, `remember`, `can`, `in Friulian`...), you MUST reuse it verbatim — inventing a synonym is a ZUT violation, not style.
2. **Clitic discipline is non-negotiable.** Every finite verb you emit gets its subject clitic; every negated 1sg drops `o`; every embedded clause restores it. Re-check this on every single seed — it is the error a Romance-trained model makes by default.
3. **Verify, don't conjugate by analogy.** Friulian conjugation classes are treacherous (-î verbs split into infix/non-infix; futures are irregular). For any NEW verb form: find it in the GDBtf/DLF or in running Friulian prose (La Patrie dal Friûl, fur.wikipedia, Bibie par furlan are the usable corpora). If you cannot attest the exact inflected form, use a form you *can* attest, or flag it.

Expect thin coverage on: verb + preposition selection (a/di/par), adverb placement, "as X as Y" comparatives, anything colloquial/idiomatic, and all interrogative clitic forms.
