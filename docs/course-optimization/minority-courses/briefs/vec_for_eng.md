## Orthography

**Pinned standard: modern vec.wikipedia house style (Grafia Veneta Unitaria-derived).** Sources of truth, in order: vec.wikipedia article text → vec.wiktionary → Boerio (1844, for lexical existence only, spellings often archaic) → en.wikipedia "Venetian language" (grammar tables only — its 1sg èser row is a trap, see Gotchas #1).

Hard rules (all deliberate; do not "fix"):

| Rule | Write | Never |
|---|---|---|
| **No geminates** (degeminated GVU) | speso, poso, calcosa, deso, tuta | spesso, posso, qualcosa, adesso*, tutta |
| **Evanescent intervocalic L = ⟨ł⟩** | paroła, queło, ła frase | parola, quello, la frase |
| **⟨n⟩ before p/b** | inparar, tenpo, senpre | imparar, tempo, sempre |
| **⟨x⟩ only for /z/** | xe (is, 3sg) | ze, se for /z/ |
| **1st-conj future keeps -ar-** | provarò, parlarò, farò pràtega | proverò, parlerò |
| **Accents** | only where attested/contrastive: vèneto, pràtega, ancò, pì, -ò futures; seeds write **drio** unaccented — keep it | drìo (don't reintroduce the accent mid-course) |

Two facts to hold simultaneously: (a) en.wikipedia correctly notes historical Venetian **never used ⟨ł⟩** — it is a modern vec.wikipedia convention; (b) we pinned it anyway, on purpose, per the grounding. Never mix ł and non-ł spellings ("na paròla" from the original grounding is superseded by **na paroła**).

**Unresolved edge:** 'vojo' vs 'voło' (both attested 1sg of vołer). Course currently uses **vojo** in seeds 1/7/8. Do not change it, but this is native-check Q10 — settle before seeds 11+ introduce more vołer forms.

*'adeso/adèso' exists but the course pins **deso** (seeds 1, 9).

## Core grammar the builder needs

Venetian is SVO, no cases, no metaphony. The three systems that will make or break new seeds:

**1. Subject clitics — obligatory in exactly 2sg / 3sg / 3pl, forbidden-by-default elsewhere:**

| Person | Clitic | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1sg | — (none) | **vojo**, **parlo**, **son** — bare, correct as-is |
| 2sg | **te** (obligatory) | **te parli** = you speak |
| 3sg | **el** (m) / **ła** (f) (obligatory) | **el vol** = he wants; **ła parla** = she speaks |
| 1pl | — | parlemo |
| 2pl | — | parlè |
| 3pl | **i** (m) / **łe** (f) (obligatory) | **i parla** = they speak |

Optional tonic pronouns can precede (mi parlo, ti te parli, mi son) — the course uses **bare forms without tonic pronoun** in seeds 1–10; keep that unless emphasis is in the English.

**2. Verb morphology used so far:**

| Form | Pattern | Attested in course |
|---|---|---|
| 1sg pres, -ar verbs | -o | parlo, poso (poder), vojo (voler) |
| 1sg pres, èser | **son** (pinned — see Gotchas #1) | son drio, no son sicuro |
| 1sg future, -ar verbs | **-arò** (stem keeps -ar-) | provarò, farò |
| Infinitive | -ar (final -e dropped: parlar, dir, inparar, spiegar) | throughout |
| Progressive | **èser drio a + INF** (a pinned; see Gotchas #2) | son drio a provar |
| English "going to" | **simple future** — Venetian has no andative future | provarò a spiegar |

3sg pres 'is' = **xe** (not yet in a seed; will appear early — spell with x).

**3. Interrogative enclitics (-tu, -lo etc.)** — the grounding flags them as a headline feature, but **no seed 1–10 uses one and the exact central forms are UNVERIFIED**. When a question seed arrives (e.g. "do you want…?"), do not invent the enclitic form: mark low-confidence, propose from vec.wiktionary conjugation tables only, and route to native check. This is the single thinnest area of the course.

**Other pins:** negation = plain **no** + verb (no emphatic miga/mìa); reflexive infinitive attaches the clitic (**ricordarme**, not climbed **me poso ricordar** — pending native Q9, keep infinitive-attached consistently, matching seeds 6/10); preposition + tonic pronoun (**co ti**, never co te); partitive **de** after quantities (**un fià de vèneto**); F agreement is live (**tuta ła frase**; sicuro/sicura per course-voice gender — open Q12).

## Gotchas — each with the ONLY correct form

1. **so/son:** 1sg èser = **son** (course pin). en.wikipedia's table says 'mi so' — real variant, but 'so' collides with 1sg saver ('I know'). Any builder/reviewer "correcting" son→so from that table is wrong by contract.
2. **drio vs drio a:** progressive = **drio a + INF** (central pin, per vec.wiktionary). Bare 'drio + INF' (en.wikipedia, Venice city) is a real variant, not an error — but not ours. This multiplies from seed 11; don't drift.
3. **Clitic insertion/omission:** never add a subject clitic to 1sg/1pl/2pl (**vojo**, not *mi te vojo*); never omit it in 2sg/3sg/3pl (**te parli**, **el vol** — bare *parli*, *vol* are wrong).
4. **Orthography reversion:** speso/poso/calcosa/deso (never geminates); paroła/queło/ła (never plain l); inparar/tenpo (never m); xe. ł is a modern convention deliberately pinned — don't "historicize" it away, don't half-apply it.
5. **vojo/voło:** currently **vojo**; frozen pending Q10 — no builder changes it unilaterally.
6. **Italian calques are the #1 failure mode:** provarò not proverò; poso/speso not posso/spesso; **un fià** not un poco; calcosa not qualcosa; and never invent Italian-shaped locutions ('far pràtega de' is exactly this risk — HELD).
7. **'today':** **ancò** (central pin). ancùo/incò/uncò/incoi are all real elsewhere; a Venice-city native will "fix" ancò→ancùo — that's a variety question (Q5), not an error report.
8. **No andative future:** "I'm going to X" = **-rò** future. *vado a + INF* is an Italianism.
9. **ZUT landmine (seed 5 ↔ seed 8):** if seed 5 ever adopts 'provarò a parlar', then 'provarò a' = both practise and try → ZUT violation vs seed 8. Resolve seed 5 (native Q4) before decomposition; fallback (1) 'me eserçitarò a parlar' preserves distinctness, fallback (2) is last resort requiring explicit ZUT resolution.
10. **Clitic climbing:** 'me poso ricordar' and 'poso ricordarme' are both grammatical; course pins **infinitive-attached** (ricordarme) pending Q9. One pattern course-wide or reflexive LEGOs fork.

## Instructions to Opus for continuing (seeds 11+)

**Process per new seed:**
1. **Reuse before you translate.** Check the LOCKED DECISIONS checklist first — if a known chunk already has a target, use it verbatim. New renderings of already-mapped English = ZUT violation.
2. **Attest before you write.** Every new content word/locution needs a hit on vec.wikipedia, vec.wiktionary, or Boerio (spelling then normalized to the pinned GVU rules). If you can only find the Italian cognate, you have NOT found the Venetian word — do not degeminate Italian and call it Venetian (that's how 'far pràtega de' happened).
3. **Confidence discipline:** attested word + attested construction = medium-high max (this is a low-resource language; nothing is "high" without a native). Attested words + *inferred* construction = low-medium and a `notes` flag. Unattested locution = HOLD, add to native-check list, do not record.
4. **Flag, don't guess**, in these thin zones: interrogative enclitics (nothing attested in-course), 1pl/2pl verb forms, past tenses (none pinned yet — when the first past seed arrives, establish the auxiliary + participle pattern as a new locked decision with citations), conditional, imperatives, any 'you'-plural, and any idiom.
5. **New pins go in writing.** When a seed forces a new choice (first 3sg clitic sentence, first question, first past tense), add it to the LOCKED DECISIONS list in this brief's file with the citation — the next builder inherits your pin, not your memory.
6. **Orthography sweep before submit:** grep your draft for geminates (ss, tt, ll, pp…), plain intervocalic 'l' where ł belongs, 'mp/mb', -erò futures, and Italian giveaways (qualcosa, adesso, un poco, vado a). Any hit = fix or justify.
7. **Do not touch seeds 1–10.** They are the locked reference set. Seed 5 stays HELD (not recorded, not decomposed) until native Q4; the sicuro/sicura slot in seed 10 stays open until Q12.
8. **Native check is the gate, not a formality:** nothing records and nothing scales past ~seed 30 without answers to Q1–Q5 at minimum, because son/drio-a/ancò/deso appear in a large fraction of all future seeds — a late flip means mass rework.
9. When Venetian genuinely lacks an English structure (andative future — Gotchas #8 — is the model case), render with the attested Venetian strategy and note the mapping; never import the English/Italian structure to be "faithful".
