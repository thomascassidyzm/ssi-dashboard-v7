## Orthography

**Standard: the Cademia Siciliana modern normalized orthography as reflected in prose on scn.wikipedia.** Copy scn.wikipedia usage; where scn.wiki itself mixes variants, the locks below decide. These are **course choices, not facts** — record them in the gotchas doc and get native ratification (Q2, Q3, Q6).

Hard rules (locked):

| Rule | Correct | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| Unstressed final vowels raise: **-u / -i**, never -o / -e | sicilianu, spissu, pussìbbili | siciliano, spesso, possibile |
| Glide spelled **⟨j⟩** | staju, aju | staiu, aiu |

| One koine, course-wide — **no regional variants** even when attested | oggi, chiḍḍu | oi/oji, picciriddru-type spellings |

## Core grammar the builder needs

**1sg presents are learned forms, not derivable stems.** Metaphony and irregularity mean the 1sg often shares little surface material with the infinitive. Present each as its own LEGO fact:

| Lemma (inf.) | 1sg present | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| vuliri | **vogghiu** | I want (full: vogghiu/voi/voli/vulemu/vuliti/vonnu) |
| putiri | **pozzu** | I can |
| èssiri | **sugnu** | I am |
| aviri | **aju** | I have |
| stari | **staju** | I am (locative/progressive aux) |
| parrari | **parru** | I speak |

**No synthetic future exists.** "Going to / will" = **aju a + infinitive** (futurate: `aju a fari`, `aju a circari di`) or plain present. Never let any builder conjugate a future tense — if one appears, it is an Italianism.

**Progressive** = stari + gerund: `staju circannu` (gerund in **-annu/-ennu**: parrannu, circannu).

**Clitics:** both orders are grammatical and both occur in the locked set —
- **Enclitic on infinitive:** keep the full infinitive **-ri** before the clitic, add the accent: `arricurdàrimi` (never Italian-style truncation *arricurdarmi).
- **Clitic climbing** over a modal: `mi pozzu arricurdari`.
Do not force one pattern onto the other; copy whichever the reference seed for that frame uses.

**Word order:** SVO, adverbs typically sentence-final (`ora`, `oggi` final in seeds 1/7/9). Negator **nun** directly before the verb/clitic cluster (`nun sugnu`).

**Superlative-adverb frame:** `lu cchiù + ADV + pussìbbili` = "as ADV as possible" (seed 3).

**Partitive after quantity words:** `tanticchia **di** sicilianu` (seed 9).

**Predicate adjectives agree with the speaker's gender** — see gender-pair contract below.

**"What / that which"** = `chiḍḍu ca` (seeds 7, 8). "Something" = `quarchi cosa`. "If/whether" (indirect question) = `si` (siddu is conditional-if; pending native tick, Q5).

## Gotchas

1. **Italian interference is THE failure mode.** Every form that looks Italian must be re-attested: `parrari` not parlari; `palora` not parola; `vogghiu` not voglio; endings -u/-i never -o/-e. Plausible ≠ correct.
2. **Orthography locks are course choices** (⟨j⟩ glide, h-less `aju`, `ḍḍ` underdots, `lu/la/li`, aphaeresis apostrophes) — scn.wiki itself mixes variants; apply the locks mechanically and note they await native ratification.
3. **No synthetic future.** `aju a` + inf or present. A conjugated future = builder error, reject on sight.
4. **`circari di` is "try to", everywhere.** `pruvari` is banned for this sense.
5. **`pratticari` is a false friend** (Traina: frequentation/dealings — 'pràttica ccu zoppi'). Never render "practise a skill" as pratticari + inf. `fari pràttica` (seed 5) is provisional and native-blocking.
6. **1sg presents are opaque learned forms** (vogghiu, pozzu, sugnu, aju, staju, parru) — present as LEGOs, never as stem+ending; metaphonic vowel alternation across paradigms is normal, don't "fix" it.
7. **Clitics:** enclitic keeps full infinitive `-ri` + written accent (`arricurdàrimi`); climbing (`mi pozzu arricurdari`) equally valid. Never Italian-style truncation.
8. **Proparoxytone accents are load-bearing** (pussìbbili, arricurdàrimi, pràttica, àutra, èssiri) — missing accent = orthography error + TTS/text mismatch fuel.
9. **Seed 7's known side is deliberately non-canonical** — do not revert without Kai's say-so.
10. **Gender-pair adjectives** (sicuru/sicura) keyed to voice gender; audit every new seed for speaker-referring adjectives/participles before TTS.
11. **One koine.** Reject western/eastern regionalisms (oi/oji, ddr-spellings) even when dictionary-attested.

## Instructions to Opus for continuing (seeds 11+)

1. **Reuse before you translate.** For every new canonical seed, first scan seeds 1–10 (and your own accepted seeds) for chunks that already carry the meaning — `staju circannu di`, `aju a`, `chiḍḍu ca`, `vogghiu`, `pozzu`, `nun sugnu` — and reuse them verbatim. A known-side string that already has a target mapping MUST get that same target (ZUT). Same known "now" → `ora`, final position; same "try to" → `circari di`; no exceptions, no synonyms for variety.
2. **Attest, don't derive.** For any NEW content word: check scn.wiktionary → scn.wikipedia prose → Traina, in that order. If you can only find the Italian-shaped form, that is a red flag, not an answer — search for a distinctly Sicilian variant (the parrari/palora pattern: Sicilian often has -rr-, metathesis, or a different lemma entirely). If attestation is thin, ship the best candidate at **confidence: low** with the evidence in notes and add a native-check question. **Never invent by de-Italianizing** (swapping -o→-u on an Italian word is not attestation).
3. **Apply the orthography locks mechanically** (section 2). Normalize whatever spelling the source uses into the locked convention, and note when you did so.
4. **New 1sg forms:** look up the full conjugation each time (metaphony makes guessing unsafe). Record the 1sg as a learned form in the LEGO, per gotcha 6.
5. **Grammar red lines:** no future tense ever; negation with `nun`; gerunds -annu/-ennu; enclitic infinitives keep -ri + accent; check every seed for speaker-gender adjectives and register the gender pair before the TTS plan.
6. **Where Sicilian is thin — flag, don't guess.** Expect attestation gaps for: modern/abstract vocabulary (technology, scheduling, feelings-talk), verb+preposition government (which preposition a verb selects is rarely documented — attest the whole frame, not the verb), collocations of the `fari pràttica` type (light-verb constructions), and anything involving subjunctive/conditional sequences. For these, write your best candidate at low confidence, add it to the native-check list, and DO NOT generate audio for low-confidence seeds.
7. **Audio gate:** no TTS for any seed until (a) the native pass has cleared the BLOCKING items and orthography locks, (b) the gender-pair list is resolved, and (c) Kai has approved the plan. This is an approval gate, not a suggestion.
8. **Log every judgment call** (new lock, new ZUT mapping, canon edit) in the scn gotchas doc in `docs/course-optimization/minority-courses/` the moment you make it — the next agent must be able to reconstruct why every non-obvious form is the way it is.
