## Cross-cutting findings (decisions for you + Aran)
1. **Two actively-wrong glosses** to retire: 了 = "already" (→ 已经), 把 = "take" (it's a structural marker, not a verb). These mislead, not just under-serve.
2. **Modal-particle policy** (啊, and by extension 吗/呢/吧): *fuse into the host word, keep silent, never introduce as a concept.* 啊 is the clean test case — confirm this as a general rule.
3. **Multi-sense particles** (了, 都, 还): each debut is scoped to the seed's sense; the *other* senses (perfective 了, plural-subject 都, additive 还) get their own later in-context debuts where seeds actually use them. So a particle may be "introduced" more than once — **driven by usage coverage**, not a fixed count.
4. **Two cases reach beyond their seed** (把 needs 放进/包/里 staged earlier; 过 needs 见 introduced) — these are wider edits, not single-seed rebuilds.
5. **A bigger gap surfaced**: several seeds' *frame vocab* isn't cleanly taught either — standalone 你 (only in 和你), 吗 (question particle), 说完/完, 哪儿. This is a **separate, larger audit** (decomposition completeness beyond particles) worth scoping on its own.

## Recommended sequence
1. **还, 啊, 都** (🟢) — cheapest, prove the pattern + the modal-particle policy on a clean case.
2. **了, 也** (🟡) — single-seed rebuilds; retire the "already" mis-gloss.
3. **过** (🟡) — needs 见 added; slightly wider.
4. **把** (🔴) — needs disposal scaffolding staged first; tackle last / deliberately.
5. **Separately**: scope the frame-vocab completeness audit (#5 above).
