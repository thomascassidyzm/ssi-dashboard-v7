## How to read this

1. **Reordering alone wins the early game outright.** Through R200 — where 79% of real learner activity lives — ALIGNED (1.44–1.57×) beats not only production but every free-inventory designed build. For the typical learner, the seed-aligned reorder of the EXISTING course delivers the same lived advantage (1.34×) as the best from-scratch design (1.36×).
2. **The R548 endpoint (0.85×) is an accounting artifact, not a capability deficit.** ALIGNED and production contain the identical 548 LEGOs — terminal producibility is equal by construction. The metric books each LEGO's value at its arrival (top-10 against the inventory of that moment), which flatters late placement: production's deferred openers arrive into a mature inventory and book big. The same artifact partly explains v2's "narrowing" in the May synthesis. Arrival-value is the right metric for the learner's lived early experience; it is the wrong metric for terminal comparison of identical inventories.
3. **The residual v2 A advantage is mid-course (R300–400: 1.53–1.60× vs ALIGNED's 1.34–1.20×) and it is an INVENTORY effect, not an ordering effect.** v2 builds atomise what the canon decomposition never atomises. The scoring agents independently confirmed: no atomic 吗, 的, 有, 个, 别, 可以, 着 exists anywhere in the canon 548 — several top-anchor frames (你可以再说一遍吗, 真的吗, 别担心) are unproducible at ANY order. The remaining gap to v2 is a decomposition question, not a sequencing question.

**The differential, in one line: order buys the early game (≈ all of v2's learner-experienced advantage, seeds intact); atomisation buys the mid-game; neither requires abandoning the seeds.**

## Inventory defects surfaced by the scoring pass (order-independent, feed the quality workstream)

- Missing atoms cap anchor frames at any order: 吗, 的, 有, 个, 别, 可以, 着 (atomic forms absent across all 548).
- ~12–20% of rounds are inert under any order: literal re-introductions (担心, 问, 见面, 开始, 告诉, 试试, 准备好, 等, 一会儿, 弄清楚, 给, 离开, 让…) and M-chunks fully derivable from already-introduced atoms (不太, 没问题, 你觉得怎么样, full-sentence chunks).
- Politeness register hole: first thanking move only at R187 even under ALIGNED (inventory-limited).
- Kinship block register-dated (妇人/老妇人/老男人) — matches the earlier content-safety flag.
- Number×classifier paradigm completes only in R401–548; 扇/辆 arrive stranded without their nouns.
- Data note: build_ALIGNED.md row R107 has a corrupted Mandarin cell (rated as canon 安静).

## Caveats

- ALIGNED's order was optimized under the corpus signal, then scored by the harness — an ALIGNED build constructed *under* harness scoring would do no worse (conservative).
- Cross-run rater severity: these agents scored duplicates at 0 and derivable chunks ≈1 (slice 2 self-estimates ~30–40 pts conservative vs May conventions).
- Single rater per slice, as in May; anchors are the combinability device.
- zho-only mean-of-ratios (1.10×) reflects zho's unusually deep-positioned cohort tail plus the endpoint artifact; pooled weights are the better estimate of a typical course population.

## Implications for the MVP-300 fleet

Most courses have their first ~300 built on the canon order. This measurement says: a seed-aligned reorder of EXISTING inventory — no new content, no new seeds, coordinates intact — recovers essentially the whole early-game advantage that motivated from-scratch design. Migration cost is basket regeneration along the new availability order (texts largely reusable, audio largely relinkable). The richer prize (mid-course, v2 A territory) additionally needs selective atomisation of the missing function words — which is the same decomposition-quality work already underway (particles, ZUT, contracts).

Proposed sequence: 1) ratify the artifact-aware reading above; 2) pilot a reorder on one young MVP course end-to-end (reorder → basket regen → gates → verify); 3) fold the missing-atoms list into the decomposition workstream; 4) for genuinely new pairs, design under the harness with seed-milestone alignment from day one.
