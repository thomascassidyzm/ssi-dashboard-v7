# The little walks a learner can ask for

For whoever is editing. These are the six guided walks in the app: a learner taps a
question in the Library and the app walks them round their own screen, one step at a
time, pointing at the real thing as it talks. These are the words it says.

They are the other half of the How This Works copy — that document is the reading;
this is the pointing.

**Edit the words freely.** The `##`/`###` headings and the little `key` lines under
them are how we map your edits back into the app, so please leave those alone —
everything else is yours to change.

Each step is pinned to a real thing on the learner's screen, and the app draws a
spotlight round it while the step is showing. So a step's words should make sense
as a caption for the thing it is pointing at. If a step needs to point somewhere
else, say so in your edit and we will move it — that part is wiring, not words.

A few lines are governed by settled rulings and are fine to raise, but we will come
back to Tom on them rather than silently applying or reverting your edit:

- The names **Easy** and **Fast** for the two paces.
- The **no-streaks, no-points, no-leaderboard** framing — the decision, not just the words.
- The **honest thirty-hours arc**: that the first thirty hours are hard, and we say so.
- No learner-facing line says **lego** or **seed** — those are our words, not theirs.
- British English throughout.

### Step 1 — what it says
`choose-something-else-to-learn / step-1 / say`

### Step 2 — what it says
`choose-something-else-to-learn / step-2 / say`

### Step 3 — what it says
`choose-something-else-to-learn / step-3 / say`

## Reading the course list

### The question a learner taps
`reading-the-course-list / topic`

Reading a course card

### The heading on the walk
`reading-the-course-list / title`

Reading the course list

### Words that find this walk in the search box
`reading-the-course-list / keywords`

course, courses, card, list, for english speakers, speakers, direction, premium, try free, free, paid, pay, variants, northern, southern, which one, dot

### Step 1 — what it says
`reading-the-course-list / step-1 / say`

Each card names the language you would be learning, and under it, the language it would be taught in. That second line is the one worth reading — it is what you will hear the prompts in.

### Step 2 — what it says
`reading-the-course-list / step-2 / say`

The right-hand side tells you where you stand. A coloured dot and a position means you have already started that one and it is holding your place for you.

### Step 3 — what it says
`reading-the-course-list / step-3 / say`

**Premium** with **Try free** next to it means the course is a paid one, and that you can properly get going in it first. You carry on until the app asks — nothing stops you mid-thought and nothing is taken before you have said yes.

### Step 4 — what it says
`reading-the-course-list / step-4 / say`

Some languages offer more than one card — a different region or accent of the same language. Pick whichever you would rather end up sounding like.

### Step 4 — the line that closes the walk
`reading-the-course-list / step-4 / terminal`

Nothing here commits you. Tapping a card takes you into it, and every course you have touched keeps its own place waiting.

## What your numbers mean

### The question a learner taps
`what-your-numbers-mean / topic`

Your numbers

### The heading on the walk
`what-your-numbers-mean / title`

What your numbers mean

### Words that find this walk in the search box
`what-your-numbers-mean / keywords`

numbers, stats, activity, time, total time, minutes, hours, phrases, spoken, counting, count, tally

### Step 1 — what it says
`what-your-numbers-mean / step-1 / say`

These three are a record of what you have already done. None of them is a target, and none of them is being compared with anybody.

### Step 2 — what it says
`what-your-numbers-mean / step-2 / say`

Time is the time you have actually spent learning. If it ever shows a **~** in front of it, that is us working it out from how far along you are, because we have not logged the sessions themselves.

### Step 3 — what it says
`what-your-numbers-mean / step-3 / say`

Phrases is how many you have said out loud. It counts the sessions this device still remembers, so treat it as a recent tally rather than a lifetime one.

### Step 3 — the line that closes the walk
`what-your-numbers-mean / step-3 / terminal`

Nothing here changes what you get next. The course follows your position, not these numbers.

## Where you are in this course

### The question a learner taps
`where-you-are-in-this-course / topic`

Where you are

### The heading on the walk
`where-you-are-in-this-course / title`

Where you are in this course

### Words that find this walk in the search box
`where-you-are-in-this-course / keywords`

belt, belts, progress, position, how far, colour, colours, dots, bar, track, level, grade, score

### Step 1 — what it says
`where-you-are-in-this-course / step-1 / say`

This card is your whole position in the course in one glance. Nothing here is a score, and nothing here is counting anything against you.

### Step 2 — what it says
`where-you-are-in-this-course / step-2 / say`

Those eight coloured dots are **belts**, and the filled one is where you are now. A belt marks how far along the course you have come — it is a position, not a grade, and there is nothing to pass. You move to the next one simply by carrying on.

### Step 3 — what it says
`where-you-are-in-this-course / step-3 / say`

The bar underneath lays the whole course out end to end, with each belt as its own band of colour. The marker on it is you — so it shows how far you have come rather than how far is left.

### Step 4 — what it says
`where-you-are-in-this-course / step-4 / say`

Tap the card itself to open the belts up. You can look through everything you have met so far, and start again from any point you fancy revisiting.

### Step 4 — the line that closes the walk
`where-you-are-in-this-course / step-4 / terminal`

That's your position. Close this whenever you like and press play — it always picks up exactly where you left off.
