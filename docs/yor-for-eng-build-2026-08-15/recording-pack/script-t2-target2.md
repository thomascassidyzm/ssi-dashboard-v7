# Yoruba course recording script — Yoruba Voice 2

**Course:** Yoruba for English speakers · **Your part:** Yoruba Voice 2 (Yoruba)

**251 lines**, split into **5 sessions**. About 29 minutes of actual speaking in total — allow two to three times that with pauses and retakes.

> **Please read exactly what is written, including every tone mark and every dot under a letter.** They are part of the word, not decoration — `kọ́` and `kọ` are two different verbs. If a line looks wrong to you, **record it anyway as written, then tell us** — you are almost certainly right and we want to fix it.

## Before you start

1. Somewhere quiet. No music, no fan, no traffic if you can help it.
2. Phone or laptop is fine. Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to the side.
3. **Record one session into one single audio file.** Do not try to make a separate file per line.
4. At the very start of the file, say out loud: *"Yoruba Voice 2, session one"* (then two, three...). This is how we tell your files apart.
5. **Say the line number before each line**, e.g. *"one"* ... then the line. Then pause for a breath before the next one.
6. If you fluff a line, just pause, say *"again"*, and repeat it. We will keep the last good one.

Some lines are whole sentences, some are short building blocks, and some are a **single word on its own**. That is deliberate — learners are taught the pieces as well as the sentences. Say the short ones the way you would say them inside a normal sentence, not slowly or over-pronounced.
