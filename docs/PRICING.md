# SSi Products & Pricing — canonical (founder ruling, 2026-07-29)

The single source of truth for what we sell and at what price. Any price shown anywhere in the product, in partner material, or in billing code must trace back to this table.

## Price list

| Product | Monthly | Annual | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Individual learner** | £15 | £150 | Direct to SSi |
| **Family plan** (household) | £25 | £250 | Direct to SSi, single payer |
| **Tutor-group student** | £10 | £100 | Student of a tutor's group |
| **School student** | £5 | £50 | Comes via a school; paid direct to SSi |
| **Org / workplace seat** | £15 | £150 | Standard pricing, no volume scaling |

**Annual rule:** annual = monthly × 10 (two months free), across all products.

## Orgs / workplaces

- **Price:** £15/seat/month or £150/seat/year. Standard pricing — no volume scaling.
- **Billing:** card upfront only, via Paddle. Monthly or annual.
- **Seats:** reset monthly. Cancel anytime. Access expires at the end of the paid plan period.
- **Trial:** 30-day free trial covering all languages.
- **Seat model:** the same as for teachers in schools.
- **Upgrade:** converts the trial org in place — no re-provisioning — and is available at any time during the trial, not only at expiry.

## Revenue splits

- **Individual & family:** SSi keeps everything (less payment commission/transaction costs).
- **School students:** the £5/month comes straight to SSi in full.
- **Tutor-group students:** of the £10/month, SSi keeps £5 and the tutor receives £5 **paid one month in arrears** — the delay ensures the student has stayed and nothing has been claimed back before the tutor's share is released.

## The invariant

**SSi's floor is £5/month per learner regardless of channel.** School rate = the floor; tutor rate = floor + tutor's £5; direct rates = floor + full-service margin. Any future price or partner deal should preserve this floor.
