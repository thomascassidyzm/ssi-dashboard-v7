## Notes on the count

- Distinct texts are the union of seed, LEGO and practice-phrase Welsh texts across the four Welsh courses, compared after lowercasing, trimming and stripping punctuation — the same comparison used in the 2026-08-13 estate recount, so the totals line up with it exactly (11,721 distinct texts over 13,164 slots).
- A text counts as recorded when a live clip marked as human-recorded exists for it, matched on the same stripped comparison. Clips still marked pending are not counted as recorded.
- The four Welsh courses share one language code, so the north and south courses are deduplicated against each other. Treating a north text as covered only by a north recording moves the figure by 8 texts (1,084 rather than 1,076), so the dialect split makes almost no difference to the size of the job.
- Straight and curly apostrophes are compared as written. Unifying them would match only 3 further texts, so apostrophe style is not hiding any meaningful amount of already-recorded material.
- The Welsh anthem course for Japanese speakers has nothing outstanding.
