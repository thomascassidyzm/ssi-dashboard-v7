## Two things I had to decide, said plainly

**The course.** `zho_for_spa` does not exist — no course row, no LEGOs. I used **`zho_for_eng`**: live, 1,190 LEGOs, the real canonical Chinese course, which is the nearest thing to "Chinese, real course". If you meant a different one, the run is 0.9K credits and re-firing costs nothing.

**Which 20 Italian LEGOs.** I could not find a named set anywhere — not in this thread, not in estate-search, not in any other room's job. So I took **20 sampled evenly across `ita_for_eng`'s 1,457 LEGOs**, by the same rule as the Chinese 50, which at least makes the two directly comparable to each other. **If the "20 Italian LEGOs" are a specific existing set, tell me where and I will re-render to match them line for line** — that is the one thing a wrong guess would actually corrupt, since it is the measured baseline.

**Sampling rule**, so it is reproducible: every *n*th LEGO in course order, `n = total / sample size`. No cherry-picking, no skipping the awkward ones. The manifest with every `lego_id` is at `sampling-run/manifest.json`.

## What to listen for

The known side is the register you already approved. **The target side is the new question** — this is your English-cloned voice speaking Mandarin and Italian, steered only by `locale`. Chinese is the hard case: tone is carried in the voice, and a clone built from 6 seconds of English has never been asked for it.

Both sides of each LEGO are stacked so you can hear the pair the way a learner does.
