## What this asks

**The English side is the register you have already approved.** The Chinese side is the real question: your English-cloned voice — built from six seconds of you speaking English — being asked for Mandarin, steered only by a `locale` parameter.

**Tone is the thing to listen for.** Mandarin carries meaning in pitch contour, and nothing in the source audio ever taught this clone to do that. If it fails, this is where it fails, and it will not fail subtly.

**Sampling rule**, reproducible: every *n*th LEGO in course order, `n = 1190 / 50 = 23`. No cherry-picking and no skipping the awkward ones — the sample includes tone-heavy single syllables like 难 and 正在 precisely because they are the hard cases. Every `lego_id` is in the committed manifest.
