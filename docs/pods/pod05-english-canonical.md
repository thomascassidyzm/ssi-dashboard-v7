## 1. Do You Mind If We Don't Use English? — *The language contract*

*Difficulty: beginner · 3 sentences*

| # | Speaker | English |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Learner | Do you mind if we don't use English? I know I'm asking a lot… and it takes a lot to be patient enough… to speak [target language]… with someone who doesn't speak it very well… but I really want to improve… and that means I really need to practise. |
| 2 | Friend | No, that's fine… I'm happy to speak only [target language]. Do you understand what I'm saying at the moment? |
| 3 | Learner | I understand a lot of it, I think. Maybe I understand most of it… when you speak slowly. If I don't know how to answer… I'll ask you to say it again more slowly. |

## 2. Where Do You Come From? — *Origins, and why you're learning*

*Difficulty: beginner · 2 sentences*

| # | Speaker | English |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Friend | Okay, that's perfect. So, where do you come from… and why are you learning [target language]? |
| 2 | Learner | I come from [home country]… and I live in [home city] at the moment. I've been living there for about five years. I'm learning [target language]… because I want to visit [target country]… on holiday this year… and I think it's polite… to learn a little of the language… wherever you go. Also, I have a friend who speaks [target language]… and it would be really nice… for me to be able to speak… a little of her language as well. My family are looking forward… to going on holiday to [target country]… but they feel nervous… about learning [target language]… so I'm going to need to be able… to order food in restaurants… and maybe ask questions in shops and so on. |

## 3. Do You Speak a Lot of Languages? — *Learning styles*

*Difficulty: intermediate · 2 sentences*

| # | Speaker | English |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Friend | Do you speak a lot of languages? |
| 2 | Learner | I usually say… that I only really speak two languages well… but I'm also upper intermediate… with two others. And then I can say a few words… in several other languages… because I always want to be polite… when I travel. But I'm very lazy… so I always like to find… the easiest way to learn a new language. I don't like learning… lists of vocabulary… or studying grammar… I get bored very quickly. As a result of that… I'm always looking for courses… that focus mostly on speaking and listening. I really enjoy starting… to have conversations in a new language… even though it feels very difficult at first. I'm disappointed… that I don't speak [target language]… well enough to joke… because I like being light-hearted… and not taking things too seriously. But it's very difficult to tell jokes… in a new language… because people just think… they're not understanding you properly. |

## 6. Tell Me About Your Family — *Turning the tables*

*Difficulty: intermediate · 8 sentences*

| # | Speaker | English |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Friend | I think you can speak [target language] very well… for someone who hasn't been learning… for a very long time… congratulations. Do you have any questions… you'd like to ask me… while we're still talking? |
| 2 | Learner | That's a good question. The truth is, I'm not sure. It's not easy to think of something to ask! So, let me think. How about your family? Yes, tell me about your family. |
| 3a | Friend | I'm not married. I don't have a partner at the moment. |
| 3b | Friend | I do have a partner. My husband works in a tech company. |
| 4a | Friend | We don't have children. |
| 4b | Friend | We've got three children, two girls and a boy. |
| 5 | Friend | We live in [target city]… but I'd like us to move out of the city… maybe when either my husband or I… can get a raise. But it would be awkward for my husband… to live outside the city with his work… so we'll have to figure out… what we might be able to do later on. I'd really like the children to grow up… with more fields and trees around them. I don't think it's very healthy… to live in the middle of the city. And it gets far too hot in the summer… I really don't like it when it's hot. I prefer to be cold… because at least you can put on… an extra sweater or a coat or something. How about you? Do you want to keep living… in the same place you are now… or would you like to move to somewhere else? |
| 6 | Learner | We're very happy where we are… but we would like to be able… to travel a bit more. We really like visiting other countries… and meeting people… from different backgrounds and different cultures… people who speak different languages. That's always really interesting. |
