## The one line of numbers you asked for

Aran has recorded **137 clips** on `cym_n_for_eng` between 15 June and 10 August, averaging 3.6 seconds. Against the whole course that sits on 13,592 distinct texts with 19,914 human clips already in place; the outstanding recording worklist is **1,076 distinct texts still to record**.

## Two things I found while assembling this, which you did not ask for

**26 of Aran's 137 recent clips are dead.** They are all exactly 834 bytes, all stamped 15 June 2026, and all on the *English* track rather than the Welsh one — "Good morning, Sarah!", "My name is Anna. And you?". That is the signature of a known bad write, and one of them carries a duration of 10.8 seconds against 834 bytes of file, so the database thinks it is a real recording. I pulled them out of the list above; if I had not, you would have tapped one and heard nothing and reasonably concluded Aran's recording was faulty. It is not — the write was.

**There are 409 TTS clips sitting on Welsh courses.** Including 190 on a *Japanese* voice, `ja-JP-ShioriNeural`, and 70 on your own clone. They date from March, April and July 2026 — all before the permanent Welsh TTS exclusion went in, and the guard now blocks new ones. Nothing is being synthesised over Aran and Catrin today. But these are still there, and they are not a render backlog to fill; they are old clips to decide about. I have not touched them — deleting generated assets needs a plan and your say-so.

Neither of these is a verdict on the recordings themselves. That is what the twelve clips above are for.
