standing text-approval policy (`docs/pods/text-approval-policy-2026-08-16.md`), no worker signs

**No audio pass is queued by this document, and none may be queued until the draft flag clears.**

**What this is.** The Italian target side of the learning flagship
(`docs/pods/learning-flagship-pod-2026-08-30.md` — 11 chapters, 367 turns, English-only by
commission; its header names the language layer "a later overlay". This is that overlay.) The
English is canonical and untouched. For each chapter this document gives: the **full authored
Italian dialogue**, then the **mapping table** — `chunk | Italian | class | note`, classes
**D** deterministic / **S** split / **I** inversion / **E** erasure — in the shape of the North
Welsh health overlay (`docs/sector-pods/health-welsh-pair-overlay-2026-09-01.md`) as adapted
for dialogue by the Method Pod overlay
(`method-pod-italian-pair-overlay-2026-09-01.md`), whose §0 frame ledger **this document
shares and obeys**: one English intention → exactly one Italian form, across the pair of
documents. Convergence is reception and welcome; divergence is resolved by re-cutting, never
by blocking a word.

**Register:** *tu* throughout, both speakers, old friends, spoken — the Method Pod's settled
call and the standing tu-first law, taken as this overlay's default and flagged as such (one
Aran-look to overturn; the change would be mechanical). Loanword rule inherited (R2): *pattern,
lockdown, telemetry → la telemetria* (assimilated), *pysgod*, *woo*, *Talk Bollocks*, "to
boldly go" stay as the things they are; methodology terms travel as the Method Pod ruled them
(*collegamenti* for edges).

**Mapping granularity, stated:** chunk size rides at conversation granularity per the ruling —
a LEGO is the smallest chunk that is deterministic known→target. This pod is denser than the
Method Pod (median turn 18 words against 8), so uniformly-deterministic stretches inside holds
map as sentence-sized chunks with their machinery noted inside; every inversion, erasure,
split, frame and near-neighbour point gets its own row. The near-neighbour rows feed the
consolidated register (`spanish-italian-near-neighbour-register-2026-09-01.md`).

**Ledger additions this document mints** (shared back with the Method Pod overlay, no
collisions): "properly" → *come si deve* · "parked/noted and parked" → *parcheggiato* (kept
distinct from the pause ledger — "on pause" is *in pausa* and only that) · "Asserted." →
*Asserito.* · "the repair budget" → *il budget di riparazione* (budget the loanword Italian
actually uses) · "the ladder" → *la scala* (Method Pod Ch. 6 already owns *una scala* for the
learner's ladder — same intention, same form ✓) · "the assembly" → *l'assemblaggio* · "rung" →
*piolo* · "the fortress" → *la fortezza* · "creed" → *credo*.
