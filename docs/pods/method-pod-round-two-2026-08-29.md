The methodology talk — items and edges, effort in memory, one-sentence value, play — is the mask over those shapes for this particular pod, because it is these two learners' daily conversational water.

| | English | Italiano |

| TOM | It's not that you know a person because you know facts about them. | Non è che conosci una persona perché sai delle cose su di lei. |

### Rung 1 — the floor

### Scene 5 — *Non lo so* (I don't know) — **ZERO-ADMISSION**

**Owns:** everything — verified below. **Newly admits:** the shape only — the honest "I don't know" held with status, plus the question-as-handover. This is the response family the live canon most conspicuously lacks: in 231 pod sentences nobody ever fails to know anything (`/d/1bf2b483`). **Attested:** 1:54, 4:70–77. The scene deliberately ends on the handover, ball in the air — that is the shape.

| | English | Italiano |
|---|---|---|
| ARAN | Is this a good idea, or a bad idea? | È una buona idea, o una cattiva idea? |
| TOM | I don't know. | Non lo so. |
| ARAN | You don't know? | Non lo sai? |
| TOM | No. I don't know, and you don't know. | No. Io non lo so, e tu non lo sai. |
| ARAN | You're right. I don't know. | Hai ragione. Non lo so. |
| TOM | Good. Now we can start to think. What do you think? | Bene. Adesso possiamo iniziare a pensare. Tu cosa pensi? |

**Shape witnessed (general node):** NOT-KNOWING AS FLOOR — a direct question is met with a flat "I don't know" that loses no status, the not-knowing is confirmed and shared, and the shared blank space is then explicitly converted into licence to think together, closed by handing the first move to the partner. Any subject — a diagnosis, a recipe, a chess position — can walk this node.

**Surface forms admitted:** none — zero-admission. **Verified against live `ita_for_eng:pod-1` (231 sentences, read-only query 2026-08-29):** every English content word — idea, good, bad, know, right, now, start, think — occurs in the canon (e.g. "That's a bad idea", "Do you know where…", "Am I correct? / Ho ragione", "iniziare a parlare", "penso sia educato").

**Near-neighbour points:** *Non lo so* sits one letter off *no lo sé* — an anchor that then bites: the Italian keeps the clitic ALWAYS, where Spanish freely drops it ("no sé"); *sai/sabes* — cognate root, non-matching ending; *hai ragione* — avere where Spanish reaches for *tener* (*tienes razón*), the systemic avere/tener remap on its most frequent idiom.

### Scene 6 — *Cento ore* (A hundred hours) — **ZERO-ADMISSION**

**Owns:** everything — verified below; turn 3's "E poi?" is verbatim canon (pod-1 sentence 125). **Newly admits:** the shape only — the precision haggle: bid, counterbid, convergence, settlement formula. **Attested:** 2:120–132, closed by Aran's actual settlement line "Chop them how you want".

| | English | Italiano |
|---|---|---|
| TOM | How much time is it going to take? | Quanto tempo ci vorrà? |
| ARAN | A lot. | Tanto. |
| TOM | How much? Ten hours? | Quanto? Dieci ore? |
| ARAN | More. Ten hours — and then ten more. | Di più. Dieci ore — e poi altre dieci. |
| TOM | And then? So how much altogether? | E poi? Quindi quanto in tutto? |
| ARAN | A hundred hours. Take them as you want. | Cento ore. Prendile come vuoi. |

**Shape witnessed (general node):** THE PRECISION HAGGLE — a vague quantity is refused, bids and counterbids narrow it in alternate turns, and the converged number is handed over with a settlement formula that gives the partner ownership of how to spend it. Works for hours, money, tomato plants, kilometres.

**Surface forms admitted:** none — zero-admission, with one honest caveat: "hundred" appears in the canon as the spoken numbers line "100. 200. 1000." (pod-1 sentence 140) — attested aurally as a word, on the page as digits. Every other content word — time, take ("quanto tempo pensa che ci vorrà"), a lot, ten, hours, more ("altri due bicchieri"), then ("E poi?"), altogether ("in tutto") — is attested in full.

**Near-neighbour points:** *ci vorrà* — the ci+volere construction for "it will take" has NO Spanish neighbour (*tardará*, *llevará*); the Spanish-adjacent learner reaches and finds nothing, which is the point; *in tutto* against *en total*; *prendile* — imperative with attached clitic, where the Spanish shape (*tómalas*) matches exactly — an anchor placed right after the trap.

### Scene 7 — *Un tiro di dadi* (One roll of the dice)

**Owns:** question-and-answer floor (pod-1 passim); the reformulation staple. **Newly admits:** the epistemic status flag — rating your own answer before giving it. **Attested:** 2:294–296 ("I'm out of practice there… but if I was allowed to give one roll of the dice"), 2:349.

| | English | Italiano |
|---|---|---|
| TOM | Schools. How would you do it in a school? | Le scuole. Come lo faresti in una scuola? |
| ARAN | It's been a long time since I've worked in schools. I'm out of practice. | È da tanto tempo che non lavoro nelle scuole. Sono fuori allenamento. |
| TOM | But? | Però? |
| ARAN | But if you give me one roll of the dice — one day a week. | Però se mi dai un solo tiro di dadi — un giorno alla settimana. |
| TOM | That's a big bite out of the timetable. | È un bel morso all'orario. |
| ARAN | It looks big. But I suspect you wouldn't even need the whole year. | Sembra grande. Ma sospetto che non servirebbe nemmeno l'anno intero. |

**Shape witnessed (general node):** THE FLAGGED GUESS — before answering, the speaker prices their own authority down ("out of practice"), the partner explicitly buys the guess anyway ("But?"), and the speculation is then given at full strength wrapped in provisional markers ("one roll of the dice", "I suspect"). The flag is what licenses the confidence. Reusable wherever one partner is asked outside their patch.

**Surface forms admitted:** *fuori allenamento*, *un tiro di dadi*, *un morso*, *l'orario*, *sospetto che* + subjunctive-flavoured tail, *alla settimana*. Availability only.

**Near-neighbour points:** *è da tanto tempo che non…* — Italian builds the since-construction on essere where Spanish builds on *hacer* (*hace mucho que no…*): a systemic remap on a high-frequency frame; *però* against *pero* — the anchor with a stress-and-vowel trap; *alla settimana* against *a la semana* — near-identical, deliberate ground-holding after two traps.

### Rung 2 — response positions between equals

### Scene 8 — *Troppo complicato* (Too complicated)

**Owns:** question-as-handover (round one Scene 4's staple); backchannel. **Newly admits:** Aran's razor — complexity-as-refutation, a flat verdict allowed to stand. The canon has no flat refusal of a claim anywhere; this is the house version. **Attested:** 1:88–97. Subject: grammar-in-the-head — the razor's home context, conversational water.

| | English | Italiano |
|---|---|---|
| TOM | So the rules of grammar — where do they live, in the head? | Quindi le regole della grammatica — dove stanno, nella testa? |
| ARAN | Ask the question at the level of neurons. How would it even work? | Fai la domanda al livello dei neuroni. Come funzionerebbe, anche solo in teoria? |
| TOM | A parallel system? Word types, categories, a machine for order… | Un sistema parallelo? Tipi di parole, categorie, una macchina per l'ordine… |
| ARAN | Listen to yourself. It gets complicated very fast. | Ascoltati. Diventa complicato molto in fretta. |
| TOM | And so? | E quindi? |
| ARAN | It's complicated — so it can't be a thing. | È complicato — quindi non può essere una cosa vera. |

**Shape witnessed (general node):** THE RAZOR VERDICT — a hypothesis is invited to elaborate itself, the elaboration is allowed to run until its own weight shows ("listen to yourself"), and then a one-line house heuristic closes it, flat, no hedge, no apology — and the partner's "E quindi?" shows the verdict was requested, not imposed. Any domain with a house heuristic can walk this node.

**Surface forms admitted:** *le regole*, *la testa*, *al livello di*, *funzionerebbe*, *ascoltati*, *in fretta*, *una cosa vera*. Availability only.

**Near-neighbour points:** *diventa* — selection by absence: Spanish has no cognate (*se vuelve*, *se convierte*), the learner must select rather than transpose; *in fretta* against *deprisa* — no bridge; *quindi* again, deliberately recurring across scenes as the set's connective spine, against *así que/entonces*.

### Scene 9 — *Che fine ha fatto?* (What happened to it?)

**Owns:** anecdote-with-flag (round one Scene 2). **Newly admits:** the audit question — a warm challenge landing inside the partner's glowing story, answered honestly, with no loss of face and a joint diagnosis extracted. **Attested:** 4:144–169 ("And what's happened to that group now?" / "it's gone a little bit cold" / "how many people were involved?" / "they're caught in the capitalist structure"). Surface masked from the think-tank to a street food-growing project — same shape, off-stage subject.

| | English | Italiano |
|---|---|---|
| TOM | A few years ago we had a group — brilliant people. We wanted to grow food for the whole street. | Qualche anno fa avevamo un gruppo — gente in gamba. Volevamo coltivare cibo per tutta la via. |
| ARAN | Sounds fantastic. And what happened to that group? | Sembra fantastico. E che fine ha fatto, quel gruppo? |
| TOM | …It's gone a bit cold. | …Si è un po' raffreddato. |
| ARAN | How many of you were there? | Quanti eravate? |
| TOM | Fifteen. All of them busy. All of them time-poor. | Quindici. Tutti impegnati. Tutti poveri di tempo. |
| ARAN | There's your answer. It wasn't the idea that failed. | Ecco la risposta. Non è stata l'idea a fallire. |

**Shape witnessed (general node):** THE AUDIT INSIDE THE GLOW — enthusiasm is ratified first ("sounds fantastic"), then audited with one pointed question about present state; the honest deflating answer costs nothing, because the auditor immediately converts it into a joint diagnosis that acquits the idea and names the real constraint. The move that makes honest deflation SAFE. Reusable for any abandoned project, diet, band, or garden.

**Surface forms admitted:** *gente in gamba*, *coltivare*, *che fine ha fatto*, *raffreddarsi*, *impegnati*, *poveri di tempo*, *fallire*. Availability only.
