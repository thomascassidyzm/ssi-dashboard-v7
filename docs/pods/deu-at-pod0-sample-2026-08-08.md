## Two things for your ear, not the machine's

1. **Austrian, or German German?** The voices Sonja and Felix are the German pool, not an
   Austrian one — there is no separate Austrian voice pool. The *words* are properly Austrian,
   carried over from how this course already speaks. Whether the accent is close enough is your
   call, and it applies to the whole fleet, not just this course.
2. **Aran's script says miles and pounds.** Scene 2 measures the distance into town in miles and
   a couple of scenes price things in pounds. In an Austrian course those would be kilometres and
   euros. The translation followed the English faithfully rather than quietly changing it. Say
   the word and it gets changed here and everywhere else it appears.
