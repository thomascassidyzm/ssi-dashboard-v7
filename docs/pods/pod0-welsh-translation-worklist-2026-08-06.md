# Welsh pod-0 — the lines that need a person

Built 2026-08-06 against Aran's 2026-08-06 pod-0 canonical (231 sentences, 22 scenes).

Nothing here was translated by a machine and nothing was overwritten. These are the
lines where Welsh already exists but the English it was written for has changed, so a
human has to decide whether the Welsh still stands. Until someone does, the slot
carries **no Welsh** and is **not in the recording queue** — a recorder cannot be
served it by accident.
