## 3. A drill tail that had to be repaired

*Scene 14's coda was drafted as "100. 200. 1000. Nedjelja. 12 sati." In Croatian a digit followed by a full stop is the ORDINAL, so the engine would have said "hundredth, two-hundredth". Eight tails had this. All eight are now spelled out in words.*

**Narrator** · Gabrijela · draft text

> 100. 200. 1000. Sunday. 12 o'clock.
