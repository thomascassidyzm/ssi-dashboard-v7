# Show notes: Tom and Aran talk about the environment

Recorded in North Wales, looking out over the Menai towards Ynys Môn. The conversation is kept as it was said; only the speech-to-text's mishearings were fixed.

## One line changed in the text

- **The data centre's water.** Aran said it would take water from the Menai Strait. On the developer's own account it takes Dŵr Cymru mains drinking water: a "most likely" 700,000 litres a day, up to almost 12 million at peak. Only clean rainwater would go into the Strait. The English keeps his words; the Italian translation says mains water ("l'acqua della rete idrica"). [North Wales Chronicle](https://www.northwaleschronicle.co.uk/news/26562052.developers-data-centre-near-caernarfon-defend-plans/)

## Worth knowing

- **Small nuclear reactors and waste.** Tom says small reactors produce no waste. They do produce radioactive waste, and a 2022 PNAS study estimated small modular reactors may produce 2 to 30 times more waste per unit of energy than large reactors. The nuclear industry disputes that estimate. Nuclear's safety record, in deaths per unit of energy, is among the best of any source. [Krall, Macfarlane and Ewing, PNAS 2022](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2111833119), [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/safest-sources-of-energy)
- **The nuclear station on Ynys Môn.** The old Wylfa station closed in 2015 and is not being recommissioned. The site has been chosen for new Rolls-Royce small modular reactors, with a delivery contract signed in April 2026. [World Nuclear News](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/contract-signed-for-delivery-of-uks-first-smrs)
- **"Ask 100 physicists."** There is no poll of physicists on small reactors. Scientists do lean more pro-nuclear than the public: 65% of AAAS scientists favoured more nuclear plants in a 2015 Pew survey, against 45% of US adults. [Pew Research 2015](https://www.pewresearch.org/science/2015/01/29/public-and-scientists-views-on-science-and-society/)
- **Trees warning each other.** Airborne warnings between plants are well supported. Warnings and sharing through fungal networks are contested: a 2023 review found those claims poorly supported. [Karst, Jones and Hoeksema 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-023-01986-1)
- **The sweating horse.** Base and superstructure is Marx's idea, but the image of culture as the heat off a sweating horse could not be traced to Marx.
- **Computational irreducibility.** Wolfram's point is that there is no shortcut: you have to run every step. It shows up even in tiny programs, so the size of the computer is not the point. [Wolfram, A New Kind of Science, ch. 12](https://www.wolframscience.com/nks/p737--computational-irreducibility/)
- **Smoking.** The evidence was settled in the 1950s and the US Surgeon General reported in 1964; the tobacco industry held out until the late 1990s, and UK smoking bans came in 2006 and 2007.
- **Hiding in Argentina.** Peter Thiel bought a house in Buenos Aires in 2026 and moved his family there for a time. "A bunch of them" is more than the reporting shows. [Fortune, July 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/07/16/the-global-rich-peter-thiel-safe-haven-argentina/)
- **The internal combustion engine.** Practical engines date from the 1860s and 1870s, so about 200 years puts the end in the 2060s. Road traffic kills about 1.19 million people a year. [WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries)
- **Sabre-toothed tigers.** They died out about 10,000 years ago. Humans are one suspected cause, alongside climate change; it is still debated.
- **Old growth forests "there for millions of years".** Old growth stands last centuries to millennia; forests as a kind of ecosystem go back about 385 million years.
- **Battersea Power Station** is Grade II* listed and was redeveloped as flats and shops, opening in 2022.
