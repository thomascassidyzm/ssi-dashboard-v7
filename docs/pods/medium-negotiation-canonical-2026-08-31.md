## One page — the verdict

**One canonical shape comes out, not a family: N1201 "Medium contract"** — the name is kept from
the proposed N101, because the stripped shape is recognisably the same object. What the stripping
changed *inside* it:

1. **The shape is role-neutral.** In the health corpus the *professional* is the learner and the
   patient is the native — the exact inversion of the Bologna case where the learner is the
   customer. Nothing in the positions depends on who serves whom; either party may be the one
   declaring the limitation. N101 never said this; N1201 says it explicitly.
2. **Position 1 has two attested realisations, and only one of them asks permission.** The Welsh
   flows are a medium *offer*: two shared languages exist, the speaker proposes conducting business
   in the weaker one, names a fallback, and asks. The English flows ("English isn't my first
   language, so if you have any trouble understanding me, just let me know") are a medium *notice*:
   no alternative medium exists, so there is nothing to accept or decline — the declaration plus
   the repair licence is the whole content. Same shape, different admission. This is the strongest
   single piece of evidence in the corpus that the shape is not "language-learner asks permission
   to practise" but something broader: **a declared limitation on the medium of the encounter, plus
   a standing repair licence, issued before any business starts** — which is N101's own gloss,
   already stripped without knowing it. The decline family exists only on the offer side, because
   only an offer can be declined.
3. **The stakes-raiser is sector-conditional, not canonical.** "It's really important that you
   understand everything" belongs to the duty-of-care register of health, not to the shape. In
   Bologna it vanishes from the café and reappears — correctly — the moment the pharmacist reaches
   the dosage.
4. **The face-repair tail is canonical in function, cultural in form.** Both attested declines end
   in mutual repair ("no offence meant" / "none taken at all"); the *function* — yield instantly,
   ratify the choice as sensible, de-personalise the refusal, re-anchor rapport — is the canonical
   recovery. The *offence-disclaimer form* is a Welsh agreement: declining Welsh in a Welsh
   institution is politically freighted and both parties disarm it. Declining Italian in Bologna
   is not, so the tail shrinks to a bare warm ratification. Strip the form, keep the function.

**The decline family:** S1201 (the offer declined, canonical re-cut of S1101 — and the "busy
decline" is the *same edge* with a different account, refused as a separate mint), S1202 (the offer
diverted into biography — a genuine third branch, newly minted, attested twice). Three commissioned
members are **honest gaps**: the switch-back mid-conversation, the third party who does not know
you are learning, and the over-accommodation. None is attested anywhere in the 73 flows; none is
minted.

**CORE siting:** a prologue — scene 0, before the first transaction — which *prepends* to CORE
without reordering anything, so the audit's constraint ("CORE's existing order does not change")
is honoured. The decline branch attaches at position 2 of the prologue in exactly the mechanics of
the six recovery attachments.

**The Bologna acceptance test: PASS**, with three named costs (below, §6).

**On invariance, in #448's terms:** this document makes a *shape-layer* claim under the metagraph
ruling — shapes are compressions, surface forms are agreements, culture is a sector — not a
frame-inventory claim. #448 re-cut the frame inventory as KNOWN-CORPUS-invariant, not
pair-invariant; nothing here disagrees, because the objects differ. Where this material touches
#448's layer at all — the opener's fixed known-side lines — its constraint applies unchanged: pods
contribute frame attestation and **zero vocabulary**, so the Bologna instantiation below
demonstrates that the *shape* instantiates and asserts nothing about whether `ita_for_eng` (or any
pair) has cut the target chunks to say it.

## 2. The canonical shape — N1201 "Medium contract"

Role-neutral: either party — server or served, professional or customer — may be the declarer.
Gloss: **one party declares a limitation on the medium of the encounter itself and pre-negotiates
the repair protocol before any business starts; the licence is issued in advance of the trouble it
covers.** (N101's gloss, kept — it was already stripped.)

Positions:

1. **Declare own limitation on the medium** — two attested realisations:
   **(a) the offer**: a shared alternative medium exists; the declarer proposes conducting the
   encounter in the weaker medium, names the fallback, and asks ("I'm learning X… I'll try to stay
   in X… if we get stuck I might ask you to speak Y, if that's okay"). **(b) the notice**: no
   alternative exists; the declaration and the repair licence fuse into one turn ("X isn't my first
   language, so if anything isn't clear, just tell me"). Only the offer can be declined.
2. **Grant + reassure** — or one of the three branches: declined (S1201), diverted into biography
   (S1202), or met with a participant admission (C1203).
3. **Issue the standing repair licence** — "just stop me, any time at all"; at full strength it is
   mutual ("and I'll do the same if I don't follow you") and pre-disarmed ("I won't be offended",
   "it's never a silly question"). In the notice realisation this fuses into position 1.
4. **Accept the licence.**
5. **Pivot to business** (pivot-capable).

**What survives of N101's five positions:** positions 2–5 survive unchanged in name; position 1 is
re-worded from "declare own limitation + ask licence" to the two-realisation form above, because
"ask licence" presumes the offer variant and the corpus's own English flows never ask. The `why_not
an_existing_node` argument survives verbatim (N6 is trouble handled when it arrives; N1201 is the
licence granted before it arrives; N9 asks whether the world permits X; N1201 declares that the
*speaker* may fail; nothing in the ratified 29 negotiates the medium of the encounter). The smoke
test survives and is now *passed in writing* — §6 is the instantiation.

**Refused as a second shape — the "medium notice"** (recorded, not recommended): same positions
minus the ask; a realisation of position 1, not a shape. One shape with branches beats two shapes
sharing four positions.

## 3. The decline family — survivability edges on N1201

### S1201 — the medium offer declined (canonical re-cut of S1101)

The native opts out of the offered medium. Attested twice, recovery complete both times:

- **1.0 Welsh flow 3** — *"Actually, I'd rather stick to English today if that's alright, love.
  I'm not feeling up to it."* → instant yield → mutual face-repair.
- **2.0 Welsh flow 2** — *"To be honest, doctor, with something medical I'd feel safer in English.
  I want to be sure I follow everything."* → *"That's completely sensible, and exactly the right
  instinct."* → *"None taken at all - you've done me a favour."*

The canonical recovery, in four moves: **yield instantly** (no relitigating, no second offer),
**ratify the choice as sensible**, **de-personalise the refusal**, **re-anchor rapport and pivot**.
The account is a *slot* with two attested types: **capacity** ("not feeling up to it"), scoped to
today — an implicit licence to re-offer next time; and **stakes** ("with something medical"),
scoped to a domain — an implicit licence to keep the medium *outside* that domain. The
offence-disclaimer tail is stripped as cultural form (verdict point 4); what canon keeps is the
de-personalising function, however a culture spells it.

**The "busy decline": refused as a separate mint, one line** — same positions, different account;
the account is a slot, not a shape (and the calibration read already cited 1.0 Welsh flow 3 as an
S1101 attestation, so minting it separately would split one edge's own evidence in two).

### S1202 — the offer diverted into biography (new mint)

The declaration is met with neither grant nor decline but with a personal question about the
declarer's own story — and acceptance of the medium happens silently, by conduct. Attested twice,
once on each side of the realisation split, recovery complete both times:

- **1.0 Welsh flow 2** — *"Where are you learning, then? Are you doing the classes?"* → biography
  answered → *"That's lovely to hear. Right, shall we make a start? Just stop me if I lose you."*
- **1.0 English flow 2** — *"Where are you from, if you don't mind me asking?"* → biography
  answered → *"The weather takes some getting used to! But the people are kind. Now then, let's
  have a look at you."*

The recovery is the same both times: **answer the biography warmly, then self-initiate the pivot**
— the diversion never returns you to business; you must take yourself back, and (first attestation)
the repair licence gets issued on the way through. Why an edge and not just a composition: the
interlude's *turns* are N5 Acquaintance mechanics (recorded as C1201, exactly as C109 recorded N7
inside the decline), but no composition edge captures what the learner must *survive* — a granted
contract with no grant ever spoken, and a pivot nobody else will make. The learner who waits for
"of course, that's fine" before proceeding will wait forever.

**Not minted — the participant admission**: the family helper (1.0 English flow 3, 2.0 English
flow 2) is a feasibility ask composing into position 2 (C1203) — a third party joining *with
everyone's knowledge*. It is a branch of the contract's negotiation, not a threat to its premise,
and N9's own mechanics cover the turns. Refused with that one line.

## 4. Honest gaps — commissioned members not attested, not minted

- **The switch-back mid-conversation** (the medium agreed, then abandoned partway through).
  Swept all 73 flows: once an opener completes, no flow ever revisits the medium. **Not attested,
  not minted.** What would attest it: a corpus whose stakes *escalate mid-flow* — the natural site
  already exists in this corpus's safety-critical flows (1.4 flow 3, refusing tablets; 1.5 flow 3,
  wrong tablets), where a real professional would drop into the stronger medium for the critical
  span and return after; a health v4 or any sector corpus with escalation would attest it in six
  lines. Note it may be *scoped* rather than total — the stakes-account decline (S1201) already
  attests domain-scoping at the opener; the switch-back is plausibly the same scoping arriving
  late. That reading stays speculation until a corpus writes it down.
- **The third party who does not know you are learning.** Not attested; the corpus's only third
  parties are announced helpers (C1203). What would attest it: any corpus where a colleague,
  family member or second customer *arrives mid-encounter unbriefed* — a workplace or social
  corpus, not a bedside one where the cast is fixed at two. Distinct from the participant
  admission and must stay distinct (the admission joins the contract; the stranger tests it).
- **The over-accommodation** (the native slows to unnatural speech — a decline in effect, not in
  form). Not attested, and structurally the hardest of the three: the *trigger* is paralinguistic
  — it lives in delivery, not in lines — so a text corpus may never attest it cleanly. What a text
  corpus *can* attest is the learner's recovery line ("you don't need to slow down — I'll stop you
  if I'm lost"): a corpus that stages that line would attest the edge from its recovery side.
  Audio-side evidence (real recordings of native-with-learner speech) would attest the trigger
  directly. Until one of those exists, this is the family's best-known and least-evidenced member,
  and it stays off the list.

## 5. The CORE siting

Tom has ruled the column: CORE, and currently nowhere. What follows is the placement, argued in
the compulsory-set audit's own idiom.

**The bar.** The audit's CORE test: *a learner who never meets it is missing something they must
own.* N1201 passes it more strongly than anything on the existing list, because it is upstream of
the list itself: **you cannot practise the ask if you never got permission to speak.** Every one of
the 18 — the transaction, the arrangement, repair itself — presupposes that an encounter in the
target language is already underway; N1201 is the shape that makes that true. And its licence is
what makes CORE's own repair economy work in the wild: S3 says repair is "the prerequisite to
asking anything at all", and N1201 is repair's *insurance policy* — the licence bought before the
trouble, which the corpus shows being exercised mid-business (§2.5 flow 2). S1201 is the
survivability edge on the learner's own existence in the language: the offer refused, at zero
cost, with the refuser saying thank you. A learner who has never walked that branch abandons the
language the first time a native answers in English.

**The placement: a prologue — scene 0, before CORE's first transaction.** The corpus fixes this
itself: "**Before we start** -" (2.0 Welsh flow 1) — after the hello, before any business, before
even names (§1.0 precedes §1.1 "Names and first meeting"). Watson's read — "the first thing in the
trunk, not an addition to it" — is confirmed with one precision: it is first *after N1's open*,
which the contract wraps rather than replaces; the hello has already happened when the declaration
runs.

**Does siting it first recut CORE? No — and this is the load-bearing point.** The audit's
constraint, honoured by the six recovery attachments, is that *CORE's existing order does not
change*. A prologue **prepends**: no existing scene is reordered, no existing walk changes, the
set grows 18 → 19 — which Tom's ruling already entails, since a shape ruled CORE-and-currently-
nowhere must be added somewhere. The cheapest honest form, in the attachment mechanics the audit
established: **one short scene 0** (six turns — exactly the corpus's own flow length), staging
positions 1–5 on the offer realisation, with **the decline branch attached at position 2** as the
other continuation of a turn that already exists — S1201's recovery arriving while the learner is
in trouble, which is the only time it is worth anything. S1202's diversion is the third
continuation of the same turn and can attach at the same point or travel as the sector pods'
opening variant.

**Composition: C109 and C110 both survive**, re-minted as C1201–C1203 against the canonical id.
C1201 (new): N5 Acquaintance runs inside position 2 in the diversion. C1202 (successor of C109):
N7 Arrangement runs inside position 2 in the decline — the counter-proposal of a different medium
is arrangement mechanics. C1203 (successor of C110): N9 Feasibility runs inside position 2 in the
participant admission. All three mechanical, all attested in §1.

**What it means for CORE that a shape now sits upstream of the whole lattice:** the trunk gains a
wrapper. CORE teaches the contract once, in scene 0; every sector pod then *re-instantiates* it in
the pair's and sector's own terms as its opening — which is Tom's standing ruling for sector
authoring, and functions for sector walks the way the every-optional-walk floor functions for
optional walks, without being part of that floor. **The every-optional-walk floor itself is
untouched**: N13, N14, O7, O8 and the story-with-challenge exemption all stand; this is CORE
business and the floor is about optional walks (flagged per the brief; my reading found no
interaction).

**Outcome note:** S1201 gives CORE's undeclared-outcomes worklist one more natural declaration
point — the decline is a non-delivery-with-a-reason shaped refusal (O1's neighbourhood) landing on
the learner's *project* rather than their order. Recorded as a note for the outcome-declaration
commissioning list, not as a new outcome shape.

## 6. The Bologna acceptance test

Tom's test, verbatim: *"the canonical version instantiates in a pair with none of Wales's history —
Italian in Bologna, say, where the same move exists and carries none of the same weight."*

Setting: `ita_for_eng` — an English-speaking learner, a Bologna **pharmacy**, learner as
**customer** (the role inversion is deliberate: the corpus's declarer is the professional, so a
passing instantiation with the roles swapped proves role-neutrality rather than assuming it).
Known side only — this demonstrates the shape instantiates; it asserts nothing about target-side
cuts (#448's constraint, §verdict).

**The trunk walk, positions 1–5:**

1. *Declare + offer + fallback:* "I'm learning Italian, so I'd like to try this in Italian if
   that's OK — I'm still pretty shaky, so if I get stuck I might have to switch to English."
2. *Grant + reassure:* "Of course — go ahead. We'll manage."
3. *Repair licence:* "And if I say something that makes no sense, just stop me."
4. *Accept:* "Don't worry, I will."
5. *Pivot:* "Right — I'm looking for something for a sore throat."

**The decline branch (S1201), capacity account:** "Sorry — it's a busy morning, English is quicker
if that's alright." → "Of course, no problem — English it is." → "Thanks — another day, happily."
Yield instant, choice ratified, refusal de-personalised by the account itself, pivot follows.

**The decline branch, stakes account, roles swapped again:** the pharmacist — the *native* this
time — invokes it at the dosage: "For the instructions, let me say it in English — you need to get
these right." The domain-scoped decline of 2.0 Welsh flow 2, produced by the other party, mid-walk:
the stakes account survives translation and role-inversion intact, and even re-manufactures the
sector's stakes-raiser exactly where the sector logic demands it.

**The diversion branch (S1202):** "Italian! Where are you from?" → "England — I've been coming to
Bologna for a couple of years." → "Well, your Italian's better than my English. So — the throat."
(Or the learner self-pivots, as in both attestations.)

**Verdict: PASS.** Every position and both attested branches instantiate with none of Wales's
history. **The three named costs — what had to be dropped, and whether it mattered:**

1. **The applause economy** ("Chwarae teg i chi", "Fair play", "It's good to see a doctor
   learning"). In Wales the offer is a recognised civic act and the culture applauds it; in
   Bologna it is a personal preference and earns at most friendly curiosity. Nothing structural
   goes missing — but the *reward signal* the Welsh learner gets at position 2 is genuinely
   thinner, and sector authors for low-stakes pairs should know the grant will be flatter.
2. **The mutual offence-disclaimer tail** ("no offence meant" / "none taken at all"). Declining a
   minority language inside its own institution is politically freighted; declining Italian in
   Italy is not, so the mutual disarming shrinks to a bare warm ratification. The de-personalising
   *function* survives (the account does the work); the ceremonial *form* does not. This is the
   stripping working as intended, not a loss.
3. **The institutional fallback** ("I can call someone who speaks Welsh more confidently") and the
   native's *stake* in the learner's project (the grandson learning in school). Both are Wales:
   language provision as infrastructure, language survival as shared business. In Bologna the
   fallback is just "switch to English" and the native has no stake. The shape survives; the
   surrounding warmth economy thins. That thinning is real and it is exactly the pair-and-culture
   layer leaving the room — which is what the test was for.
