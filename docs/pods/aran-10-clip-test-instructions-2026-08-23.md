# Aran: how to run the 10-clip test

## 1. Where to click

Go to **popty.app/r/human_aran_cym_n** — your own recording link.

That's it, no login. You'll land on a page that says "Hello Aran" with your count of lines recorded so far. There's one big button at the top with the first line you need to read written on it. Tap it.

- It starts the microphone straight away and shows you the line to read.
- Read it at a normal pace. When you stop talking, it moves on to the next line by itself — you don't need to tap anything.
- If you want to re-read the one you just did, tap **Again**. To skip ahead, tap **Next**.
- When you're done — after your 10 — tap **Stop here**. It saves itself, nothing else to do.

You won't see a "mode" choice — there's no menu to pick from on this page, it just starts.

## 2. How to hear each take back

After every single line, right there on the same screen, a bar appears saying **"You just read: [the line]"** with a **▶ Hear stored clip** button next to it.

Tap it. That plays back exactly what got saved on the server — the actual bytes, not just what your phone/browser has sitting in memory. One tap, right after each line, before you move to the next one. That's the whole loop — no menu, no "review" screen to go find.

There's also a **Raw vs processed** button next to it once a take has landed, if you're curious to hear the untouched microphone take against the cleaned-up version.

When you tap **Stop here** at the end, you'll also get a full "Listen back" list of everything you read in that session, in case you want to check any of them again.

## 3. What it looks like when a bad take gets caught

We've built a new check that listens for whether your voice is actually on the take, not just whether it saved. This is the whole point of tonight's test — genuinely try to make it fire once on purpose, so you know what it looks like and aren't caught out by it later.

It can say one of two things, word for word:

> **"That take didn't capture any speech — check the right microphone is selected, then read the line again."**

That's if nothing was picked up at all.

> **"That take came out far longer than the line, so it looks like it caught the room rather than your voice. It hasn't been saved — please read the line again."**

That's if it picked up room noise (or silence) for way longer than the line should take to read — the exact problem from your last session.

Either way: **the take is not saved.** Nothing reaches a learner. The fix is just to read the line again — tap **Again** and go.
