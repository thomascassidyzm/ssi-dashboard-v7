# Three doors onto a live pod slug — guarded, and whether three is all

**2026-09-02. Job: pod doors. Branch `fix/pod-doors-promote-clone-guards-2026-09-02`, merged to `main`.**

Job #91 fixed the known-side readiness hole in `pod-switchover.cjs` and then found the thing that
matters more: **an invariant guarded at one door is not guarded.** This job shuts the other two doors
that were named, and then goes looking for a fourth.

**The headline: three is NOT the whole set. There are ten write paths that put content onto, or empty,
a serving pod slug. Seven are now guarded. Three are not, and one of those three is a button in the
Popty admin UI whose slug parameter DEFAULTS to `pod-0`.**

---

## What landed

### Door 1 — `tools/pods/promote-pod.cjs`

It had never heard of `known_text` or `known_audio_id`. A pod with a perfect target side and a silent
or empty known side promoted onto the live slug with nothing refusing — the identical hole #91 closed
on the switchover the same afternoon.

It now **imports the exported `readinessBlockers()`** from `pod-switchover.cjs` rather than growing a
second copy. Its own structural blockers (mis-slugged ids, id shape, clash on other pods, zero rows)
stay: those are promotion mechanics, not content readiness. The three target-side `--allow-*` escapes
keep working, by zeroing the count they waive. **The known side has no escape** — it is new here and
nobody has a documented waiver for it. Flagged rather than decided: if a promotion ever legitimately
needs to ship a silent known side, that is a flag to add deliberately, not a gap to leave open.

### Door 2 — `tools/pods/clone-pod.cjs`

It knew how to refuse a destination that already held sentence rows, and nothing else. It never asked
whether the destination **slug** is one learners are served. It now refuses a serving destination in
dry run as well as under `--apply`, unless `--serve-now` is passed, and the refusal names the learners:
how many of the course's learners have progress on that pod, counted the way `pod-switchover.cjs`
scopes it. Zero learners still refuses, but says "0 learners currently". A count that cannot be read
refuses and says the count was unavailable — never allows.

Green after both fixes: **47/47** across `promote-pod-readiness`, `clone-pod-serving-destination`,

In both tools the logic lived inside `main()`, which is exactly why nothing tested it — the same
structural lesson as #91. Arg parsing moved into `parseArgs()`, the predicates into pure exported
`promotionBlockers()` / `serviceRefusal()`. Nothing else in either tool changed.

### Two places the guard went further than the brief, because the code said so

1. **`visibility` is not a defence.** The brief defined "serving" as including "visible to learners
   (`visibility`)". Checked against the code: **no learner-path consumer reads
   `listening_pods.visibility` at all.** `servedPod.ts` selects on `pod_type='core'` and
   `slug in ('pod-1','pod-0')`; `api/courses/[code]/bundle.ts` duplicates exactly that literal and
   says so in its own comment ("Retiring a pod by setting visibility='held' did not reach this
   consumer, because nothing here reads visibility"). A grep for `visibility` across `api/` and the
   player composables returns that comment and nothing else. So a `held` destination is refused too.
2. **A destination that does not exist yet is refused too**, because creating the core header row on a
   serving slug IS the moment serving begins.

## Is three the complete set of doors? No. It is ten.

### How the number was counted, so it is checkable

| | this repo (Popty) | learning app |
|---|---|---|
| files mentioning `listening_pods` / `listening_pod_sentences` | 3,613 | 271 |
| in parallel worktrees (`.worktrees/` × 24, `wt-i18n/` × 7, `promote-worktrees/`) | **3,433 (95%)** | **238 (88%)** |
| archived `database/migrations/` | 6 | — |
| **canonical real files** | **174** | **33** |
| of those, containing any write verb on a pod table | **67** | **0** |

Excluded: `node_modules`, `dist`, `.next`, build output, gitignored `scripts/`, this repo's archived
`database/migrations/`, and every parallel worktree copy. **The learning app writes nothing to either
pod table** — it is read-only on pods, so every door is on the Popty side.

Of the 67 Popty writers, most are a different class: in-place content and audio-pointer writers
(splice tools, recasts, relinkers, take renderers) that change rows on whatever pod id you name. They
do not change *which* pod is served, and most carry their own gates. **A door, in the sense this
thread means, creates, renames, empties or moves a pod on a slug the player serves.** Ten do that.

### The seven that are guarded

| # | file | what makes it a door | guard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `tools/pods/pod-switchover.cjs` | promotes a staged pod onto the live slug, migrating progress | readiness + cast + split-audio + inflight fold (#91) |
| 2 | `tools/pods/promote-pod.cjs` | renames a source pod onto the live slug | structural + shared `readinessBlockers()` — **landed today** |
| 3 | `tools/pods/clone-pod.cjs` | inserts a pod header + rows at `--to=<slug>` | refuses a serving destination without `--serve-now` — **landed today** |
| 4 | `tools/pods/archive-pod.cjs` | renames a pod OFF the live slug | refuses if any `learner_pod_state` row references it: "this is a switchover, not an archive" |
| 5 | `tools/pods/delete-blank-pod-sentence.cjs` | deletes a sentence row | literal staging-slug allowlist plus a `visibility='held'` check. Safe by construction via the allowlist; note its visibility check is weaker than it believes, for the reason above |
| 6 | `tools/pods/align-pod0-to-canonical.cjs` | the destructive rewrite clone-pod exists to protect from | refuses to rewrite the live `pod-0` of a `released` or `beta` course, auto-clones to `pod-0-unrecorded`; `--force` is the visible escape |
| 7 | `tools/pods/rehearse-switchover.cjs` | creates and deletes pods and progress wholesale | scoped to `zzz_` scratch courses; the switchover refuses `--rehearsal` on anything else |

`tools/pods/reslug-pod-rows.cjs` rewrites row ids on whatever `--pod=` you give it and has no serving
check, but on a correctly-slugged `pod-0` it is a no-op by construction — it only ever rewrites the
slug segment to match the pod's own slug. Listed here for completeness, not as an open door.

### The three that are NOT guarded — this is the finding

**8. `tools/pod-sync.cjs`** — `--slug=pod-0` is in its own usage examples.
> `const { error: delErr } = await db().from('listening_pod_sentences').delete().eq('pod_id', podId)` (line 846)

Wholesale replace semantics: DELETE every sentence row of the named pod, then INSERT the markdown's.
No serving-slug check, no learner-progress migration, no make-before-break. Re-inserted rows carry no
audio ids — its own header says audio links "must be re-established by the Phase 8 pod-audio step".
Run it on a live course's `pod-0` with a markdown that is one line different and you empty and refill
a served pod under learners, orphaning their progress rows. **Needs a guard**, and it is the same
guard `clone-pod` just got.

**9. `services/pod-dialogue-generator.cjs`** — creates the pod header on any slug you pass.
> `if (!existing) row.visibility = 'held'` … `await supabase.from('listening_pods').upsert(row, { onConflict: 'id' })` (line 415)

Its comment reasons carefully that "nothing should become learner-reachable merely by being CREATED",
and enforces that with `visibility = 'held'`. **That reasoning is void**: no learner consumer reads
`visibility`. A core header row on `pod-0` or `pod-1` is served the instant it exists. And
`deleteAllSentences(podId)` (line 436) wipes every sentence of that pod on a force/full regen.
**Needs a guard** — and its held-on-creation comment needs correcting, because it is currently telling
the next reader something the app does not do.

**10. `POST /api/admin/pods/generate`** in `services/production-api.cjs` (line 4604) — the HTTP mouth
of #9, and a button in the Popty pods page.
> `const slug = String(req.body?.slug || 'pod-0').trim()`

**The slug defaults to `pod-0`** — a serving slug for ~68 courses — and the body may carry
`force: true` and `mode: 'full'`, which is the wipe-and-re-flex path. This is the shortest route from
a human hand to an emptied live pod in the whole estate, and it is one click behind an admin session.
**Needs a guard**, and it is the highest-value of the three because it needs no command line.

`services/pod-bulk-migrate.cjs` drives #9 across every `pod-0` course. It is a multiplier on that
door, not an independent one — guard #9 and #10 and it inherits the guard.

**Reported, not fixed.** Guarding a fourth, fifth and sixth door is a fresh decision for Tom, and the
honest finding is worth more than an unbriefed patch on a service route.

## The learner-progress guard on `promote-pod.cjs` — what it would cost

Not built, deliberately, per the commission. Here is the estimate.

**What it would have to do.** `promote-pod.cjs` renames pods by slug, and `learner_pod_state.sentence_id`
embeds the slug. A promotion therefore silently re-points every learner's progress at whatever sentence
now occupies that id — the mis-credit `pod-switchover.cjs` carries ~170 lines of machinery to prevent
(and which cost a real `nld_for_eng` learner 14 rows around the 2026-08-24 flip). A correct guard
would: snapshot `learner_pod_state` before the transaction; plan the old-canon → new-canon mapping;
apply carry/keep/merge/drop inside the same commit as the renames; catch rows written while the plan
was being made; and post-check that no state row points at a sentence that no longer exists.

**How much is reusable.** More than you would guess. `planMigration()` is already a separate, exported,
tested module (`tools/pods/pod-state-migrate.cjs`), and `planInflightFold()` is already exported from
`pod-switchover.cjs` with its own test file. What is NOT reusable is the ~170 lines of apply-and-verify
that sit inside the switchover's `main()`: the snapshot, the straggler re-read, the delete/insert loop
and the orphan post-check. Rewriting those in `promote-pod.cjs` would be a second implementation of the
rule this whole thread says not to write — the exact mistake this job avoided on the readiness gate.

**Recommendation, plainly: do not build it. Make `promote-pod.cjs` refuse instead.** Have it run the
same `learner_pod_state` count `archive-pod.cjs` already runs, and refuse with "this is a switchover,
not a promotion — use `pod-switchover.cjs`" when any learner has progress on the destination. That is
about ten lines, it is testable as a pure predicate exactly like the two guards landed today, and it
leaves one implementation of the fold in one place. If the two tools genuinely need to merge later,
the honest version of that is extracting the switchover's apply-fold into `pod-state-migrate.cjs`
alongside `planMigration` and having both tools call it — a day's careful work with a rehearsal on a
`zzz_` course, not an afternoon.

Sizing: the refusal, ~1 hour including its test. The full fold, extracted and shared, ~1 day plus a
rehearsal run. The difference is 8× and the refusal is strictly safer, because a tool that refuses
cannot mis-migrate.
