Aran's new pod-0 canonical is 231 sentences in 22 scenes. The Welsh recording
queue was still serving the old 142. This is the line-by-line comparison of the two,
per Welsh course, taken from the database rather than from any document.

The short version: **the great majority of the old English survives word for word,
so the Welsh already recorded against it stays valid.** The work is almost all new
material, not rework.
