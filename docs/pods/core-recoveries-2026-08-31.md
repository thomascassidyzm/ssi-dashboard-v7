# The five withheld recoveries — attached to their CORE scenes

*2026-08-31. The compulsory-set audit found that six of CORE's fifteen survivability edges have
no attested recovery anywhere in the estate — CORE teaches the learner to ASK, and asking
summons the answers it never taught anyone to survive. Five of the six attach to scenes that
already exist (A1001–A1006, `services/shared/metagraph/proposed/core-recovery-attachments-2026-08-31.json`);
the sixth, M3's mid-story challenge, cannot attach without giving CORE a story turn it does not
have, and lives on the every-optional-walk floor instead. This is the authoring of the five.
Same act as CORE scene 0: attached, not appended — CORE's order untouched, every recovery
arriving inside the encounter that made the trouble.*

**The five in one line each:** a hedge acted on and corrected by a road sign, not a mood; an
"I don't know" that names where the answer lives; a counterbid settled by formula because the
learner has a real constraint; a modest premise audited into evidence; and a disagreement set
down warm, unresolved, unruptured.

## The five walks, read aloud first

### W1301 — the hedge acted on, and the sign that corrects it *(S2, scene 2)*

The bus into town, the morning of the day of greetings. The seat business has already run.
S2 is the only corpus-attested edge with no recovery anywhere: five hedges in the canon, not
one ever taken up. This is the first.

> **Sarah:** Can you tell me how far it is into town?
>
> **Passenger:** It's not very far. Maybe three or four miles.
>
> **Sarah:** Three or four miles — I could walk that. I might get off at the next stop, then,
> and walk the rest. It's a nice morning for it.
>
> **Passenger:** Oh — hold on. There's the sign: six miles to town. It's further than I
> thought. I'm sorry.
>
> **Sarah:** Don't be — you said maybe, and maybe was right. Six is too far for me. I'll stay
> in this seat and ride the whole way.
>
> **Passenger:** Do that. It's the last stop, anyway — you can't miss it.

### W1302 — "I don't know", held with status: the third arm at the café *(M1, scene 3)*

The café, mid-order: the coffee is already being made. The canon's one stored branch forks the
crisps question into yes and no; this is the answer that is neither — and the first turn in
CORE where a responder fails to know anything.

> **Sarah:** Do you have crisps, or nuts, or anything?
>
> **Barista:** I honestly don't know — today's delivery is still in its boxes. There might be
> crisps in there; there might not.
>
> **Sarah:** Would you have a look? No rush — I'm waiting on my coffee anyway.
>
> **Barista:** Right — you're in luck. Crisps, two kinds. No nuts, though.
>
> **Sarah:** Crisps will do nicely — salted, please.
>
> **Barista:** Here's your coffee — and your crisps.

### W1303 — the counterbid over when, settled by formula *(M5, scene 4)*

The street at seven in the evening — the corpus's only two-sided negotiation, which had
already walked the haggle's first two positions without knowing it: "tomorrow" is a number on
the table, "Saturday" is its counterbid. This adds the counter-counter and the settlement.
The price-haggle is explicitly NOT attached.

> **Sarah:** Hello! I'm sorry but I can't talk at the moment. I need to go home now. Can we
> talk tomorrow?
>
> **Friend:** No, I'm sorry, I'm busy tomorrow. But let's talk on Saturday. See you then.
>
> **Sarah:** Saturday's good — but only the morning. I'm away all afternoon.
>
> **Friend:** Saturday morning, then. Ten o'clock, at the café?
>
> **Sarah:** Ten at the café — perfect. See you Saturday!
>
> **Friend:** Go on home, then — Saturday at ten!

### W1304 — the premise audited, and the audit received as a gift *(M2, scene 22)*

The first conversation. The learner's opening carries a claim — "I haven't been learning for
very long" — and in the canon it sails past unexamined, straight into a compliment. Here it
gets a friendly audit, and the ground supplied argues against the learner's own self-downgrade.

> **Learner:** Would you mind if I tried to practise speaking your language with you? I
> haven't been learning for very long, and I still feel a little nervous about speaking with
> other people.
>
> **Friend:** Of course, no problem. Not very long — how long is that, exactly?
>
> **Learner:** About six months. A little every day — usually just ten minutes. It doesn't
> feel like very much.
>
> **Friend:** Six months, every single day? That's more than most people ever do. I can
> understand you easily — and now I know why.
>
> **Learner:** Thank you. Maybe it does add up after all. I still feel nervous — but a little
> less than when we started talking.

### W1305 — the disagreement parked, warm and unresolved *(M4, scene 22)*

The same conversation, at its only disagreement. The canon runs the contradiction round twice
and then the learner changes the subject, so no disagreement is ever seen to END anywhere in
CORE. Here it ends the way the Method Pod attests: seen, named, and set down.

> **Learner:** It's just a little frustrating when I can't think quickly enough to express
> myself properly. But I know that I need to keep practising if I want to speak more
> confidently.
>
> **Friend:** You should be confident already. I think you're doing much better than you
> realise. I feel comfortable speaking with you, and I'm not talking very slowly.
>
> **Learner:** That's kind — but I don't think we're going to agree about this. You hear my
> sentences; I hear all my gaps.
>
> **Friend:** Then let's leave it there — you hear the gaps, I hear the sentences, and we
> don't have to settle it now. Keep talking to me, and one of us will be proved right.
>
> **Learner:** It's a deal. Either way, I need the practice — so let's keep talking.

## What each world is doing

**W1301 — the correction is a road sign, never a mood.** The scene 0 lesson applied: a hedge
turning out wrong needs a reason the learner can see, and a distance sign on the way into town
is exactly where a hedged estimate meets ground truth mid-journey. The hedge propagates
honestly into the plan — "I *might* get off", because a hedged answer breeds a hedged plan —
and the recovery is one line: "you said maybe, and maybe was right." Nobody promised, so
nobody is owed the apology the passenger offers. The last turn hands over certainty exactly
where certainty exists — the distance was an estimate, the last stop is a fact — and that
contrast is the quietest lesson in the walk. Structurally the walk runs N4's bound
read-back/confirm pair twice, once around the wrong number and once around the right one:
acting on an answer out loud IS a read-back, and it is what gives the world its chance to object.

**W1302 — the world explains why a barista can fail to know their own stock.** Today's
delivery is still in its boxes. The not-knowing is held with status — the barista names where
the answer lives — and the status is a door, not a dead end: it licenses Sarah's re-route from
the person to the means, which is O2's recovery ("nobody knows → re-route the ask") staged
transactionally, in a scene the learner meets in week one. The world also makes the request
cheap to grant: her coffee is still being made, so the minute the boxes cost was already
spoken for. The detour costs one turn each way and the transaction resumes as if the loop had
never happened — because to the transaction, it hadn't.

**W1303 — the clock is Sarah's own opening line.** "I need to go home now" is not scenery; a
leisurely negotiation would contradict the world it stands in, so the haggle runs in four
brisk turns and the friend's last line sends her home — the constraint she declared is
honoured by the person she declared it to. Her counter-counter earns its turn by carrying a
real constraint ("I'm away all afternoon"): reopening a closed "see you then" on a mere
preference would fail the read-aloud test. The settlement formula is delivered whole — take
the overlap, seal it with "then", add the precision that makes an agreement an arrangement —
and Sarah accepts by reading the precision back, the motif the canon reserves for numbers
that matter. O9's recovery is "produce a third position, or yield"; her third position is
Saturday morning.

**W1304 — the audit produces the evidence the compliment lacked.** The friend's "how long is
that, exactly?" doesn't doubt the learner — it goes looking for the ground the learner was
too modest to stand on, and hands it back. The canon's own compliment ("I can understand you
easily", g222's line, kept on purpose) turns from kindness into evidence once the numbers are
on the table. The learner's close is concede-and-hold, scene 22's signature move: the
arithmetic is accepted, the nervousness is held anyway — feelings don't yield to arithmetic
and nobody here pretends they do. The grant of permission is not withheld to make room for
the branch: the audit rides on top of the canon's own "of course".

**W1305 — the park costs nothing to sign, and that is why it lands softly.** The learner's
hold locates the disagreement in vantage, not in error — "you hear my sentences; I hear all my
gaps" — which is what lets it be held without offence. The friend's park restates BOTH
positions in the learner's own frame (borrowing the other side's words is what makes a park
warm rather than dismissive) and defers resolution to evidence neither of them has yet. And
the parked question is the one disagreement in the world whose two branches point at the same
next move: whichever of them is right, the remedy is more talking, which is what the scene is
for. An unresolved disagreement is not a rupture — the canon's g229 follows the last line
without a seam.

## The rulings, honoured

- **Attached, not appended.** Every walk names the pod-0 walk and step it hangs off
  (`attaches_to`), restages that turn as a quotation, and recovers inside the same encounter.
  `walks/pod-0.json` is untouched; CORE's order is unchanged.
- **Not specified edge-by-edge.** Each walk was written as a scene, and the recovery moves
  arrived as dialogue: the re-route, the formula, the concede-and-hold, the bilateral park are
  turns people would say, not realised specifications.
- **The world motivates every move**: a road sign, a delivery in boxes, a queue-less coffee
  wait, an afternoon away, a claim too modest for its own evidence, a parked question whose
  both answers prescribe the same remedy. No decline, no correction, no hold is a mood.
- **Canonical layer only.** English known side, deictic language naming (scene 22's
  `[target language]` bracket rendered as "your language" per the scene 0 convention), no
  pair, no course code. Sector-free and culture-free: a bus, a café, a street, a first
  conversation — each instantiates in a pair carrying none of Wales's history. Miles are the
  canon's own unit (g9).
- **M3 left alone**, on the every-optional-walk floor where job #443 put it.
