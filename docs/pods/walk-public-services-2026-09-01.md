## 1. The register, and why this walk is shaped the way it is

Public services is the register of **entitlement and proof**. The counterpart cannot simply
decide to help — they are bound by rules they did not write — and the learner is usually trying
to establish that they qualify for something. It is the one register in the set where
**non-delivery-with-reason — U1, the floor of the outcome overlay — is the ORDINARY case**, and
the walk is built around that fact rather than in spite of it: the story arc ends on a refusal
that survives its own appeal, because the register's one irreplaceable lesson is the no that
cannot be argued with — and the future that survives it. A walk that ended on a won appeal
would be teaching a consolation the world will not honour.

**Culture-free and nation-free, by construction.** No Ireland, no Wales, no Gaeilge, no agency
names, no currency units, no dialect warmth (the health corpus's "bach" and "love" have no
analogue here — the warmth is structural instead). The furniture is furniture found everywhere:
a public office with a front counter and a deciding desk, a register of payments, an allowance,
an appeals office, a proof-of-address. The acceptance test — that this instantiates in a pair
carrying none of Ireland's or Wales's particulars — passes by inspection: a national instance
is a mask laid over this set, never inside it.

**Scene 0 referenced, not re-authored.** The medium contract (N1201) sits at the front
unconditionally — both ratified realisations (`core-scene-0.json` W1201–W1204,
`core-scene-0-notice.json` W1401) are referenced in the file header. This walk begins at
scene 1, in the granted medium, and stays there. Nothing proposes anyone's language anywhere;
the deixis rule has no surface to fail on.

## 2. The world

One town, one public office, one continuing story across four visits. **Ana Marsh** (Resident)
is newly arrived with her family — which is what makes every encounter necessary rather than
staged. Two threads:

- **Registration** (scenes 1–7): wrong desk → identity → intake → suspended for one missing
  paper → the queue held → finished on the spot → a certificate bought. This thread
  **completes** — the machine works — which is what earns the second thread its weight.
- **The allowance** (scenes 8–14): the verdict (nine months short of five qualifying years) →
  the story the record can't see (a season at the harbour, paid cash, never recorded) → the
  refusal → the appeal lodged → the wait bounded → the complaint → **the second no, survived.**

The stake is engineered so the record is *right*: the harbour year really happened and really
was never paid in, no paper can ever be produced, the refusal stands, and the appeal honestly
fails — while the months now being paid in complete the five years next summer. Every no in the
walk is a rule with a reason standing in the room; every decline is world-motivated, never a
mood.
