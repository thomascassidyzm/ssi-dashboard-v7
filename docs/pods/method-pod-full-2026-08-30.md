# The Method Pod in Italian — the full script

> **PRIVATE. Unlisted, Italian only, audience of two — Tom and Aran.** Nothing here is listed, scaled, rendered or written to production. No row is written, no pod is edited, no audio is queued or rendered, no course is touched. This is one document holding the whole canonical Italian Method Pod script, end to end, so it can be read by ear from start to finish and, next, loaded by the configs/script tool as its first script. The three earlier documents — `method-pod-first-specimens-2026-08-29.md` (scenes 1–4), `method-pod-round-two-2026-08-29.md` (scenes 5–16), `method-pod-re-cut-2026-08-30.md` (the container of all sixteen) — stay in place as the working record; nothing in them is rewritten here.

**What this document is.** Forty-three scenes. The sixteen ratified scenes, copied in their re-cut form with their per-scene detail restated so the file is self-contained; and **twenty-seven new scenes, 17–43**, selected by coverage of the shape graph (`shape-graph-2026-08-30.md`) and by the round-two deficit list, written on the re-cut grammar. Every scene carries the same six things: **Owns**, **Newly admits** (one response position), **Shape witnessed** (the general node, written so a pod about cooking or grief could walk it), **Surface forms admitted** (availability only — heard is not owned, never a prompt target), the near-neighbour selection points (Italian against its Spanish shadow), and one sentence tying the container to a measured number. English column, Italian beside it. Scenes are walks; delivery order stays derived and is not authored here.

**Mints are labelled.** Where a scene rests on something the corpus does not attest, the word **MINT** appears in its header and the sentence saying what is invented sits in the scene. Nothing minted is dressed as attestation. §7 lists every mint in one table.

**Corpus references** are `part:line` into `docs/corpus/talk-bollocks/part-{1,2,4}.md`. Part 3 is lost; the file called part 5 is Part 4's audio. Said once.

## 2. The grammar every new scene is written on

The re-cut's seven rules (`method-pod-re-cut-2026-08-30.md` §5), restated once so this file stands alone. Six-turn symmetry is retired (Tom, 2026-08-29: *"SIX TURNS IS TOO FORMULAIC AS A STRUCTURE… ESPECIALLY AS THE TURNS ARE SYMMETRIC"*).

- **G1** Scene length is the shape's attested closing length. The corpus's attested closes run 2, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3, 3, 4, 5, 5, 7, 8 turns; delivered range 3–8.
- **G2** Exactly one floor-hold per scene, 3–4× the scene's own median (the corpus runs 4–34×; the undershoot is deliberate — a 4× hold at scene scale is already ~40 words of Italian).
- **G3** **The hold is always a HEARD turn.** Never the owned exchange, never the newly-admitted position. That is what keeps a 40-word turn affordable: everything the learner is asked to *produce* stays short.
- **G4** At least one minimal turn of two to four words.
- **G5** At least a third of scenes carry an `A → token → A` sandwich — the same speaker resumes after the partner's token.
- **G6** Inside a scene one speaker owns 65–75% of the words; the balance evens out across the set, never within a scene.
- **G7** Scene CV ≥ 0.6, aiming 0.8–1.0 — except where the shape's own attested container is fast and flat (the haggle, CV 0.65; the arrangement).

Two standing rules from the ratified sets: **heard ≠ owned** (surface forms are availability for phrase baskets only, never prompt targets), and **one owned exchange + one newly admitted response position per scene**. Methodology terms travel as loanwords — LEGO, play, edge where it is a house term — and are not re-minted per language.

### Scene 1 — *Il palazzo in mezzo al campo* — joint construction

**Owns:** backchannel ratification (live canon). **Newly admits:** joint construction — completing the partner's clause and having it ratified. **Attested:** 1:342–343, 1:220–222, 2:237–240; closing length 2–3 turns, the completion always 1–4 words. Subject: items and edges.

**Shape witnessed:** JOINT CONSTRUCTION — one speaker leaves a clause open, the other closes it in a handful of words, the first ratifies, and the closer is allowed one extension. Any subject two people finish for each other can walk it.

**Surface forms admitted:** *i collegamenti*, *le strade*, *i palazzi*, *in mezzo a un campo*, *poverini*. Availability only.

**Near-neighbour points:** *palazzo* ≠ sp. *palacio* (building, not palace — a false friend on the key noun); *strada* has no live Spanish neighbour (pull toward *calle* → nothing); locative *ci* ("non **ci** abita nessuno") — Spanish drops it, selection by absence; *quindi* against *entonces/así que* — no cognate anchor.

| | English | Italiano |
|---|---|---|
| TOM | It's not that you know the words. You know what's between the words. The edges. Like a city — you don't know a city because you know the buildings; you know it because you know the streets. So a new word without streets… | Non è che conosci le parole. Conosci quello che c'è tra le parole. I collegamenti. Come una città — non conosci una città perché conosci i palazzi; la conosci perché conosci le strade. Quindi una parola nuova senza strade… |
| ARAN | …is a building in the middle of a field. | …è un palazzo in mezzo a un campo. |
| TOM | Exactly that. | Esatto, proprio così. |
| ARAN | Nobody lives there. And schools keep building them. Poor things. | Non ci abita nessuno. E le scuole continuano a costruirli. Poverini. |

**Why this container:** every attested completion in the corpus is at or below 0.1× its exchange median; the whole scene is the run-up to one small clause and its two-word ratification (42/9/2/10 words, max/median 4.4×).

### Scene 2 — *Il colpo basso* — anecdote-with-flag, challenged

**Owns:** anecdote-as-evidence with a status flag (1:344–349, the crocodile). **Newly admits:** the sceptical challenge landing mid-story and being survived — then the anecdote turned on the listener. **Attested:** 1:344–349; closes in 3 turns, the flag inside the hold's first sentence. Subject: effort implicated in memory.

**Shape witnessed:** THE FLAGGED ANECDOTE, CHALLENGED — a story is entered with its evidential status declared, the partner attacks the sample size, and the teller neither defends nor withdraws but turns the story's own mechanism on the challenger.

**Surface forms admitted:** *dimostrare*, *accettala lo stesso*, *una studentessa*, *le sono costate fatica*, *sparite*, *costruire una chiesa su*, *colpo basso*. Availability only.

**Near-neighbour points:** *fatica* ≠ sp. *fatiga* (effort, not tiredness — the false friend carrying the invariant itself); *niente* vs *nada* (no anchor); *ricordarsi* — cognate verb, non-matching reflexive pattern ("te la ricorderai" vs "la recordarás"); *sparite* vs *desaparecidas* (selection by absence).

### Scene 4 — *Dov'eravamo?* — reformulation, flagged digression and return

**Shape witnessed:** THE FLAGGED DIGRESSION — a speaker announces a detour and its length, the partner licenses it with a clock, the detour runs and returns on its own marker, and the partner restores the thread and claims the detour as evidence.

**Surface forms admitted:** *nel momento in cui*, *diventa un gioco*, *aspetta*, *promesso*, *il tempo corre*, *foglia per foglia*, *comunque*, *dov'eravamo*, *dare ragione*. Availability only.

### Scene 5 — *Non lo so* — **ZERO-ADMISSION** — not-knowing held with status

**Owns:** everything — verified word by word against the live canon (idea, good, bad, know, right, now, start, think). **Newly admits:** the shape only — the honest "I don't know" held with status, plus the question-as-handover. **Attested:** 1:54, 4:70–77; in the corpus the whole move is one 110-word turn, unreachable at Layer 0, so the ping-pong stays and the hold is built by repeating words the scene already owns.

**Shape witnessed:** NOT-KNOWING AS FLOOR — a direct question is met with a flat "I don't know" that loses no status, the not-knowing is confirmed and shared, and the shared blank is converted into licence to think together, closed by handing the first move to the partner.

**Surface forms admitted:** none — zero-admission.

**Near-neighbour points:** *Non lo so* one letter off *no lo sé* — an anchor that bites: Italian keeps the clitic always, Spanish drops it ("no sé"); *sai/sabes* — cognate root, non-matching ending; *hai ragione* — avere where Spanish reaches for *tener*.

### Scene 7 — *Un tiro di dadi* — the flagged guess

**Owns:** question-and-answer floor; the reformulation staple. **Newly admits:** the epistemic status flag — rating your own answer before giving it. **Attested:** 2:294–296, 2:349; 3 turns, flag + guess + reason in one breath.

**Shape witnessed:** THE FLAGGED GUESS — before answering, the speaker prices their own authority down, the partner explicitly buys the guess anyway ("But?"), and the speculation is then given at full strength wrapped in provisional markers. The flag is what licenses the confidence.

**Surface forms admitted:** *fuori allenamento*, *un tiro di dadi*, *un morso*, *l'orario*, *sospetto che*, *alla settimana*. Availability only.

**Near-neighbour points:** *è da tanto tempo che non…* — Italian builds the since-construction on essere where Spanish builds on *hacer*; *però* against *pero* — anchor with a stress-and-vowel trap; *alla settimana* against *a la semana* — near-identical, ground-holding after two traps.
