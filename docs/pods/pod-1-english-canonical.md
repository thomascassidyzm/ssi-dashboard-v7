## 1. Hello Stranger — *I haven't seen you for ages*

*Difficulty: beginner · 15 sentences*

| # | Speaker | English |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Grace | Hello stranger! I haven't seen you for ages. |
| 2 | Paul | Grace! I was just thinking about you the other day. |
| 3 | Grace | Were you? All good things, I hope. |
| 4 | Paul | Of course. How are you, anyway? How's the new place? |
| 5 | Grace | Really good, thanks. We're nearly straight… just a few boxes left in the spare room. |
| 6 | Paul | That was quick. And how did it go on Friday, in the end? The interview. |
| 7 | Grace | I got it! I start in two weeks. |
| 8 | Paul | No! That's brilliant. We should celebrate properly. |
| 9 | Grace | Definitely. Are you still at the same number? |
| 10 | Paul | I am. Text me — we'll sort something out this week. |
| 11 | Grace | I will. Oh — before I forget. You know Sara's back? |
| 12 | Paul | Sorry — who's back? |
| 13 | Grace | Sara. From the old office. She's moved back from Leeds. |
| 14 | Paul | Sara! Well then. It'll have to be a proper night out, won't it? |
| 15 | Grace | Deal. It's so good to see you, Paul. |

## 2. And What About You? — *How was your week, anyway?*

*Difficulty: beginner · 14 sentences*

| # | Speaker | English |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Amy | How was your week, anyway? |
| 2 | Joe | Busy, but good. We finally finished the kitchen… so we can stop eating in the front room. |
| 3 | Amy | At last! You've been saying that since Easter. |
| 4 | Joe | I know. And what about you? How did the interview go? |
| 5 | Amy | Better than I expected, actually. I'll hear next week. |
| 6 | Joe | That's great news. What happens if they say yes — would you take it? |
| 7 | Amy | Honestly… I don't know yet. Ask me next week. |
| 8 | Joe | I will, don't worry. Are you still running, by the way? |
| 9 | Amy | Most mornings, yes. It keeps me sane. And you? How's the five-a-side going? |
| 10 | Joe | Don't ask. I did my ankle in two weeks ago… I've been watching from the bench ever since. |
| 11 | Amy | Oh no. Is it getting any better? |
| 12 | Joe | Slowly. Enough about me, though — how's your mum doing? |
| 13 | Amy | Much better, thanks for asking. She's back in the garden already. |
| 14 | Joe | That's really good to hear. Say hello from me, won't you? |
