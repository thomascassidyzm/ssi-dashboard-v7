## 1. Hello Stranger — *I haven't seen you for ages*

*Difficulty: beginner · 15 sentences*

| # | Speaker | English |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Grace | Hello stranger! I haven't seen you for ages. |
| 2 | Paul | Grace! I was just thinking about you the other day. |
| 3 | Grace | Were you? All good things, I hope. |
| 4 | Paul | Of course. How are you, anyway? How's the new place? |
| 5 | Grace | Really good, thanks. We're nearly straight… just a few boxes left in the spare room. |
| 6 | Paul | That was quick. And how did it go on Friday, in the end? The interview. |
| 7 | Grace | I got it! I start in two weeks. |
| 8 | Paul | No! That's brilliant. We should celebrate properly. |
| 9 | Grace | Definitely. Are you still at the same number? |
| 10 | Paul | I am. Text me — we'll sort something out this week. |
| 11 | Grace | I will. Oh — before I forget. You know Sara's back? |
| 12 | Paul | Sorry — who's back? |
| 13 | Grace | Sara. From the old office. She's moved back from Leeds. |
| 14 | Paul | Sara! Well then. It'll have to be a proper night out, won't it? |
| 15 | Grace | Deal. It's so good to see you, Paul. |
