# CORE scene 0 — the medium contract, at the counter and the desk

*2026-08-31, second authoring. The first (`feat/core-scene-0-2026-08-31`) was rejected for the
world it was staged in — two neighbours arranging a parcel cannot motivate a decline, so the
decline read as a mood and the scene read as nonsense. The shape was ratified at `cd9d93e2f`
and is unchanged here: N1201 Medium contract, S1201, S1202, C1201–C1202, sited as the CORE
prologue before the first transaction. What changed is the world. Per Tom's ruling, scene 0 now
lives inside two existing pod-0 functional scenarios — the café order (W1) and the hotel
check-in (W10) — casts inherited, none invented.*

**The repair in one sentence: the branch is the state of the counter.** Same ask every time; an
empty counter grants it and says why, a lunchtime queue declines it for capacity, a misheard
booking would cost money so the desk fences off dates and numbers, and a quiet chatty moment
diverts it into biography. Every continuation is motivated by something the learner can see.

The learner says "your language" / "mine"; the native says "mine" / "yours". Deictic, so the
canonical known text is pair-invariant with no template slot.

The hotel check-in. A booking is dates and numbers with money on them — the one thing here

> **Receptionist:** Jones… a double, three nights, Friday to Monday — all correct. And the rest

## What each world is doing

**The trunk grant has a reason.** "There's nobody behind you" is the same arithmetic as the
capacity decline, run the other way: the weaker medium costs time, and the grant is the barista
seeing that the time exists to be given. In the first authoring the grant was flat because the
neighbour's world offered no reason to give one.

**The capacity decline is the attested account, verbatim in function.** The source's "I'm not
feeling up to it today" becomes a server who is rushed, in effect "sorry — let's do this in
English": refusal, account standing visibly in the room, counter-proposal of the fluent medium,
and a scope ("any other day", "quiet after three") that is the licence to re-offer — taken up
two turns later. Under a queue the recovery compresses, and the compression *is* the
ratification: the fastest way to agree the rush is real is to stop discussing it and order.

**The stakes decline names its stakes in money.** "Anything with dates and numbers" needed a
domain where a miss costs something; a booking misheard is a bill for the wrong nights. The
scope bites at the pivot — the booking itself runs in the fallback, honouring a contract agreed
thirty seconds earlier — and the read-back (W10's own motif) closes the loop before the target
medium is handed straight back for everything outside the fence. A domain-scoped decline is the
offer accepted with a boundary drawn; the learner still gets the encounter.

**The diversion is bought by the same quiet that bought the grant.** Curiosity needs time, and
the world supplies it. The recovery is one natural step because the barista's own joke — "all I
can remember is how to order a coffee" — sets the business down within arm's reach: the
self-pivot picks the joke up rather than lurching away from the chat. The grant is never spoken
anywhere in the walk; acceptance happens by conduct, and the repair licence that the trunk's
positions 3–4 would have carried is salvaged on the way through.

## What was kept from the first authoring, in one line each

- **Tom's ruling on position 1**: the notice is the primitive (declaration only, no ask, no
  branch); the offer is the notice plus a request for a grant, mintable only where a shared
  fallback exists — the survivability branch belongs to the request, so the offer branches and
  the notice does not. Scene 0 stages the offer for exactly that reason.
- **Role-neutrality**: here the learner is the customer because that is who the host scenarios
  cast; in health the learner is the professional; no position cares.
- **The decline attaches at position 2** — every branch arrives at turn 2 of the contract and
  recovers inside the same encounter, never in a later scene.
- **S1202's mechanics**: grant never spoken, acceptance by conduct, self-initiated pivot.
- **Deictic language naming**, so the canonical layer stays pair-invariant.
