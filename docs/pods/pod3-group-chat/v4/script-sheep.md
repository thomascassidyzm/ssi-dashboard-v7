# Do sheep know your face? (script)

*How to read it:* a **/** marks where the next speaker starts talking over the line. A line ending in **—** is cut off there. Short back-channels like *(Dean: mm)* sit in brackets where they land. *[move F16 …]* is a stage direction naming the layer-3 group-contribution move that turn performs (metagraph id); it is never spoken. [Watch it as a timed replay](https://watson-1.tail4968cb.ts.net/evidence/pod3-group-chat/sheep.html).

*Hugh has just said his ewes know him by his face from across a field and ignore Will, and Jess has said that's sentimental nonsense. Femi says he half-remembers reading that sheep can recognise faces, maybe even from photos, but can't remember how many or where he read it.*

**JESS** Photos? Of sheep? Who's holding the camera, Femi?

**FEMI** Researchers. Cambridge, I think? Maybe.

**HUGH** *[move F302 It proves nothing — accept it anyway]* I've no study, Femi, just forty-odd years at the gate. But they know my face. Will walks /up, nothing—

**SUNNY** Is it your face though, or your walk? I'd know my son's slouch across a car park before I saw his face.

**HUGH** *[move F2 Do it and throw something in]* Could be. Will walks like his mother.

**FEMI** So swap coats with Will.

**DANNY** Wasn't it famous faces? I've a nagging feeling one of them /was Obama—

**RHI** Welsh Mountain ewes, they were, Danny. And Fiona Bruce. Course they beat you, they're from Merthyr way.

**DANNY** And Obama. I did say Obama.

**HUGH** *[move F305 Absorb and build]* Fine, say it's the walk, Sunny. They've still learnt mine and not Will's, and that's cleverer, not less.

**JESS** Hang on, hang on. Who feeds /them, Hugh?—

**HUGH** Will feeds them, Jess. Has done since my hip went, three winters now. And they still come to me.

**RHI** For what, though? You carry Polos?

**SUNNY** Or Will's the one with the needle and the foot shears. My two used to vanish whenever I picked /up the thermometer.

**JESS** *[move F306 Exactly where I'm about to go]* Yes, that's where I was going with who feeds them. They know Will /fine, Hugh—

**HUGH** Will carries the bucket, Jess.

**DANNY** So they snub the waiter.

**FEMI** Wait, when you say come to you, Hugh, do they walk over, or just lift their heads? Different claims.

**HUGH** Walk /over—

**JESS** *[move F9 Let me check I’ve got it]* Right, so, so you're saying the bucket's Will's, so it can't be food, and they still walk over for you?

**HUGH** That's it, Jess. Bucket's his, and they still come to me. I've watched Will rattling it at the top gate while half of them stood by me at the bottom.

**RHI** Which half, though? The old girls you lambed yourself before the hip, or the young ones Will brought on?

**HUGH** Old girls, mostly, Rhi. Though there's one of Will's tegs follows me about like /a collie—

**SUNNY** Was she bottle-fed, that one?

**DANNY** *[move F304 Comment on the medium]* Long pause from Hugh. That's a yes.

**HUGH** She was. Old girls weren't, though.

**FEMI** So it's history, /then—

**RHI** Isn't that how anyone knows a face?

**JESS** Okay, fine, I'll take back sentimental. But then it's not your face doing it, Hugh, it's the lambing nights.

**DANNY** Who held the bottle, though, Hugh?

**HUGH** Will did. Kitchen floor, every four hours, /this April—

**FEMI** Then she should follow Will.

**SUNNY** Does she not, though? You'd notice every one that trots over to you, Hugh, and never count the ones /trailing after Will.

**JESS** That's every teacher with favourites, that.

**DANNY** Go on, then. Name yours.
