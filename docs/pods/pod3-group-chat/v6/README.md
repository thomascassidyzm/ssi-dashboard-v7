# Pod 3 group chat, round 6: two hand-written drafts

Written by hand (no chain.cjs run) to Tom's brief of 2026-11-22: "the important thing with a real conversation is the interruptions, the improvising, the callbacks and so on, the talking over each other - not in a confrontational way, but in a 'this is real life kind of way'".

- `script-lightsout.md`: a fresh zombie evening. A power cut in the Kingfisher, and the table plans what they'd do, on a beer mat.
- `script-carp.md`: after dinner at a curry house, nobody can agree what the restaurant used to be.

## What changed against round 5

- **People come in at once.** Openers are a word or half a line, never a full sentence, so the second voice is there inside two seconds.
- **Two people talking at the same moment** is written as a *together* block, and a side-conversation that runs under the main one as an *aside* block that merges back. Rounds 1 to 5 only had one person cutting in on another.
- **Callbacks, more than once each.** Lights out: the eleven percent, the beer mat, morale, bait, the peacock, "what you want is a good—", Danny's pint. Carp: the fish, "it matters", Bowen's, the memory thing, and Hugh giving the answer three times before anyone hears it.
- **Improvising out loud.** The plan and the timeline are built at the table, wrongly, and corrected (Daleks and stairs; Rhi working out she never ate in the building).
- **Sentences finished wrongly** by someone else (gun / dog; Golden Dragon / Palace).
- **Mixed disfluency**, answering round 5's "the stammer is a bit uniform": stalls, wrong words, abandoned lines, a lost word, not only the doubled restart.
- **Plainer words.** Shorter lines than round 5, everyday vocabulary, no layer-3 move tags, no surprising-fact angle. The interest is in how the talk moves.
- **Five speakers each**, on the round 5 cards (`../personas.md`).
