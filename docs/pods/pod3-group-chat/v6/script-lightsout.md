# Lights out at the Kingfisher (script, round 6)

*How to read it:* a line ending in **—** is cut off or given up on. A **/** marks where the next person starts talking over the line. A boxed block marked *together* is two people talking at the same moment; *aside* is a side-conversation running under the main one. Small noises from listeners sit in brackets, *(Rhi: mm)*.

*The lights in the Kingfisher have just gone out: the back room, the bar, the street outside. Phone torches are coming on. A lad at the bar has just shouted "it's started!"*

**JESS** Oh— okay. Okay, that's everything, that's the /street as well—

**RHI** It's not the fuse, look. Chippy's out.

> *together*
>
> **DANNY** Nobody move, I've got a full pint here somewhere.
>
> **FEMI** Has anyone got a— hang on, mine's on eleven percent.

**HUGH** Well. That's it, then.

**JESS** That's what?

**HUGH** That's how it starts. In the films. Lights go, and then some lad at the bar goes—

**JESS** "It's started", yeah, he did, he actually /said it—

**DANNY** He's been waiting years to say that.

**FEMI** No, but— go on, though. Say it has. Say that's it, it's zombies. What do we do? Like, now. First thing.

**RHI** Stay here.

**DANNY** Here? In the— no. No, you go to Hugh's.

> *together*
>
> **HUGH** You're not coming to mine.
>
> **RHI** There's beer here, Danny, there's— there's crisps, there's a fire—

**DANNY** He's got land! He's got a, a— what's it. A tractor.

**HUGH** I've got a peacock next door that screams at four every morning. You'd not last a night. *(Jess: is that still going on?)* It's still going on.

**FEMI** No, but that's— hang on, that's good, that is. That's an alarm. Anything comes up the lane, the peacock goes off.

**HUGH** It goes off at a /leaf, Femi—

**JESS** Right, so Hugh's, with the peacock. I'm writing this down.

**RHI** On what?

**JESS** Beer mat. Has anyone got a pen— thank you. Okay. One: Hugh's.

**HUGH** I haven't said yes.

**JESS** One: Hugh's, question mark.

**FEMI** And then jobs. Everyone needs a— you need jobs, or it all falls apart by the, the second /day—

**RHI** I'm water. Water and heating. That's me done.

**DANNY** And Hugh's food, obviously.

**HUGH** What you want first, before any of that, is a good—

**JESS** Gun.

**HUGH** Dog. I was going to say dog.

**RHI** He's got a gun as well, mind.

**HUGH** It's in a cabinet. It's all above board.

**FEMI** Okay, so, dog. Jess, put dog.

**JESS** Dog. And what am I? What's my job?

**DANNY** Well, you're— you teach English.

**JESS** I know what I teach, Danny.

> *together*
>
> **RHI** She could do the kids. If there's kids.
>
> **FEMI** Morale?

**JESS** Morale. That's not a— that's what you give the person with no job.

**FEMI** It's on the mat now.

**JESS** I haven't written it!

**FEMI** Well, write it.

*[the barman comes round with tea lights in pint glasses]*

**RHI** Cheers, Gar. Is it the whole town, or—

**DANNY** And me? Nobody's given me a job.

> *aside — Hugh and Rhi, under the others*
>
> **HUGH** It'll be the substation, by the bridge. Went in ninety-eight, same thing.
>
> **RHI** The one by the— that floods, that does. I've said.
>
> **HUGH** You have said.

**FEMI** Depends. Are they fast ones or slow ones?

**DANNY** What's that got to do with /my job—

**FEMI** Because if they're slow, there's time for a solicitor. If they're fast you're, sorry, you're bait.

**JESS** Danny... bait...

**DANNY** Don't write that! Slow. They're slow, they've always been slow. It's the new ones that run, and it's wrong.

**FEMI** And they can't do stairs.

**JESS** That's Daleks.

**FEMI** Is it? No— yeah. Yeah, that's Daleks. Okay, they can do stairs.

**RHI** Who can't do stairs?

**DANNY** Nobody. Everybody can do stairs. Where've you two been?

**RHI** Substation. By the bridge. Hugh reckons it's just that.

**JESS** So not zombies.

**HUGH** I never said not zombies. I said substation. Could be both.

**FEMI** Nine percent.

**JESS** Turn your torch off, then, we've got candles.

**FEMI** I'm not sitting in the dark with Danny.

**DANNY** I'm bait. I'm no danger to anyone.

**RHI** Go on then, Jess, read it out. What've we got?

**JESS** One: Hugh's, question mark. Dog. Rhi, water. Hugh, food. Me, morale. Danny—

**DANNY** Legal.

**JESS** Bait, slash, legal. And Femi. We've not done Femi.

**FEMI** I'm floods. I mean, not floods— walls. Building things. I'd do the, the defences.

**HUGH** You do rivers.

**FEMI** It's the same thing! It's keeping a thing out of a place. Same job.

**RHI** And who's in charge?

> *together*
>
> **DANNY** Me.
>
> **HUGH** It's my farm.
>
> **JESS** Not Danny.

**RHI** No, listen, listen. I've got eight lads and half of them can't find their own van. I'm in charge.

**DANNY** We'll have a vote.

**HUGH** We'll not have a vote on my farm.

**JESS** Oh, it's your farm now? A minute ago we weren't /even coming—

**HUGH** If you're coming, you're coming properly. You're not turning up and, and voting.

**RHI** And nobody gets in who's been bitten. That's the rule. Because somebody always hides it, don't they, there's always one who—

**JESS** It's always the nice one.

**DANNY** It'd be Femi.

**FEMI** Why would it be me?

**DANNY** Because you'd want to check first. Before you said anything. You'd be upstairs on your own with a, with a ruler, going, well, it could be a bite—

**FEMI** I'd want a second opinion, yeah. That's not hiding, that's— *(Rhi: that's hiding)* —that's being careful!

**JESS** See, I'd tell everyone. Straight away. I'd be awful, I'd be crying, I'd want a, a /speech—

**HUGH** Morale.

**JESS** Shut up, Hugh.

*[the lights come back on. A cheer from the bar.]*

**RHI** Oh.

**DANNY** Oh, well, that's—

**JESS** Is that it?

**HUGH** Substation.

**FEMI** Six percent. I've used five percent on that.

**DANNY** Has anyone seen my pint? I had a full— Rhi, is that mine?

**RHI** It was.

**JESS** Don't put your glass on the mat! That's the plan, that is.

**RHI** Give it here, I'll keep it in the van.

**JESS** You're not having it. I'm morale, it's mine.

**HUGH** What you want, for next time, is a good—

**JESS** Dog.

**HUGH** Torch. A proper one. Yes, and a dog.
