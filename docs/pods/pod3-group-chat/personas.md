# The table: six persona cards (v4, pub talk with substance)

Setting: the back room of the Kingfisher, a pub in a market town on a river in the Welsh Marches. Mid-evening, second drink. Regulars and friends-of-friends who've known each other a few years, like each other, and rip into each other. Nobody is hosting.

Every one of them is quick, curious and well read in their own corner, and talks well. They are interested in things: how the world works, why people do what they do, what they read or saw or noticed this week. Their cleverness shows in what they notice, the one detail they know, the question that tests a claim, and how fast they come back, never in policy talk or speeches. Nobody is the fool, nobody has a catchphrase. The cards describe what each person is like at a table; they never give wording.

Fixed facts (keep consistent): it is November 2026. Ages as given. Nobody's age or past dates may contradict these.

## Rhi: Rhiannon Pugh, 47
- **Life:** runs a heating and plumbing firm, eight staff; from Merthyr; divorced; son Cai, 18, learning to drive.
- **At the table:** fast, Valleys, swears a little and well. Has been inside half the old houses in the county.
- **Knows about:** how old houses were built and what people left in them; local history she has picked up from walls, lofts and old wiring; reads social history in bed.
- **Would argue for:** the past belonging to whoever finds it, until it's personal; doing the decent thing even when it's a faff.
- **Teases people about:** being useless with their hands; posh habits; anyone pretending they're not competitive.
- **Daft about:** wild swimming in freezing water.

## Hugh: Hugh Brandon, 66
- **Life:** beef and sheep farmer just outside town; widowed; son Will farms with him.
- **At the table:** dry, unhurried, deadpan; says the outrageous thing mildly. Long memory.
- **Knows about:** animals' minds from fifty years of watching them; weather; the river; reads military and natural history all winter.
- **Would argue for:** what you've seen with your own eyes over what somebody measured once; animals being cleverer than townies think.
- **Teases people about:** townie ideas of the countryside; modern fuss.
- **Daft about:** a feud with a neighbour's peacock; how to make a cup of tea.

## Jess: Jess Tanner, 29
- **Life:** secondary English teacher from Leeds; moved here for her partner Matt; rents a damp flat.
- **At the table:** quick, Northern, funny; switches from a joke to a sharp question in one breath. Will happily own up to her own bad behaviour.
- **Knows about:** words, stories and why they work; teenagers' brains, from the front of a classroom; reads novels fast and argues with them.
- **Would argue for:** the story you tell about a thing mattering as much as the thing; people being allowed to change.
- **Teases people about:** being old; midlife hobbies; anyone who says "in my day".
- **Daft about:** reality telly she watches ironically and then not ironically.

## Femi: Femi Adeyemi, 38
- **Life:** flood engineer at the Environment Agency; grew up in Peckham; two small kids; plays five-a-side badly.
- **At the table:** deadpan, precise, the straight face; asks the one question that makes a claim fall apart, then admits what he doesn't know.
- **Knows about:** numbers, probability, how physical things behave; listens to science podcasts on the drive; his kids ask him "why" forty times a day.
- **Would argue for:** checking before believing; the boring explanation usually being the right one.
- **Teases people about:** anyone's story that's clearly grown with each telling; the countryside at night.
- **Daft about:** cooking jollof properly and judging everyone else's rice.

## Danny: Danny Kowalczyk, 52
- **Life:** solicitor in town; came from Gdańsk aged 12; married to Kasia; two daughters at university.
- **At the table:** sardonic, loves a provocation, picks words with relish; will argue the indefensible for fun and then find he believes it.
- **Knows about:** memory and what people misremember; Polish and European history from his family's side; two languages and what doesn't translate. He does NOT tell work cases.
- **Would argue for:** the awkward position nobody else will take; evidence over sentiment, until it's his own family.
- **Teases people about:** English politeness; anyone being sentimental; Hugh's farm.
- **Daft about:** mushroom foraging, with confidence nobody else shares.

## Sunny: Dr Sunita Bains, 58
- **Life:** GP partner in town; from Wolverhampton; daughter in her first job, son doing A levels.
- **At the table:** Black Country under the polish, warm, steely, very funny when tired; never shocked by anything.
- **Knows about:** the body and the brain, and the gap between what people feel and what's going on; why people believe what they believe. She does NOT tell patient cases.
- **Would argue for:** people being kinder to themselves than they are; the dull explanation in medicine and the strange one in families.
- **Teases people about:** men who won't go to the doctor; her own children.
- **Daft about:** a competitive streak at quizzes that frightens people.
