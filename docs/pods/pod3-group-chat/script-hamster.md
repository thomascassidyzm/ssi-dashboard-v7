# The replacement hamster (script)

*How to read it:* a **/** marks where the next speaker starts talking over the line. A line ending in **—** is cut off there. Short back-channels like *(Dean: mm)* sit in brackets where they land. *[Hugh's pint goes over]* is something physical happening at the table. *[move F16 …]* is a stage direction naming the layer-3 group-contribution move that turn performs (metagraph id); it is never spoken. [Watch it as a timed replay](https://watson-1.tail4968cb.ts.net/evidence/pod3-group-chat/hamster.html).

*Over half-term the hamster in Femi's daughter's class died, and the school quietly bought an identical one. His daughter worked it out because the new one bites. At tea tonight she asked him whether he'd do the same if she died. He has just told the table.*

**JESS** Hang on, how old is she? Because that's a horribly good question.

**FEMI** Six. She's— six and three-quarters, she'd want me to say the three-quarters. And she asked it over fish fingers, completely calm.

**SUNNY** Over fish /fingers—

**FEMI** And I said no, obviously, and she goes how would I— how would she know, because they kept the name, Nugget, same ginger, same everything, except /this one bites.

**HUGH** Did the— the old one never bite? Not once, in what, two years of being passed round thirty children?

*[Rhi's elbow nudges Femi's pint towards the table edge]*

**FEMI** *[move F2 Do it and throw something in]* Never. Mind your pint, Rhi. Never once, and the old one had a nick out of one ear, which she's already checked the new one for.

**RHI** Got it, sorry— she /checked—

**DANNY** Course she checked, it's a body double, the school's run in a body double and she's— she's doing forensics on it.

**SUNNY** My mum did that with budgies. Three of them, all /called Raja—

**FEMI** Three? Budgies live, what, ten years?

**SUNNY** No, not— over years, Femi, years. One went, and a new Raja was in the cage by teatime, same green, and nobody, nobody in that house ever said a word about it.

**HUGH** We do it with lambs. Skin the dead one and— tie the skin on an orphan, and the ewe mostly falls for it.

*[Jess's packet of crisps splits open across the table]*

**JESS** Oh for— they're everywhere. Sorry, Hugh, you dress the orphan up in the /dead one?—

**HUGH** Yes. Legs through the holes.

**FEMI** *[move F16 Fair enough, but I still]* Yeah, all right, the ewe falls for it, fine, but she's going on smell, isn't she? Mine went straight for the ear.

**DANNY** No, but go back— you said no, obviously, and I'm not sure it is obvious. Same ginger, same name, doesn't bite. Half the parents in this town would /be tempted, Femi.

**RHI** Go on /then—

**DANNY** Not in this pub, I won't.

*[the barman reaches in to clear the empties and goes for Jess's half-full glass]*

**JESS** No, that's— that's still mine. It'll have been a TA, always is, driving round pet shops with a photo on her phone.

**RHI** Never the TA, it's the— the caretaker.

**SUNNY** Would you, though, /Danny?—

**DANNY** *[move F11 And you?]* Probably, yes. Would you, Hugh?

**HUGH** Me? Every collie I've had's been Fly.

**RHI** And did any of the Flys bite?

*[the landlord's spaniel shoves out from under the table between Hugh's boots]*

**HUGH** Mind your feet, that's the spaniel. Second Fly had my thumb at the show, and Will reckons she's the /only real—

**JESS** The only real— hang on, so for Will the biter's the real one? That's, that's the exact opposite of your six-and-three-quarters, Femi.

**RHI** *[move F303 The shared line]* So the body double— the body double won, then. Will's done his forensics and he'd rather have the one that had your thumb.

**HUGH** No, he— he meant real collie.

**SUNNY** Did he, /though—

**HUGH** He— well. He might not've.

*[Femi's phone lights up face-up beside the crisps]*

**FEMI** That's her mum. She's still awake.

**JESS** Is she still— still on Nugget?

**FEMI** No, worse, she's— she's asked her mum now, and her mum's said yes, /she would—

**DANNY** Good. Honest woman, your wife.

**RHI** I had a loft out past Clun, there was a tin, a biscuit tin, and in it— four collars, cat collars, every one of them said Sooty, and the—

**FEMI** Same size? The collars, were they— same size?
