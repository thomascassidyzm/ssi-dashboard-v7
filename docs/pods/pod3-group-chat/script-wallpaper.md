# The note under the wallpaper (script)

*How to read it:* a **/** marks where the next speaker starts talking over the line. A line ending in **—** is cut off there. Short back-channels like *(Dean: mm)* sit in brackets where they land. *[Hugh's pint goes over]* is something physical happening at the table. *[move F16 …]* is a stage direction naming the layer-3 group-contribution move that turn performs (metagraph id); it is never spoken. [Watch it as a timed replay](https://watson-1.tail4968cb.ts.net/evidence/pod3-group-chat/wallpaper.html).

*Rhi stripped wallpaper in a front room on Broad Street today and found a note pencilled on the plaster by the man who papered it in 1962: his name, the date, the score of a Cardiff City match, and the words 'whoever reads this, I hope you're happier than me'. She's photographed it and is passing her phone round.*

**HUGH** Cardiff lost, then. That'll be the unhappiness, half of it.

**RHI** No, they won. Read it again.

**FEMI** Hang on, so /he—

**RHI** They won, they— two-nil, Femi.

*[Rhi's phone reaches Femi]*

**FEMI** Right, so not /the football, then.

**SUNNY** *[move F306 Exactly where I'm about to go]* That's— no, that's where I was going, if they won two-nil then it's not the football, it's whatever was waiting at home.

**DANNY** Or he was /just—

**RHI** Oi. Watch it, Gdańsk.

**JESS** Go on, Danny, just what?

**DANNY** Bored. Just bored, Jess.

*[the phone, passing back to Rhi, catches the rim of Jess's glass]*

**HUGH** Mind, that's your /pint—

**JESS** Got it, got it. Bored how?

**DANNY** Bored like— you're on your own in someone's front room all afternoon, wet paste, a pencil behind your ear. Gloom is what you write when nobody's watching.

**JESS** *[move F301 So what you're saying is]* So it's— it's desk graffiti, isn't it. It's what Year Nines carve under the lid, all doom and their initials.

**DANNY** Desk graffiti, yes, that's it. Under the lid, where nobody can /see you—

**RHI** No, he signed it, though.

**FEMI** Signed and dated, though, so it's checkable. Give us the /date again—

*[Rhi pulls her phone back and zooms in with two fingers]*

**RHI** Third of March, sixty-two. Saturday.

**HUGH** Had the wireless on, then.

**SUNNY** Saturday afternoon, though, papering someone else's front room while his team's playing. That's a man staying out of /his own house.

**HUGH** Married, was /he?—

**RHI** Doesn't say. Femi?

**FEMI** Well the Saturday's right, I did it in my head while you were zooming. The score I can't— I'd have to look it up. If it's wrong, mind, he wasn't listening to any wireless.

*[the dog under the table noses into Jess's bag after the crisps]*

**JESS** Get off my bag, you— no, but why write the score at all? That's not gloom, that's him wanting you to know which day.

**SUNNY** Because that's the one bit that went right that day, Jess. He puts the— the win /down first—

**HUGH** Or he'd a bob on it.

**RHI** Nah, they all put the date, paperhangers. I've had loads under old paper, name, date, once what he charged for the room. Never had one— never had one talk to me, mind.

**SUNNY** *[move F16 Fair enough, but I still]* Fine, they all date it. But the score?

**DANNY** An /alibi—

**JESS** Ooh, no, unless that's it, he wants it on record he was here papering and not wherever his wife thought he was.

**HUGH** Bit of a useless alibi, under three coats of paste. My father did ours, the back kitchen, about then, and— no, whatever he wrote, Mam never saw it. House was sold before /anyone stripped it.

**RHI** Hang on, which— where's the house?

**HUGH** Bottom of Mill /Lane—

*[Rhi's phone, face up by the crisps, buzzes and lights up with a message]*

**FEMI** Your phone's going, Rhi. Is that Broad Street? Are you telling them about it, or does it get papered over Monday?

**RHI** That's Cai. And /I— dunno yet.

**DANNY** Paper it. That's the whole point—
