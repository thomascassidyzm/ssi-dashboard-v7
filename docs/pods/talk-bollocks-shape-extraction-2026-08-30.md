## 0. The prediction — written before any mapping, committed as its own commit

Stated now, scored in §5, including where it is wrong. The two commissioned outcomes: a corpus that mints almost nothing means the trunk already covers symmetric discussion; one that mints heavily means the trunk was built from asymmetric material throughout.

**P1 — The corpus mostly LANDS, and the mint count is modest: between three and six genuinely new ids across nodes and moves, no more.** Reasoning: this corpus has already fed the trunk twice — N13–N17, O2/O5/O8 and M1–M5 are *made from it* — so its biggest shapes are already store furniture. The residue I expect to mint is the handful of §1c moves with no store home as the *admitted position*: the callback proper, the co-owned line, the metaphor handover, the live intrusion folded back, meta-comment on the medium, and possibly enacted dialogue. I predict the eight summit shapes will split roughly half-and-half: the specimen, pre-emption and the listener-names-it landing on existing material once looked at coldly; the misreading-corrected and the stacked commission likelier to need ids.

**P2 — The zero-asymmetry shapes will be the co-authorship cluster, and the kind that kills them will be K1 or K2, not K3 or K4.** Prediction: joint construction dies at K1 (when one party holds the information, completing their clause stops being co-authorship and becomes a guess to be graded); the mirrored tease and the specimen die at K2 (you cannot make an experiment of, or turn a tease on, the party who may end the encounter); the premise audit ("how do we know that?") dies at K1 (against an information-holder it becomes either deference or challenge-to-authority, a different shape); the haggle-of-precision survives to K1 and dies at K2. I predict K3 (record/instrument) and K4 (body/stake) kill almost nothing here — this corpus has no instruments and no stakes, so its shapes never learned to depend on their absence.

**P3 — At least one shape is invariant across the whole ladder, and my candidate is the reformulation ("so what you're saying is").** Health attests its cousins (read-back, F9/F104) at rung 4; POD-1 attests read-back at rung 1; if the summit form is the same positions, that is a second invariant to put beside #283's O7. Second candidate: the flagged digression-and-return, which the health corpus's N110 signposting resembles from the powerful side.

**P4 — The fifth kind (who holds the floor by role) will come out an ARTEFACT, not a kind.** The store's test for a kind is that *a position appears, disappears or changes hands*. My prediction: the 73/27 word split changes turn LENGTHS and hold ratios (G2/G6 grammar facts), but no position of any shape exists or vanishes because of it — the interviewer's two-word turns occupy the same response positions a symmetric partner would. If mapping finds even one shape whose positions require the role split, I am wrong and will say so.

**P5 — The null result holds.** The store records that all ten corpus survivability edges rest on non-delivery or relational moves, never on failure. I predict this corpus keeps the pattern: its survivable branches (challenge mid-story, the audit, the park) are all *relational*, and no edge minted here will rest on anything failing.

## 1. Method

All three parts were read once, closely, in order. Granularity is **the exchange**, as the health mapping used; where a shape only exists across a longer arc (the callback, the digression-and-return, the co-staged scene) the arc is used and said. Citations are `part:line` into the corpus files as they stand on `main`; the Method Pod's cited ranges drift by a line or two in places against the current files — same passages, no discrepancy of substance. Every verdict below was made against the seed rule the brief set: **LANDS** cites the store id it lands on; **MINTS** carries a proposed id in the 301–399 range; nothing existing is re-cut. The prior extraction (method-pod-full §1a–§1f, M1–M5, the O-overlay findings) is treated as baseline throughout — confirmed or extended, never re-discovered.

One deduplication policy, stated once: where two or three of the 23 corpus moves turned out to be one shape wearing different clothes, they were **consolidated into one mint**, health-style (its N102 merge is the precedent). Every consolidation is named in §2c and in the proposal file's `explicitly_not_minted`.

That is measurable text, not rhetoric: the specimen's failed recall is absorbed *into the claim being demonstrated*, because the claim was about forgetting.

- **P2 — LARGELY RIGHT, with one wrong kind.** Joint construction dies at K1 as predicted; the specimen changes at K2 and transforms at K4 (predicted K2/K4 via N102 — right); the premise audit **survives** rather than dying at K1 — health attests its descendants (N106 challenge, N104 challenge-for-meaning), so N14 is closer to invariant than I predicted; wrong there. K3/K4 kill almost nothing here, as predicted — with the one real exception (F305 dies at K4) noted.

- **P5 — WRONG, and gladly.** The null result does not hold here; it inverts (§3). The prediction repeated the store's pattern instead of asking what zero asymmetry does to the *price* of failure.

Score: two right, one half, two wrong. The two wrongs are both in the direction of under-estimating what this corpus uniquely carries — the same blindness the health mapping confessed to, pointed the other way.

## 6. The zero-asymmetry ruling — the axis measured

Ruler: #283's, unchanged. Kinds K1 (information) / K2 (authority to refuse) / K3 (record and instrument) / K4 (body or stake), held by the other party; a kind changes a shape only if **a position appears, disappears or changes hands** — never merely gets harder. Every shape below is attested at rung 0, `kinds_held_by_other_party: []`.

**Dies at K1 — five shapes. Information symmetry is the load-bearing wall of the summit.**

| Shape | What breaks, and the remnant |
|---|---|
| N301 joint construction | The completion position stops being a contribution and becomes **an answer to be graded**; ratification becomes marking. Evidence by absence: zero completions in pod-0's 231 rows, zero in health's 438 turns — nobody finishes the barista's or the doctor's clause. Remnant: P3/F9 read-back — you may *restate*, never *complete*. |
| N304 the reported claim | The park-it-unresolved position vanishes: against an information-holder someone owes a verdict ("is that true, doctor?"). Remnant: N8 recommendation-with-grounds, or N104's norm-referencing. |
| N305 the proxy pitch | The owner's *adopt-the-improvement* position vanishes; pitching the holder's own material back becomes a read-back to be corrected. Remnant: F9, hardening to F104 by rung 4. |
| F301 (gift form) | The adoption position dies — the K1-holder's claim is not yours to upgrade. The family survives as verification: F9 at rung 1, F104 solicited at rung 4. **The reformulation is the axis's clearest specimen: one act, four shapes, one per rung.** |
| F306 pre-emption | "Exactly where I'm about to go" presupposes equal access to the terrain; across an information gradient the ratification position cannot be honestly occupied. No remnant — it simply has no higher-rung form, which is why nothing like it exists in pod-0 or health. |

**Dies or changes at K2 — three.** N302 (the licence to digress becomes a grant from whoever controls the encounter; health's N110 signposting is what replaces it), N306 (you may not script the voice of a party who can refuse the real scene; remnant N4 instruction), F304's joke position (the medium belongs to the encounter-controller; the remnant is health's N101 medium *contract* — the same content, hardened from play into licence, the cleanest graded-transformation pair in the two corpora). N303 the specimen changes here too: a consent position materialises.

**Dies at K3 — the record half of N302.** When one party holds the record, the joint-memory position ("where were we?" answered by *either* party) changes hands permanently. Noting for honesty: at 2:144 the tease is prosecuted **by invoking the tape** ("because this is recording…") — at rung 0 even the record is a shared toy; K3 is precisely the moment it stops being one.

**Dies at K4 — two.** N303 completes its transformation into health's **N102 consent-and-narrated-act** (consent formalises, narration becomes duty, the result changes owners — the exact O3→O101 pattern). F305 absorb-and-build is *forbidden* at K4: absorbing the acted-upon party's counterexample is what N106 stop-and-verify exists to prevent; the halt replaces the absorption.

**Invariant across the whole ladder — two, plus #283's O7.**
- **F302, the epistemic flag that licenses.** Rung 0: "It proves nothing — accept it anyway" licenses the anecdote. Rung 4: "I won't promise what I can't be sure of" (health N109/S105) licenses the promise — *the bound IS the reassurance*. Same positions at every rung: flag → full-strength delivery → uptake. This is the strongest trunk result in this file and belongs beside O7.
- **F303, the shared line** — invariant to asymmetry but gated by a **second axis the ladder does not carry: shared history**. No kind kills a callback; the absence of a past does. Named as an axis-candidate, not smuggled onto this one.

**The census, said plainly:** of twelve mints, five die at K1, three at K2, one at K3, two at K4, two are invariant. **The summit is mostly a K1 phenomenon** — what the zero pole needs above all is that *neither party knows more*, and authority only kills what information has not already killed. That is the axis measured: each kind, added, strips a nameable position from a nameable shape, and the remnants are — repeatedly — the store's existing rung-1 and rung-4 furniture. The ladder is real, and it is walkable in both directions.

## 7. The fifth kind — floor-by-role: ARTEFACT, ruled with evidence

The known artefact is real: these are interviews, Aran holds ~73% of the words, Tom's median turn is two words. The question commissioned: is **who holds the floor by role** a fifth kind, or a recording artefact?

**Ruling: artefact — it fails the store's own test for a kind.** A kind changes a shape only if a position appears, disappears or changes hands. Applying that:

1. **No position is role-locked.** Every mintable position that could be checked is attested in both directions: the completion (Aran completes Tom at 1:26–28; Tom completes Aran at 2:169–171, 4:84–85); the reformulation (Tom at 4:177–180; Aran at 4:33–35, reformulating *Tom's* scarcity line); the epistemic flag (Aran's crocodile; Tom's "based on almost nothing other than a sense of what I know about China", 2:349); the audit (Aran audits Tom's think-tank at 4:148; Tom audits Aran's Bjork claim at 1:336). The one exception is honest and telling: N303 the specimen runs one direction only — and it tracks who owns the *demonstrable method* in that passage, not who holds the microphone.
2. **What actually produces the 73/27 split is K1 rotating slowly.** The floor follows who knows the current topic; the recording's topic is Aran's methodology, so topic-local information asymmetry sits with Aran for most of the hour — and the moment the topic moves to Tom's terrain (the think-tank, 4:150–161; memory research, 1:315–317; the currency mechanics, 4:302–305) the floor, the holds and the audited seat all move with it, instantly. A *kind* would not rotate on a sentence boundary.

Which yields the sharpest thing this extraction found, offered to Tom as a frame rather than smuggled in as fact: **rung 0 is not the absence of asymmetry — it is asymmetry rotating freely.** Somebody always knows more about *this sentence*. What the ladder's kinds measure is asymmetry **pinned to one party for the whole encounter by the world** — the stock, the badge, the chart, the body. And that is exactly why the summit shapes die as kinds accrue: every K1-killed shape in §6 is one whose defining position must be **swappable mid-conversation** (completion, pre-emption, the adopted reformulation), and a kind is precisely a position that has stopped swapping. The zero pole is a dynamic equilibrium, not an empty scale.
