# The Italian pair overlay — the Method Pod, chapter cut

**Authored 2026-09-01. EVERY ITALIAN LINE IN THIS DOCUMENT IS DRAFT-FOR-ARAN.** Under the
standing text-approval policy (`docs/pods/text-approval-policy-2026-08-16.md`), no worker signs
off target-language text: nothing here renders, ships, or enters a pod until Aran has read it.
**No audio pass is queued by this document, and none may be queued until the draft flag clears.**

**What this is.** The pair-overlay layer over the Method Pod's chapter cut
(`docs/pods/method-pod-chapters-2026-08-30.md` — 12 chapters, 309 turns, Italian already
present). This is **frame-mapping, not translation**: the English known side owns the frames;
this document is the mapping table for the eng→ita pair, every mapping classified
**deterministic / split / inversion / erasure**, every re-cut reported, and the places where the
curriculum relocates for Italian named. Because the Italian here already exists, this overlay
does a second thing the Welsh health overlay (its template,
`docs/sector-pods/health-welsh-pair-overlay-2026-09-01.md`) did not have to: it **audits the
existing Italian for ZUT** against itself, chapter against chapter, and where the same English
intention had bought two Italian forms, it re-cuts and corrects — every corrected line in §3's
change list with its reason. Italian that is ZUT-clean is not touched, however tempting.

**Scope default, flagged for Tom:** two cuts of this pod exist and the choice between them is
formally outstanding. This overlay binds to the **chapter cut** (12 chapters), because the
learning flagship's own header says the chapter form *"is settled"* and builds on it — so the
chapter cut is where the flagship's overlay must bind anyway. The 43-scene cut shares the same
substance and most of the same Italian; §7 notes what would need re-cutting if the scene cut
wins. One line from Tom redirects it.

**ZUT, the only gate, one-directional:** one English intention → exactly one Italian form,
document-wide — and across the *pair* of documents, since the flagship overlay
(`learning-flagship-italian-pair-overlay-2026-09-01.md`) shares this ledger. Convergence
(several knowns landing on one Italian form) is reception and is welcome; divergence kills, and
every place English wanted to fork is resolved in §3 by re-cutting, never by blocking a word.
The production ledger was swept mechanically end-to-end for same-known→two-targets (§3 states
the sweep and its residue).

**The near-neighbour points** found here go to the consolidated register
(`spanish-italian-near-neighbour-register-2026-09-01.md`) — the Bologna pre-test's instrument.
Each chapter's existing Near-neighbour line was **verified, not copied**; anything I could not
stand behind is marked there, not dropped.

**Format note (the one adaptation from the Welsh template, stated):** the Welsh overlay
reproduced every seed's draft line because no other document carried it. Here the full
English/Italian dialogue already lives in the chapters doc, so §1 does not duplicate 309
bilingual turns: per chapter it gives the mapping table — `chunk | Italian | class | note`,
keyed by turn number — and quotes full lines only where the overlay **changes** them. Chunk
size rides at conversation granularity, per the ruling: a LEGO is the smallest chunk that is
deterministic known→target, so a long heard hold maps in clause-sized chunks and a would-be
fork that dies inside one of them is the granularity ruling working, not an omission.

full and listed again in §3.
