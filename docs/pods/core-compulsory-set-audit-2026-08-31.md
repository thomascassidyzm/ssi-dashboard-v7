The union-coverage job asked "what does the union cover" and answered *all 23 shapes*. Wrong
object: **no learner walks the union.** Under Tom's naming ruling — **CORE** is the compulsory
walk (the live POD 1, slug `pod-0`); everything else is an **optional walk**, and at least one
optional walk is always in play — the right question is: **what does CORE alone deliver, and is
that the right compulsory set?**

Three columns, each placement argued:

1. **CORE** — a learner who never meets it is missing something they must own.
2. **EVERY OPTIONAL WALK** — guaranteed as an authoring floor on every optional walk; effectively
   compulsory at zero compulsory cost, because one optional walk is always in play.
3. **ENRICHMENT** — genuinely fine to meet only sometimes.

Starting facts, measured: 14 of 23 shapes hang on CORE alone (the whole transactional lattice,
all six bound pairs), and all 19 composition edges are attested by CORE and nothing else. **That
concentration is the architecture working**: CORE is the trunk, and the trunk carrying the trunk's
load is not fragility. Nothing below "fixes" it.

## The 19 composition edges

**All 19: CORE.** Composition is the grammar of extension — how a two-turn order becomes six turns
of business — and extension mechanics are exactly what a learner must own to leave the phrasebook.
All 19 are attested by CORE and nothing else, and that is the architecture working: the discursive
walks declare shapes flat at chapter level and have never walked a containment. Optional walks
re-attest composition opportunistically; no floor is mandated because no floor is needed — the
trunk already guarantees it.

## The six withheld recoveries — attachment points in CORE

Class finding, proven by scenes 17 and 39: **recovery halves never appear unless commissioned by
name.** This is urgent under the naming ruling: today every attested discursive recovery lives in
the Method Pod, which is optional — so a learner taking only CORE meets branches whose recoveries
they were never shown, and CORE itself trains the ASK that summons them. Asking presupposes
surviving a plain no; the trunk must not teach a move whose failure branch it never covers.

Constraint honoured: **CORE's existing order does not change.** A pod is a branch point with its
continuations attached — each recovery ATTACHES to the scene that already stages its branch, as
the other continuation of a turn that already exists. Nothing is reordered, no walk lengthens, and
the recovery arrives while the learner is in trouble, which is the only time it is worth anything.

is authored**. Five of six attach; one honestly cannot.
