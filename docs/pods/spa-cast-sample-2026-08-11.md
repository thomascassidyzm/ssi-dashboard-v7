# Spanish, two casts, one listen

**11 Aug 2026 · `spa_for_eng`.** You asked to hear xAI's Spanish at an explicit `es-ES` handle
before settling the Iberian cast. Here it is, against the Azure pair the course is currently cast
to, on the same seven lines of the same pod conversation.

**Play A and B on each line and pick by ear.** Every clip below is newly rendered for this page. It
is a sample only: no course audio was replaced, no casting was changed, nothing was deleted.

---

## What you are choosing between

**A — Azure Elvira + Alvaro at `es-ES`.** The live cast, awaiting your approval. Azure returns both
as Spanish (Spain) and ~24,000 of the course's clips are already in these two voices.

**B — xAI Manuel + Eve at `es-ES`.** The question I could not settle without spending money. The
pod you rejected was rendered at the bare handle `es`, which is xAI's Latin American default — the
same trap Portuguese hit when native `pt` turned out to be Brazilian. These are the same engine,
steered explicitly at Spain for the first time.

**C — xAI Ara at `es-ES`, female lines only.** A second shot at the female seat, for the reason in
the next section.

## One thing you should know before you listen

**xAI has no native Spanish female voice.** Its live catalogue today returns 26 voices and every
single one is tagged `multilingual` — there is no Spanish list at all. The four Spanish-named ids
this repo holds from the older catalogue — Manuel, Javier, Diego, Andres — still synthesise, and
all four are male.

So B is not a like-for-like pair: **Manuel is a Spanish-named voice, Eve is a multilingual one
being steered at Spanish.** The male half of B is the stronger test of "does xAI do Iberian"; the
female half is the weak link, which is why Ara gets a third row on the female lines. If B's men
sound right to you and its women do not, that gap is the reason, and it is a real constraint on
choosing xAI, not a rendering accident.

One measurable difference, for what it is worth to your ear: **the xAI clips run roughly a third
shorter than the Azure ones** on identical text — 1.6s against 2.5s on the opening line, 4.7s
against 7.0s on the longest. That is pace, not truncation; every clip is complete.

## What happens after you pick

**If A (Azure Elvira + Alvaro):** nothing needs recasting — the pods already carry it. Say the word
and the cast gets approved on the record, which unlocks bulk pod generation.

**If B or C (xAI):** the four Spanish pods get recast to the pair you name and the pool reordered
behind it, same as the Azure recast was done, then re-approved. No audio is deleted in either case;
the existing pod clips were rendered on the old rejected cast and are replaced by generation, not
by deletion.

**If neither sounds Iberian enough:** say so and the next move is a different Azure Spain pair
(Azure has several) rather than more xAI — because the handle has now been tested and its answer is
whatever you just heard.
