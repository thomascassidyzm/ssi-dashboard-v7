## What was done, in order

1. **Rendered** 22 new clips (11 Italian + 11 English) through `generatePodAudio` — the
   same function `/generate-pods` calls. Nothing was unlinked first, so the pod never
   went silent.
2. **Verified** every one on the bytes S3 actually serves: stored voice, ffprobe
   duration, speech present, truncation against what the destination voice predicts,
   whisper decode. 22 of 22 passed. Then the estate's own `verify-pod-clips.cjs` ran
   independently and returned **22 CLEAN, 0 ADVISORY, 0 REVIEW, 0 ERROR**.
3. **Swapped** the 22 links in one transaction, each with a before-state assertion.
4. **Deleted nothing.** All 22 superseded clips are alive in `course_audio` and in S3 —
   which is why the players above still work, and what makes step 3 reversible.

Cost: 22 clips, roughly 700 characters of TTS.
