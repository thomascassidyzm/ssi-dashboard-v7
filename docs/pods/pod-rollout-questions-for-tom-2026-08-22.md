## 1. Welsh and three variants are already on the new content — rename them, or leave them?

Welsh north, Welsh south, Syrian Arabic, Austrian German and Canadian French already hold the
new-generation pod (231 or 232 sentences), but under the old `Pod 0` name rather than `Pod 1`.
Nothing about them is out of date — only the label is.

Welsh is the special one: it's being recorded by Aran and Catrin, human voices, not rendered. So
there is no old pod to replace and no audio to make. Renaming is pure cosmetics, and it would mean
moving real learner progress across for a label — Welsh north has the highest listening activity of
any premium course on the board.

**My recommendation: leave them on Pod 0 for now.** Renaming buys a tidier name and costs a
progress migration on your busiest course, so it fails the cheaper test. When Welsh next gets a
genuine content change, the rename comes free as part of that move.

## 3. A small inconsistency in who the male voice plays

Your two-voice ruling says the male voice carries the learner thread and the female voice plays
everyone else. Of the five courses that went live today, Croatian and Spanish do that. Italian,
French and Chinese have it the other way round — the learner is the female voice — and you passed
all five by ear. Japanese follows the same pattern as the majority and has the learner female.

It changes nothing about how a course sounds in isolation; it only means a learner moving between
languages hears themselves swap gender.

**My recommendation: leave it.** You've already heard and passed both arrangements, and fixing it
means re-rendering courses that are live and sound fine — that's cost with no gain your ear has
asked for. Worth knowing rather than worth doing.
