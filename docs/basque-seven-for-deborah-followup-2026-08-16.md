Seven more Basque sentences — a short follow-up

Thank you for the last round. Your answer on *esna* settled it: being awake takes *egon*,
not *izan*. We've changed the course's teaching piece for that sentence to *egotea* so it
matches. That leaves seven sentences in the same group still saying *esna izatea*, and we'd
like your wording for them rather than guessing.

For each one, the question is the same: is it enough to swap *esna izatea* for *esna egotea*,
or does the sentence need more than that to read properly? And if any of the seven should
genuinely stay as *izatea*, say which and why — we'd rather know.

They're all Round 152, sentence 55. A word or a line each is plenty, and you can reply to Tom
with just the numbers. Nothing to log into.

1. esna izatea gauza ona da
   "being awake is a good thing"

2. esna izatea erabilgarria da
   "being awake is useful"

3. baina esna izatea ez dut nahi
   "but I don't want to be awake"

4. esna izatea ongi dela uste dut
   "I think being awake is good"

5. esna izatea baina nekatuta nago
   "being awake but I'm tired"

6. esna izatea gauza ona dela uste dut
   "I think being awake is a good thing"

7. goizean esna izatea erabilgarria da
   "in the morning being awake is useful"

That's all seven. Whenever suits you.
