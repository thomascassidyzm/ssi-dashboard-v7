## Kai's question: does any SEED SENTENCE embed a question?

**Yes — roughly 70 of the 668 seed sentences do, and every one of them is in correct English
declarative order. Not one seed sentence carries the defect.**

Method: all 668 English seed sentences pulled from production; 127 carry a non-initial
wh-word or `if`; 84 of those sit under a question-embedding matrix predicate; hand-classification
removes 15 relative clauses and conditional `if`s (`I know a young man who wants to work with you`,
`Do you mind if I ask you some questions?`) and adds back the seed-44 fragment, leaving **~70**. A
scan for the defect shape itself — a matrix trigger followed by an inverted clause — returns **0 of
668 seeds**.

Examples, all live seed sentences:

- seed 150 — **Can you tell me what your name is?** (not *what is your name*)
- seed 177 — **I'll ask her where she wants to go.** (not *where does she want to go*)
- seed 176 — **I'll ask him if he'll be able to help next year.** — the `if` complementiser, taught from seed 10
- seed 465 — **Next time I will ask her what her name is.**
- seed 213 — **We don't know what they're trying to achieve.**
- seed 382 — **Did you ask where he wanted to put it?**

**So this is a decomposition hole, not a curriculum hole.** The seed layer teaches the embedded shape
about seventy times over and never gets it wrong. What went wrong lives one layer down: ten late
legos were cited as main-clause interrogatives — correctly, because their own seed sentence *is* a
question — and the phrase generator then dropped those chunks verbatim under `I don't know ___`.

One caveat worth Kai's eye, and it is the reason the escalation in README §6 still stands: the seeds
teach the *shape* but never the *transformation*. A learner drilled on `what would you like` has met
`what your name is` in a different sentence and has no chunk that turns the first into the second.
That is still a real gap — it is just a smaller and much cheaper one than "the course never teaches
this at all", because the target form is already all over the seed layer and could be taught from it.
