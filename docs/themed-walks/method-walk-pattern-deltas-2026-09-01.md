# Pattern deltas from the Method walk — what the next four briefs must say

**Authored 2026-09-01**, alongside the canonical Method walk pod-seed set
(`method-walk-pod-seed-set-2026-09-01.md`). The act has now run three times — health general
(`docs/sector-pods/health-general-seed-set-2026-08-31.md`), CORE / the every-optional-walk
floor (`docs/pods/core-pod-seed-set-2026-08-31.md`), and this walk — and the Method corpus
was deliberately the least like the first two. This report is the third run's residue: what
the health/CORE pattern assumed that did not hold here, what held perfectly, and the exact
sentences the learning, trade, retail and later briefs should carry. Written for the person
writing those briefs, not for the general reader.

## What held — the pattern is real

The section skeleton survived contact untouched: **projection → anchor → scene-0 reference +
floor inheritance → spine (blocks, per-seed source/seed/cut/job/ownership) → baskets at the
4/5 floors → row ledger reconciled exactly → one-line decisions + mechanical verification →
explicit gaps → Appendix A.** Nothing in that shape needed re-inventing, and the next briefs
can require it by reference. The admission-economy sequencing, the zero-manufacture hunt,
the g24 row-split precedent, the decision-12 worn-sibling rule, the block-N late-fill
device, and depth-before-breadth all transferred without strain. The mechanical
reconciliation earned its keep again: it caught nine rows dispositioned in prose but never
seeded (block L), the same class of catch CORE's census made.

## The deltas — numbered, with the sentence the next brief needs

**1. Roles may not be speaker-aligned — say how to derive them, not what they are.**
Health's roles fell out of the speaker tags (HW vs P). The Method corpus has two mouths,
ARAN and TOM, and they do not partition the acts: both probe, both hold forth, both ratify.
Role had to be derived **over acts**, and the walk's roles came out as discussant ⊂
explainer — functional chairs, not people. *The next brief must say:* "Derive roles from
whose mouths exist in the corpus, then CHECK whether the mouths partition the acts. If they
do (service corpora — trade and retail almost certainly will), project by speaker as health
did. If they do not, project over acts and demonstrate role-symmetry per act, as the Method
set does in §1." Learning-walk authors should expect a mixed case (teacher/learner mouths
with symmetric peer stretches).

**2. A walk can have TWO realisations of one pod — demand arm-invariance.**
The Method Pod lives in `canonical_pod_scenarios` twice: `method-pod-43-scene` (276 rows)
and `method-pod-chapters` (309 rows), sharing ~200 verbatim lines, with NO ruling choosing
between them. Health had one corpus document. The set had to be **arm-invariant**: cite
both row ids, count the body once (the pod-union coverage doc's own precedent), and survive
either arm being sacked. *The next brief must say:* "Name the pod slug(s) explicitly. If
more than one realisation exists, cite all arms per seed, count shared lines once, and stay
arm-invariant. Restate the union-coverage warning: outcome declarations unique to a sacked
arm must be ported to the survivor."

**3. The corpus BEHIND the pod is not extractable — only delivered pairs are.**
Talk Bollocks (parts 1, 2, 4) is the Method Pod's source, but its lines were never
delivered to a learner as known/target pairs, so Tom's "if it's been in a dialogue, they
own it" does not reach them. Extraction source = `canonical_pod_scenarios` rows only;
upstream corpora are provenance. *The next brief must say exactly that sentence.* (The
learning walk has the same shape: `learning-flagship` rows are the source, whatever
documents fed them are not.)

**4. There may be no opener block — the floor-inheritance surface moves.**
Health resolved 30 OPENER turns against scene 0 (16 defer / 14 mint). The Method corpus
starts mid-friendship at full speed; the inheritance surface turned out to be the
floor-adjacent SCENES (the repair loop, the scheduling run) plus scattered bare tokens —
27 turns, 11 defer / 16 seed, six of those at strict zero. *The next brief must say:*
"Locate the floor-adjacent material wherever it actually is — do not assume an opener
block. Resolve it turn by turn against scene 0 and CP001–CP195, and report your own
defer/mint split." Trade and retail will look more like health here (service scripts open
with greetings); the sentence still belongs in the brief.

**5. Echo density is the zero-manufacture budget — mine echoes first.**
CORE manufactured 9 strict zeros; spa_for_eng has 7 in 668. The Method set has **40 in
226**, because peer dialogue is built of echoes, quote-backs, and ratification repeats —
each one a seed whose whole cut is already owned. *The next brief must say:* "Count the
corpus's echo/repeat structure early; every verbatim echo of minted material is a
candidate zero, and zeros are the highest-yield seeds. A service-scripted corpus will
yield fewer; do not force them."

**6. Totem content is not slot content.**
In health, props were slots (names, drugs, meals). Here "the fish", "the dog", "the
crocodile", "the garden", "the room" are load-bearing CALLBACK objects — later turns lean
on them by name, so they minted as content LEGOs, while Hong Kong and Manx stayed slots.
The test that worked: *does any later turn reach back to this noun by name? Totem, mint
it. Otherwise slot.* Put that test in the brief.

**7. Long rows split by act as the NORM, and compression must be licensed.**
CORE split a handful of rows; here multi-act rows are routine (c209 split three ways;
c284 three ways; two exchanges seeded as single two-row seeds where the reply is the
act's other half — MW176, MW204). And long expository turns forced **stated
compressions**: canonical seed = the producible spine of the row, the remainder named
receptive in place. *The next brief must say:* "Split multi-act rows per the g24
precedent, naming each half's seed. Where a row is part-producible, the canonical seed
may compress it — but every dropped clause is named receptive in the entry, never
silently reworded. Two-row seeds are legal when an adjacency pair is one act."

**8. The receptive bucket needs named sub-classes or it reads as laziness.**
Health had almost no receptive rows; this walk has 155 of 585 — a peer-ideas corpus is
mostly listening material, BY DESIGN ("heard is not owned" is the pod's own doctrine).
The ledger classes them: (a) explainer exposition — the future deeper role's worklist;
(b) speaker biography and staging; (c) receptive echoes of owned material; (d)
designed-refusal content. *The next brief must say:* "Class the receptive bucket; a bare
list invites re-litigation. Class (a) is the named worklist for the walk's deeper role."

**9. Every walk ships its subset role and names its superset.**
Health shipped general and named nurse. Method ships discussant and names explainer.
*The next brief must say:* "Name the role you ship and the strict superset that waits;
leave the superset's worklist in the ledger (delta 8's class (a))."

**10. Register rulings that are now standing calls, not per-walk questions:**
profanity stays receptive, never minted (c7 "Do some cool shit."); a known-side surface
carrying two intentions never mints the second sense ("very well" concession vs CP010
wellness — drop it from the canonical seed and state the edit for the ZUT gate); coinages
mint as INTENTIONS with an overlay note, never as morphology ("un-say it");
English-structural devices (tag-echo "isn't there", bare co-completion, one-word content
echo) are logged as gaps for the pair overlay to decide per target, not minted at the
canonical layer. Carry all four into every following brief verbatim.

**11. Appendix A grows monotonically — extend by reference, never fork.**
Method added to CORE's core: "I don't know", "I like", the quotative "I say", a dozen glue
words, and one composition RULE (cardinal + bare time unit is core; seasons, "times",
"half a day" are not). *The next brief must say:* "Restate CORE's Appendix A by reference,
add only what your corpus forces, justify each addition by class, and state what is
deliberately NOT in a class you extend."

**12. Scale calibration, for planning the next four.**
585 rows (372 distinct lines) → 226 seeds, 318 new LEGOs, 40 zeros, in eleven blocks +
late fills. Health: 85 turns → 57 seeds. The working ratio is **roughly 0.6 seeds per
distinct producible-projection line**; row count alone overestimates (only 261 of 585 rows
seeded directly). Baskets: 318 LEGOs fanned out to 10 disjoint contiguous ranges of ~32
LEGOs each, one worker per range, prior-availability windows generated mechanically per
range — the CORE 13-worker shape, and it held. Budget the same way.

**13. Basket workers under-use the floor and pad with filler — put the defect classes in
the brief up front.** The first-landed range showed three systematic defects: BUILD
phrases consisting of the naked LEGO alone (violates the BUILD definition — new LEGO plus
prior material, always), filler tails (", actually" / ", exactly") padding toward the
floor instead of reaching for floor chunks, and near-zero use of the 400-chunk floor
inventory that was sitting in the brief. The assembly pass swept all three. *The next
brief must put these three named defects in the basket workers' instructions from the
start* — it is cheaper than sweeping.

**14. One assumption of the health format that genuinely broke: "the projection is a
turn-count".** Health's §1 reconciled 85 turns because turns were the unit. In a corpus
where one row can carry three acts and two rows can carry one act, the reconciliation unit
is the ROW disposition (§6's four buckets summing to the table's row count), and the
projection section states the TEST, not a number. The next brief should require "every row
of the pod's table(s) accounted in the ledger" rather than "N turns reconciled."

## What Method being first bought

The oddities found here — parallel arms, act-derived roles, echo-driven zeros, totem
content, compression licensing, the receptive taxonomy — are exactly the ones a
health-shaped brief would have crashed into blind on the learning walk (the next most
conversational corpus). Trade and retail should be EASIER than health was: speaker-aligned
roles, scripted service acts, floor-heavy openers, few zeros, few compressions. If a trade
or retail extraction starts producing Method-shaped statistics (many zeros, many
compressions, act-derived roles), that is a signal the corpus is not service-scripted and
the brief's assumptions should be re-checked before authoring continues.
