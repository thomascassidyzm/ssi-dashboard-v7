# Hospitality mapped onto the shape metagraph — the home-furniture port

**2026-08-30.** The fourth sector, mapped in PRODUCTION, not as a pilot: the port question was
answered by trades (health minted 10, near-neighbour Ireland 2, far-sector trades 1 — the cost
curve bent DOWN) and Tom has licensed the fan-out. This mapping's job is to map hospitality,
mint only what genuinely cannot be reached, and say so. The expected result, stated in the
commission, is **zero or one mints** — and a sector that mints nothing is the trunk working,
not the job failing.

Hospitality has a property no prior sector had: **it is the store's own furniture viewed from the
opposite seat.** The trunk was derived from `pod-0` — cafés, counters, orders, bills — walked
largely from the customer's chair, and POD-1's rung-1 definition ("the barista knows the stock")
IS this sector's K1. Since role assignment is a walk annotation and not a shape distinction, the
prior expectation is that hospitality lands overwhelmingly on **[store]** with the seat flipped,
and that any genuine novelty concentrates where the three prior sectors' novelty concentrated:
where duty overrides consent. The commission expects that site to be **absent here at node
grade**, and whether it is absent, attenuated, or present is a finding this document must state
plainly rather than assume.

**There is no corpus, and nothing below pretends otherwise.** Nobody has written down the
recurring spoken encounters of a hospitality worker. The inventory in §3 is generated by this
mapping — every entry labelled **derived-confident** (any competent server, receptionist or
bartender has this conversation weekly; no research needed to assert it exists) or
**constructed** (reasoned into existence; may not survive contact with a real waiter). This is a
derivation test, not an attestation test: it can show what the trunk can and cannot EXPRESS for
the sector and where any mints concentrate; it cannot show frequencies, and no percentage below
pretends to be one.

## 3. The encounter inventory — written down for the first time

The recurring structured spoken encounters of a hospitality worker, in two families: **with the
guest** (E1–E11) and **with the shift** (E12–E16). Each is a position sequence with its failure
branch, because survivability is defined on the failure branch. Labels per the standard set by
Ireland: *derived-confident* or *constructed*.

**E1 — Welcome, booking check, seating / check-in.** *(derived-confident.)* The hail returned →
the booking asked for BY A DATUM THE DESK ALREADY HOLDS ("name on the reservation?") → matched
against the system → the walk-through: what happens next, in order ("breakfast is seven till ten,
the lift's behind you, checkout's eleven") → keys/table → close. **Failure branch:** no booking
found → the record searched aloud, the alternatives offered ("nothing under that name — could it
be booked under the company?"), or the walk-in path opened with its bounds ("I can do you a table
at 9:15 if you're happy to be out by 10:45").

**E2 — Taking the order.** *(derived-confident.)* Solicit → order → the option-choice chains
("how would you like that cooked?"), repeatable → availability answered where it fails ("the sea
bass is finished tonight — the hake is from the same boat, if that helps") → the order read back
→ banked to the kitchen. **Failure branch:** the guest cannot choose → recommendation solicited
and given with grounds, uptake.

**E3 — The recommendation / concierge.** *(derived-confident.)* "What's good?" — solicited or
volunteered → recommend with grounds ("the lamb's what I'd have — it came in this morning") →
uptake; and at the desk, the concierge form: the ask, the recommendation, the DIRECTIONS
(instruction-giving with read-backs), the booking made on the guest's behalf via a named third
party. **Failure branch:** the recommendation is rejected → re-solicit against the guest's
constraint ("nothing too heavy — right, then the bream").

**E4 — The allergy and the constraint.** *(derived-confident — and the sector's highest-stakes
line.)* The constraint declared, bidden or unbidden ("nut allergy — it's serious") → checked
against the source ("the pesto has pine nuts; let me check the dressing with the kitchen") →
accommodated now → committed to the RECORD (the ticket flagged, the KP told, the handover told)
→ acknowledged. **Failure branch:** the kitchen cannot guarantee it → the honest bound ("I can't
promise the fryer is nut-free — the grilled dishes I can stand behind"), and the guest decides.

**E5 — The check-back and the complaint.** *(derived-confident.)* Mid-consumption, the house
re-enters and solicits assessment ("how is everything?") → ritual close ("lovely, thanks") — OR
the complaint enters, licensed ("actually, this is lukewarm") → the apology ON BEHALF ("I'm
sorry about that — I'll get it re-fired") → the repair, with the unasked extra where the house
judges it ("and the coffees are on us") → the re-check → close. **Failure branch:** the repair
fails or the guest stays unhappy → escalation to the named third party ("let me get the manager
for you") — the worker does not settle it and does not contest it.

**E6 — Refusing the guest.** *(derived-confident — the sector's duty strand.)* The ask the house
must refuse: another drink for the visibly drunk guest, entry without ID, the table past
last orders, the fifth person on a four-person fire-capacity snug → the refusal, disowned to the
rule ("I can't serve you — it's my licence, not my choice") → the path handed over unbidden
("water's free, and I'll call you a taxi whenever you want") → absorbed, or the person contested
("you served him!") → drop the self, hold the rule, name the forum ("you're welcome to speak to
the manager — she'll tell you the same"). **Failure branch:** the press escalates → ejection via
the process, never the person ("I'm asking you to leave now; if you don't, the door staff will
walk you out") — and, at the rarest edge, the police as forum.

**E7 — The narrated intrusion.** *(derived-confident; thin.)* Acting on the guest's space or
plate in their presence: the act announced and licence sought before it is done ("are you still
working on that, or can I take it?"; "housekeeping — is now a good time?") → granted or
counter-conditioned ("come back in ten?") → done, with the undergoing acknowledged → completion.
**Failure branch:** the guest halts it mid-act ("no — leave the glasses") → stop, ratify, resume
on their terms.

**E8 — The bill.** *(derived-confident.)* The reckoning produced → the line explained where asked
("the 12.5% is discretionary — I can take it off, genuinely") → pay-request → payment → receipt
and close. **Failure branch:** a line contested ("we only had two bottles") → the record decides
(the tab, the till roll), corrected without contest of the person, or held with grounds.

**E9 — The guest's trouble.** *(derived-confident.)* The trouble declared ("there's no hot water
in 204"; "I've lost my key card"; "the room next door is having a party") → elicited → what will
be done, by whom, by when — via the named third party ("maintenance are on until ten — I'll have
them up within the half hour") → the commitment bounded honestly ("if they can't fix it tonight,
I'll move you — we have one twin left") → banked, and the LOOP CLOSED LATER ("did they sort
you out?"). **Failure branch:** the fix fails → redress (the move, the comp) and the apology on
behalf, without self-blame theatre.

**E10 — The booking on the phone.** *(derived-confident.)* The proposal ("Saturday, eight
o'clock, six of us") → declined-with-account-and-counter until a fit is found ("nothing at eight
— I can do 6:30 or 9:15") → the constraints taken and RECORDED (the cot, the wheelchair, the
birthday, the allergy) → the read-back (date, name spelled, number) → the terms signposted ("we
hold the table fifteen minutes; card to secure it, only charged if nobody shows") → close.
**Failure branch:** no fit exists → the refusal with the alternative ("we're fully booked —
our sister place on Duke Street usually has space").

**E11 — The occasion disclosure.** *(derived-confident as a type; frequency constructed.)*
Business in progress → the guest steps out of the frame, unbidden ("it's our first trip since my
husband died") → acknowledged WITHOUT FIXING — where the hospitality pull, precisely, is to fix
by UPGRADING ("let me send up champagne"), which converts a person into a service ticket → the
discloser closes the aside → business resumes. **Failure branch:** the violation-by-fixing
committed → the recovery is the same acknowledge-without-fixing position, taken late.

**E12 — The shift handover.** *(derived-confident — named in the commission as a strand.)* The
outgoing worker to the incoming: the state of the floor enumerated ("9's on desserts and they're
comped — check on them; 12 is a nut allergy, it's on the ticket; the couple at the window are
pre-theatre, they need the bill by 7:20") → what is preserved and where it lives (the log, the
diary, the till float counted) → the open tasks handed FORWARD with their re-entry conditions →
read back → accepted. **Failure branch:** the incoming worker was not told → the record is the
arbiter ("it's in the book — look"), and the gap is repaired guest-side without blame theatre.

**E13 — The pre-service briefing.** *(derived-confident; ordering constructed.)* The lineup:
tonight's specials and their contents → the 86 list → the allergens flagged → the VIPs and the
occasions ("table 15 is an anniversary — dessert plate written, on the house") → constraints
elicited ("who's never run the terrace? — right, you're with Marta") → questions → service.
Delivered one-to-many; the load-bearing exchanges run dyadically per hearer, exactly as trades
found for the site briefing. **Failure branch:** a hearer did not take it in → re-run
dyadically, mid-service, at speed.

**E14 — The 86 call and the pass.** *(derived-confident.)* Kitchen to floor: "86 the sea bass" →
acknowledged and RELAYED (every server now carries F6 to every table) → floor to kitchen: the
sequencing negotiation ("I need mains on 12 held — they're mid-speech") → granted, bounded, or
refused with an account and counter ("five minutes I can do; twenty and it's re-fired and
you're paying the chef in pints"). **Failure branch:** the relay fails — a server sells a dead
dish → the guest-side recovery is E5's machinery; the staff-side is the own-fault admission at
the pass.

**E15 — The stop call at the pass.** *(derived-confident as a type; ordering constructed.)* The
allergy catch: "WAIT — 12 is the nut allergy, is that the satay? Don't send it" → halted,
ratified without offence, re-verified from the ticket, re-fired or re-plated → resumed. Runs in
both directions across the learner's seat: the learner stops, and is stopped. **Failure
branch:** the stop is resented mid-rush ("it's fine, it's the almond oil—") → the rank-free
hold: on allergens the stop call outranks the whites, and the shape must survive the heat of
service.

**E16 — Cabin talk.** *(derived-confident.)* The staff-room and the post-shift drink: the
banter, the shared lines, the digressions, the shift post-mortem ("that table 9 though" — "tell
me about it"), the mutual assessment after a bad service. Listed to say where the rung-0
stretches live: inside the shift's own day, exactly as trades found — the doorstep tea's
equivalent is the fire-escape cigarette. **Failure branch:** none — rung 0 has no business to
fail.

## 5. The ladder — placement ruled, proxy holding checked, and one node-death at a kind

Using the recorded test throughout: **a position appears, disappears or changes hands — not
merely gets harder or dearer.**

**K1 — pinned to the worker, SHALLOW.** The worker knows the stock, the kitchen state, the house
— POD-1's own rung-1 definition, now occupied from the inside. But nothing is hidden behind a
wall: verification costs a walk to the pass, which is exactly why bare F15 converts to N105
(§4.1) and why F501 the exhibit finds no work here — the K1-holder collapses the asymmetry by
CHECKING, not by showing. The guest holds their own share (the allergy, the occasion, the
constraint), elicited and RECORDED — split, like Ireland's, and the guest's share decides more
than the customer's did in trades: the allergy re-routes the whole kitchen.

**K2 — the sector variable: formally rotating, POLICY-pinned to the guest.** Run the test on the
worker's seat: the refusal position is genuinely MISSING from it for almost the whole encounter —
not dearer, missing. A server facing an unreasonable demand does not refuse; they escalate
(N105's named third party) or absorb. That is a position held by the world's pinning in every
sense that matters to the learner, and the pin is *the customer is always right* — a COMMERCIAL
policy, not a statute. The position reappears in the worker's seat only where a second principal
overrides the first: the licensing law, the fire capacity, the ID check — and there it arrives
pre-disowned ("it's my licence, not my choice"), which is why E6 lands so exactly on N401,
Ireland's counter in an apron. **The customer-is-always-right is not a shape and mints nothing:
it is K2 pinned by policy, and its effects are all at kind level** — F17-style contest of the
guest's account is unavailable in complaint stretches, N201's discretionary refusal belongs to
the house not the worker, and the complaint encounter (E5) runs entirely on repair moves with
the contest positions deleted from one seat.

**K3 — pinned to the house:** the booking system, the tab, the till roll, the key-card log, the
ticket rail. Two consequences, both findings. First, **N103 revives** — the desk holds a
PERSON-file, so the known-answer check ("name on the reservation?") attests strongly, completing
the Family A dropout pattern at three data points: Ireland lost N102 (no body), trades lost N103
(no person-file), hospitality holds both possessions thinly-to-strongly and holds both shapes.
**Family A's membership tracks the sector's possessions, position-perfectly, for the third
consecutive sector.** Second, **N16 precision-haggle dies guest-facing at K3** — the first
Method-Pod NODE to die at a kind, mirroring the move deaths: the menu price is held by the

**K4 — weak, and split.** No body on the table (personal care was scoped out precisely so this

**Proxy holding, checked as instructed.** Hospitality holds it in a form neither Ireland nor
trades showed: **two principals whose instructions CONFLICT.** The house says the guest is
right; the law says refuse the drink. Ireland's official held everything in trust from one
statutory principal; the tradesperson held K1 personally and one override in trust from the

to ask") — and at the drink refusal the two trusts collide. Verdict: **`held_in_trust` covers

and the collision is a walk-level fact (the worker citing the licence AGAINST the
customer-is-right register is one sentence: "I know, and I'm sorry — but it's my licence").
That a principal can be commercial rather than statutory changes nothing at shape level, and
recording it needed no new machinery. The tronc, the brand standard, the mystery-diner — all
the same annotation.

**Rungs, per stretch** (fifth corpus for the per-stretch reading): rung 0 — the cabin, the
fire-escape, the regulars' banter; rung 1 — E2/E3/E8 routine service, which IS POD-1 occupied

## 6. The second axis — tested, and Ireland's hole looked for

The candidate: weight the overlay selector by the cost of failing to recover, shape-level.

**Where the weight lands: on a RELAY, which is new.** Hospitality's costliest failure is the
allergen death, and no single guest-facing shape carries it — the weight distributes along the
chain N108 (declared and recorded) → the ticket → E14's relayed F6 → E15's stop call
(N106/O101) → E12's handover (N402). **The first sector where the highest-cost link is
worker-to-worker rather than worker-to-public**: the guest-facing declaration is the cheapest
link in its own chain, and the failure that kills is a dropped baton in the back-of-house.
Shape-level weighting survives this — each link is a shape and each carries its share — but the
finding for the selector is that cost-of-failure can be a property of a CHAIN of shapes across
encounters, not of any one shape. Low-cost shapes inside the high-cost chain stay low-cost
(the F14 rituals around the allergy ask carry no weight); the weight tracks shapes, fourth
sector running. **Verdict: supported, shape-level, with the relay refinement recorded.**

**Ireland's hole, looked for as instructed — found, in both of its forms.** The allergy never
declared produces no encounter until the failure itself; the drink that should have been
refused and never was produces no conversation at all — the guest who drives home over-served
is trades' badly-earthed circuit in a car park. No shape weight can reach a failure that
consists of never speaking. And the sector shows the SAME FILL trades recorded, now the third
case on file: the allergen solicit is mandated — "any allergies or intolerances at the table?"
is an encounter put into the service script by law (UK/IE allergen-information rules), made to
run on a schedule precisely so the silent risk speaks — and it maps entirely onto existing
shapes (a scripted N108 opening). The over-service side has its weaker analogue in mandatory
responsible-service training scripts. The pattern across three sectors is now stable enough to
name for the selector: **the axis's blind zone is filled by mandating the encounter, never by
minting a shape.**
