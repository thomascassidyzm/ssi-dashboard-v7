## 2. The innocent-out — settled by building, as commissioned

The open call: is **repair-without-admission** a distinct response position, or a cheaper version
of one that already exists? The previous worker refused the mint and flagged the refusal as their
least confident. The walk stages it at R19 and the answer is structural, not argued:

**No mint. It composes.** Three pieces of evidence from the authored page:

1. **Three exits run off one identical turn.** R19's flows 1, 2 and 3 open with the same challenge
   sentence — "I don't think the scanner got the things in the bottom of the trolley, did it?" —
   and continue into the innocent repair, the offence, and the wrongful stop. A position is
   individuated by what can follow it; if the innocent-out were a distinct position, the three
   continuations would need distinct opening turns. They don't. One challenge position, three
   attested continuations: that is a branch set, not two shapes.
2. **The repair turn fills an existing family.** The customer's natural line self-attributes the
   miss as error — "No, it didn't — the water and the cat food, look. I never lifted them out" —
   which is F203's family exactly: fault-as-mistake, no excuse, remedy in the next breath. The
   admission that never appears is the admission of *offence* — and no store shape has ever had an
   offence-confession position (O6's "admit it" is admit-the-mistake), so there is no
   with-admission sibling for a without-admission position to be distinct from.
3. **What the frame actually moves is who supplies the account.** Under a bare accusation the
   accused produces the account; under the innocent-out the challenger hands it over ready-made
   ("it misses down there all the time") and the accused merely ratifies it. Who fills a position
   was ruled walk annotation, not shape, by the mapping's own K3 finding (the receipt changing
   hands) — the same ruling closes this.

The frame is K4 management by register — the sector's dearest moment handled without one new
position, which is why R19 is the walk's ceiling scene and admits nothing. **Attested lines:**
R19 flow 1 turns 1–2 (the frame and the repair), flow 3 turns 2–3 (the frame collapsing into the
wrongful stop and the script-dropped ownership). Id range 15xx stays empty.

## 7. The pattern — deltas for trades, hospitality, care-work and Ireland

What a from-nothing authoring job needs that an extraction job did not, what the health exemplar
assumes that does not transfer, and the sentences the next briefs should carry.

**D1 — the mapping is a scene list, read upside down.** An extraction job reads dialogue and names
shapes; a from-nothing job reads the mapping's encounter inventory and inverts it: each
encounter's failure branch IS a scene's branch set, and the "also traversed" column is the
admission budget. Retail's §3 transferred to a scene list almost 1:1.
*Brief sentence: "Read your sector's mapping §3 as a scene list: one scene per encounter, the
failure branch is the branch set, and the mapping's 'also traversed' column is your admission
budget."*

**D2 — the admission order is the constraint extraction never meets.** Aran wrote freely and was
measured afterwards; from nothing you must sequence so every scene uses only what earlier scenes
admitted. Three scenes swapped places once dialogue showed a position leaking in early (F203
before F501; the flag split out of the plain check; the hold kept out of the card-declined sale).
*Brief sentence: "Draw the admission order before writing a line — list the positions your mapping
says the sector adds, sequence them, and expect two or three scenes to swap once the dialogue
shows a position arriving early."*

**D3 — write the dearest scene first, as a test.** The apex scene should compose entirely from
positions the walk has already admitted; if it can't, that's a missing earlier scene — or, once
per sector at most, a real mint. Retail's security stop needed nothing, and proving that settled
the sector's one open mint question as a by-product.
*Brief sentence: "Write your sector's dearest scene first: if it needs a position no earlier scene
admits, you've found a missing scene — or your sector's one honest mint candidate."*

**D4 — health's register is the exception, not the template.** Health's professional is never
wrong on-page, so a brief written from health under-commissions worker-fault scenes. Retail's
worker is wrong on-page five times (the wrongful stop, the double scan, the stale ticket, the
lying system, the log that told nobody) and that is where the sector's teaching weight sits.
Trades and hospitality are retail-like here, care work is health-like — decide which BEFORE
writing.
*Brief sentence: "Decide first whether your sector's register permits the learner-seat being wrong
on-page; if it does, the fault-owned scenes are your weight, and if it doesn't, your O4/O6/O9
gaps are structural and should be declared, not forced."*

**D5 — the defer target is now three layers, and it grows.** Health resolved openers against
scene 0 only. Retail had to resolve against scene 0 AND health's seed set — ZUT at the canonical
layer means the second walk to stage an act consolidates to the first walk's form or names the
difference. Each next walk inherits a bigger defer set and mints less; retail's openers minted one
act where health's minted fourteen.
*Brief sentence: "Resolve your openers against scene 0 and against every landed sibling walk's
seed set before minting anything: the second walk to stage an act consolidates to the first, or
names the difference in one line."*

**D6 — the floor is passable wherever rung 0 exists.** Health's story-with-challenge failure is
not a law of professional registers — it is the absence of a rung-0 stretch inside the sector's
world. A till, a van cab, a staff room and a hotel bar all have one.
*Brief sentence: "Find your sector's rung-0 stretch before conceding any floor item; stage the
hard floor items there, in banter, where the register licenses them."*

**D7 — recoveries: still never commission edge-by-edge.** Confirmed from the authoring side:
19 of 19 arrived with the branch, unasked. Commission by name only the register-resistance list
(§4) — retail's was O2-bald, M4, N16.

**D8 — what does NOT transfer from retail:** the zero-mint result. The curve (10 → 2 → 1 → 0)
is the accumulated store doing its job, not a promise. Care work already holds F601 and re-derived
N501; a sector whose duty overrides consent should still expect zero or one mint, but the ONE is
real when it comes. The pattern is "compose first, mint once, refuse ten times" — not "never mint."

- **This is not the mapping's strong-form test.** The mapping said the real test is "a retail
  corpus authored blind by someone who has not read §3." I read §3 as my commission — so this walk
  can INSTANTIATE the mapping but cannot independently confirm it. What it does add beyond the
  mapping: the lines exist, they read aloud, the admission ordering is proven writable, and the
  innocent-out survived contact with authored dialogue on structural grounds (three exits, one
  turn) the derived argument didn't have.
- No shop worker said any of these lines; every flow is constructed. The labels follow health's
  conventions (⚠, path tags); the register is deliberately culture-light — no scheme names, no
  currency symbols, no place names, languages deictic throughout.
- **The one blemish I know of:** R19 flow 2 ends on the customer's flat "Let's get it over with,
  then" — the offence exit closes into a process the page doesn't follow. Deliberate (the office
  conversation is a different encounter and arguably a different walk), but it is the walk's only
  flow that ends without warmth, and I'd rather flag it than have it found.
- **Not done, deliberately:** no store or proposal file modified (15xx reserved, unused); no DB
  reads or writes; no audio; no seed cutting (that is the downstream seed-set job, which inherits
  §3's one mint candidate and one consolidation question); no fan-out — one voice wrote every
  line, because one voice is what made health good; no test suite (prose verified by reading — the
  read-aloud pass was the gate, per the walk bar).
