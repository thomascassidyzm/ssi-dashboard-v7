# Care work mapped onto the shape metagraph — the near-neighbour that was made to earn it

**Date:** 2026-08-30. **Sector:** care work — UK/Ireland domiciliary care and residential care of
older and disabled adults; the near neighbour of health. **Status:** production mapping under the
licensed fan-out, not a pilot. **Learner seat:** the CARE WORKER (ruled in §2). **Proposal file:**
`services/shared/metagraph/proposed/care-work-2026-08-30.json`, id range **601–699**.

*The port verdict, readable cold, is §10. The one-paragraph version: care work maps onto the
existing trunk at a cost of **zero nodes and one move**, and its duty-overrides-consent strand —
the reason this sector was worth mapping at all — lands on the SAME node trades minted (N501
Compelled make-safe) for its speakable half, while its heaviest half (best-interests decisions,
covert medication, deprivation of liberty) produces no in-room encounter at all and is decided in
a different room, on store shapes, between professionals. That is Ireland's no-encounter hole,
found again, in the sector where it costs the most.*

## 2. The seat, ruled

Care work has two seats worth naming: the **care worker**, and the **colleague receiving the
handover**. The pod is for the CARE WORKER, in both encounter families — with the cared-for
person and their family (E1–E13, E16) and with colleagues and professionals (E8, E14, E15, E17).
The handover seat is the same person on a different day of the rota: every worker gives and
receives handover, exactly as trades' stop call runs in both directions across one seat. Role
assignment is a walk annotation, not a shape distinction, and it absorbs this completely.

The cared-for person's seat is NOT the pod's seat, and one consequence is flagged now: several of
this sector's most important recoveries (surviving being refused, receiving a disclosure, holding
a bound confidence) are learner-side burdens precisely because the learner is the worker. A pod
for the cared-for person would be a different pod with a different survivability profile.

Sector defaults taken (flagged, not asked): UK/Ireland domiciliary-and-residential spread;
dementia care treated as core, not edge, because it is where the sector's shape questions live;
regulations (Care Act, Mental Capacity Act, DoLS) named only where they do shape work as
proxy-held duty, per the standing rule. Care vocabulary — the MAR sheet, the body map, the hoist,
the care plan — is overlay and never a node.

## 3. The encounter inventory — derived, and labelled as such

**Nobody has written this down. There is no care-work corpus in this estate.** The inventory
below is generated by this mapping, not looked up, and each entry is labelled
**derived-confident** (any competent care worker has this conversation weekly, and I can say why)
or **constructed** (reasoned into existence; may not survive contact with a real care worker).
The Ireland standard is the standard met: every attestation in the proposal file cites these
E-numbers and inherits their labels.

**E1 — Personal care, narrated.** *(derived-confident — the sector's hourly bread.)* The act on
the body announced before it is done ("I'm going to help you with your top now, is that alright?")
→ go-ahead taken, or watched for → the act narrated as it goes, sensation forecast ("water's a bit
warm — tell me if it's too much") → undergoing acknowledged → completion and aftercare. Interleaved
throughout with rung-0 talk — the weather, the grandchildren — because companionship is not a
garnish on the service, it is half the service. **Failure branch:** distress or a flinch mid-act →
halt, ratify, re-plan or re-approach.

**E2 — The medication round.** *(derived-confident.)* The identity-and-sheet check, even for a
person known for years, because the MAR demands it → the meds presented and named → taken,
witnessed, signed. **Failure branch:** "I don't take the white one in the mornings" → the halt —
stop, ratify the challenge, re-check against the MAR, withhold until certain. Runs in both seats:
the worker is also the one who says "these aren't Mrs Evans's" to a colleague.

**E3 — The refusal of care.** *(derived-confident — daily, and the sector's signature texture.)*
The offer or the due task → refused ("I'm not having a wash today, and you can't make me") → the
refusal ACCEPTED without contest, because autonomy is the governing rule ("fair enough, my lovely
— we'll leave it") → recorded ("I'll just pop it in the notes that you weren't feeling like it")
→ and RETURNED TO later, differently — after the tea, after the programme, with the other worker.
**Failure branch:** the refusals accumulate past a threshold (meds three days running, no food) →
escalation: the office, the district nurse, the family — the refusal itself now the reported fact.

**E4 — The doorway.** *(derived-confident as a type; ordering constructed.)* The person is set on
something unsafe — going out alone in January in a nightdress, climbing the stairs unaided,
cooking on a gas ring they forget. Two forks by capacity. **With capacity:** the worker cannot
prevent, so the duty flips to warn-and-record — the risk named plainly, the advice given, the
telling recorded ("I can't stop you, but I have to say I don't think it's safe, and I'm going to
let the office know I said so"). **Without capacity (or while it is in doubt):** deflect and
substitute in the same breath — "let's have a cup of tea first and I'll come out with you after"
— while the real decision (the lock, the sensor, the DoLS authorisation) is made in another room
by other people. **Failure branch of the second fork:** the deflection fails and the person is at
the door → the body in the doorway and the phone in the hand, and almost none of it is talk.

**E5 — The disclosure.** *(derived-confident.)* Mid-task, unbidden, the person steps out of the
frame: loneliness, fear of a family member, pain long unmentioned, "I do sometimes think I'd
rather not wake up." Received and marked as heard, unfixed, the business resuming when the person
closes it. **The threshold branch, which is the sector's hinge:** when the content crosses the
safeguarding line — harm, abuse, suicidal intent — the receiving move flips: the telling is
ratified ("I'm really glad you've told me"), the limit on confidence is declared, and the
disclosure converts into E6. Often the person asks for the promise first — "you won't tell
anyone, will you?" — and the promise is BOUNDED, not given: "I can't promise that, sweetheart. If
it's something that worries me I have to pass it on. But you can still tell me."

**E6 — The safeguarding pass-on.** *(derived-confident as a duty; wording constructed.)* The
worker must report what they have learned or seen, against the person's expressed wish. The
concern named against its norm ("that bruising isn't from a fall, and I'm not able to keep it to
myself") → the pass-on announced and DONE — consent not sought, refusal unavailable ("it goes to
the safeguarding team today; that's not a choice I get to make") → the record made → what happens
next named ("someone will come and talk to you — you won't be on your own for it"). **Failure
branch:** the press on the person — "I trusted you. You've ruined everything." → drop the self,
hold the duty, name the forum ("you can be as cross with me as you like and I'll still come
Tuesday. The safeguarding team are the ones who decide what happens — not me").

**E7 — The soft-signs escalation.** *(derived-confident — the sector's clinical front line.)*
"She's not herself today" — the worker holds no diagnosis and no instruments, only pattern-knowledge
of one person, and must convert a gut reading into an escalation that gets uptake: the call to the
office or the district nurse, the observation reported with its epistemic price stated ("I can't
put my finger on it — she's just not right; she didn't want her toast and she always wants her
toast") → elicit chains from the professional → the undertaking banked ("the duty nurse will ring
you within the hour") or the conditional instruction taken ("if she's drowsy by lunch, 999, don't
wait for us"). **Failure branch:** the report is not taken up → persist, re-anchor to the
concrete, invoke the record ("it's in the notes three days running").

**E8 — The handover.** *(derived-confident.)* Shift to shift, worker to worker: what happened,
what changed, what must happen next, read back, and WRITTEN — because the night carer acts on the
notes, not on the memory. **Failure branch:** the read-back is wrong or the notes are thin → the
gap surfaces at 3am in someone else's shift, which is why the record position is drilled.

**E9 — The family update.** *(derived-confident.)* The daughter on the phone or at the door: the
report referenced to the person's own baseline ("she's eating less than she was — for your mum
that's a change") → the guarantee sought ("but she's alright, isn't she? You'd tell me?") → given
or explicitly bounded ("I won't promise what I can't be sure of — but she's comfortable and she's
in good spirits") → sometimes the apology on behalf ("I'm sorry nobody rang you back on Tuesday —
that shouldn't have happened; I'm flagging it now"). **Failure branch:** the family's anger at the
institution lands on the worker present → the fault is neither owned personally nor deflected
coldly; sorry-on-behalf, record, route.

**E10 — The family instruction against the person's wishes.** *(derived-confident.)* "Don't let
Dad have biscuits — I mean it." But Dad has capacity, and Dad wants his biscuit. The worker
refuses the instruction, disowning the refusal to the rule it comes from ("it's his choice — while
he understands it, I can't take it off him; that's the law, not me being awkward") → the route
named ("if you're worried, raise it at the review — the care manager can look at it properly").
**Failure branch:** the family contest the worker personally → drop the self, hold the rule, name
the forum.

**E11 — The out-of-scope ask.** *(derived-confident.)* "Just witness my will, you're the only one
I trust." "Take twenty pounds from my purse for yourself, go on." "Could you do the windows while
you're here?" → refused, disowned to the rules ("I'm not allowed, my love — not because I don't
want to; it's the rules and I'd lose my job") → the legitimate route named (the office, the
solicitor, the extra-hours request). **Failure branch:** the person is hurt by the refusal → the
relationship repaired without the rule moving.

**E12 — The reality question.** *(derived-confident in dementia care, which is core.)* "When's my
husband coming?" — and he died in 2003. "Where's my mum? Why isn't she here?" The truthful answer
is an injury delivered hourly; the false grant is a betrayal that eventually collides with the
world; and the sector's actual taught practice is a third thing: **answer the need, not the
question** — the premise neither corrected nor endorsed, the state underneath it addressed ("you
miss him, don't you. Tell me how you two met."). **Failure branch:** the question returns,
verbatim, minutes later, dozens of times a day → the same move, the same warmth, every time —
repetition without escalation is the recovery.

**E13 — The errand purse.** *(derived-confident.)* Shopping with the person's money: the list, the
till, and the accounting — every receipt kept, every amount written and countersigned, because the
money is not the worker's and not really the encounter's either; it is held in trust and the
record is the trust's instrument. **Failure branch:** a discrepancy → the record decides, which is
why the record exists.

**E14 — The professionals' room.** *(derived-confident as a type; ordering constructed.)* The
review, the best-interests meeting, the MDT: the worker — often the person in the room who knows
the person best and ranks lowest — reports observations, takes positions, is disagreed with by
people with letters after their names, and either holds or parks the disagreement upward. This is
the room where the sector's gravest business is transacted: the decision to medicate covertly, to
lock the door, to move the person — decided ABOUT the person, away from the person, on colleague
shapes. **Failure branch:** the worker's report is discounted for rank → the record and the
specifics are the counter, never the credentials.

**E15 — The office and the rota.** *(derived-confident.)* "Can you fit in a call to Mrs H?" — the
feasibility answer with its limit ("I can, but it makes me forty minutes late to the Erskines");
the visit that cannot complete ("there's no hot water in the flat — I can't do the shower; I've
done everything else and I've flagged the boiler to housing") — the state preserved, the task
routed. **Failure branch:** the undeliverable promise pressed for → bound it, in writing.

**E16 — The death.** *(derived-confident as a type; ordering constructed.)* The worker who finds
the person: the call made calmly to the office and the ambulance service; the family summoned with
bounded information ("the paramedics are with him now — I think you should come"); the
verification and its news explicitly routed to those licensed to give it ("the doctor will talk
to you as soon as she's finished — I'll stay with you till then"); the condolence, which is
ritual and real at once. **Failure branch:** the family ask the worker the question directly —
"he's gone, isn't he?" — → the bounded honest answer that neither confirms what the worker may
not confirm nor pretends ignorance ("I don't want to guess at what's the doctor's to say — she'll
be out in a minute. Come and sit with me").

**E17 — The new-client assessment.** *(constructed — usually the assessor's job, but workers do
first calls.)* The elicit chains: what can you manage yourself, what matters to you, what must we
never do → the standing constraints declared and durably recorded (the allergy, the no-male-carers
request, the cat that must never get out) → the shape of visits signposted → the plan read back.
**Failure branch:** the person under-reports need out of pride → the anchored elicit ("walk me
through yesterday, from waking up") — need read off the narrative, not the self-assessment.

## 6. The ladder — placement ruled, and where the position test actually fires

**Care work sits at rung 4 — health's rung. K4 is the body, pinned.** The near-neighbour claim is
confirmed at the ladder: same rung, same stake. Everything distinctive about the sector is in
HOW the other three kinds sit.

**K1 — SPLIT, with a drift the ladder has not recorded before.** The worker holds the plan, the
MAR, today's observations; the person holds their body and their biography, and their share
decides (Ireland's split, on the body instead of the circumstances). The care-work addendum:
**the person's K1 share degrades with the condition and migrates** — into the record, into the
family, into the worker's pattern-knowledge of them. Trades time-indexed K1 within one job;
care work condition-indexes it across years. Same annotation machinery, longer clock.

**K2 — the sector variable: PINNED TO THE OTHER PARTY.** This is the first mapped sector where
the learner's seat holds LESS refusal-right than the seat opposite, in nearly every encounter:
autonomy gives the person an absolute per-encounter refusal ("you can't make me" — and the worker
cannot), while duty of care strips the worker's walk-away — the barista can decline a customer,
the officer can close the counter, the electrician can refuse the job; the care worker cannot
refuse to care. Two consequences, both tested against the recorded rule ("a position appears,
disappears or changes hands — not merely gets harder or dearer"):

1. **The undischargeable close.** The person's refusal is real but cannot be TERMINAL — they can
   refuse today's wash, not resign from care; and the worker can accept today's refusal but
   cannot close the duty. N201's settle position is unavailable to BOTH parties at the
   relationship level. Per-encounter, though, every walk composes: F202 + F103 + the same ask
   re-walked later. So the ladder note records a genuinely new K2 configuration — refusal pinned
   per-encounter to the person, terminal-close pinned to neither — while the walks stay on
   existing shapes. A kind-configuration novelty with zero shape cost: the clearest evidence in
   this document that the ladder and the node inventory are genuinely different layers.

2. **Where the test fires, it fires on other people's mints.** At the safeguarding threshold the
   person's refusal position DISAPPEARS — that is N501, trades' node, firing identically (the act
   is a telling instead of a disconnection; content is the mask). At capacity loss the person's
   consent position CHANGES HANDS — but to an off-stage forum, and the conversation in which it
   changes hands is E14, between professionals, on N8/N15/N108. The sector's two position-test
   events cost zero mints because trades and health already paid for them.

**K3 — pinned to the worker and the agency**, and load-bearing beyond any sector yet mapped
except Ireland: the notes ARE the continuity of care (the night carer acts on the record, not on
memory), F103 saturates, F18 dies to the MAR inside the round, disputes at E13 and E14 are
decided by the record. The record is of the PERSON — which is exactly why N103 re-attests here
after failing in trades: care work has the person-file trades lacked.

**Money — not a kind here, and barely a presence.** The worker never holds the price and rarely
touches the cash; when they do (E13) it is held in trust with the record as the trust's
instrument. Trades' both-ways argument does not need re-running — the sector offers no bench to
run it on, which is itself weak confirmatory evidence for trades' ruling that money was never a
