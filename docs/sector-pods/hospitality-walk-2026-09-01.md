# The hospitality themed walk — authoring record

**2026-09-01.** The hospitality walk's canonical English dialogue now exists:
`docs/sector-pods/source/hospitality-conversations-v1.md` — **21 contexts, 55 six-turn flows,
330 turns**, authored from nothing in one sitting, one voice. Before this file the sector had a
mapping and no corpus (`proposed/hospitality-2026-08-30.json`: `"corpus": "NONE"`). This
document is the accounting that travels with it: what the walk covers, what it defers to the
floor, what it mints (nothing), the roles it yields, and where it deliberately closes gaps the
estate has on record. No store file was touched, no DB was read or written, no audio exists or
was queued.

**The walk is named by what it masks — hospitality — and is never numbered.** Its selector is
interest: someone picks it because they work a bar, or because they eat in restaurants and
want the other seat's view, or because they like the register. Sector is one reason among
several.

## 2. The opener resolution — defer/mint, per turn

Per the floor-inheritance ruling, the walk carries the shared register from the
every-optional-walk floor and mints only what it adds. Health's yardstick: 30 opener turns,
16 defer / 14 mint. This walk's two openers (1.0 × 3 flows + 2.0 × 2 flows) are likewise 30
turns. Resolved against scene 0 (W1201–W1204, W1401) and the CORE floor
(`docs/pods/core-pod-seed-set-2026-08-31.md`): **17 defer, 13 mint.**

Defer (17): the offer declaration and fallback (three instances — W1201.1's position, near
surface-identical); the repair licence "just tell me — I'd rather be told" (W1201.3) and its
re-priced pass form; the yield-instantly "Understood. Service in mine, no argument" (W1202.3);
the grant + reassure and "We will, don't worry" (N1201.2, CP013's family); the biography
curiosity question and the drinks orders in guests' mouths (W1204.2; CP072/CP075/CP078/CP081);
the drinks solicit (the pod's own promoted drill "Would you like to order some drinks?"); the
capacity decline and the concrete re-offer licence in the chef's mouth (W1202.2, W1202.4's
positions, seat-appropriate); the notice-form opener (W1401's position).

Mint (13), which is what the walk ADDS to the shared register: the service self-frame ("I'll
be looking after you tonight"); the working-here rationale; the **domain-scoped grant with a
read-back promise** ("anything about the allergy, we do in mine, and I'll say it back to you")
— the staff-seat answer to W1203, with the allergy as hospitality's dates-and-numbers; the
kitchen-check commitment (N105's staff debut); the rush-scoped fallback self-imposed ("I'll
switch to mine rather than slow the pass down"); the pass protocol pair ("clear beats clever",
"numbers first: table, then dish"); the receipt-and-posting ("Table, then dish. Got it. Where
do you want me tonight?"); the guest-proposed stakes carve-out and its allergy disclosure; the
tease-grant ("you'll get no mercy from this table"); and the two-principal safety rule spoken
("allergy calls stay in whatever the whole kitchen understands first time — that one's not
about your learning").

**The deixis rule, verified line by line:** every retreat in the corpus proposes the LEARNER'S
language — "can we do the allergy in yours?" (1.0 f3), "we do service in yours" (2.0 f2) — and
no line anywhere proposes the speaker's own. The two accounts scene 0 ratified both re-attest
from new seats: capacity (the pass, mid-rush) and stakes scoped to a domain (the allergy).

## 5. Outcomes the prior registers resisted, reached here

The health calibration's instruction: commission by name what a register resists. Health's
professional frame blocked O4, O9, S6, M3 and dialogic O6. Hospitality's frame licenses most
of them, and the corpus stages each:

- **O4, the wrong read-back corrected** — never reached in 73 health flows, because the
  professional is never wrong. A busy desk mishears bookings weekly: "Saturday the eighth, at
  seven." / "No, love — Friday the seventh." / "Friday the seventh — thank you, good catch.
  That's why I say it back." (1.11 f2). The learner is wrong, corrected, and recovers with the
  read-back's own rationale.
- **O9 / S6, the second no** — never reached in health (accepting the first no IS that
  register). The refusal stretch is where a second no lives: "I've said no twice now, and I
  mean it kindly, but it's finished." (1.7 f3), escalating to process, never to the person.
- **O6 dialogic, trouble that is your own fault** — "My fault: table nine ordered the bass
  after the 86… I'll tell them straight it was my mistake, not the kitchen's." / "Right
  answer, twice." (2.2 f3) — admission, absolution, consequence accepted ("the re-fire's on
  me"), plus the standard held ("stop ordering on autopilot — you're better than that").
- **O3 with the seats reversed** — the mapping's store-reach note, staged at 1.14: the first
  walk where the learner performs the fluent side's recovery (reformulate, point, check it
  landed) for a struggling other — and hands back the encouragement every learner has needed
  ("your words are doing the job — that's all words are for").

Still unreached, honestly: **O2** bald nobody-knows (the register routes instead — "let me
check with the kitchen" is N105, the sector workhorse, exactly as the mapping predicted);
**M4** disagreement parked (the cabin settles its disagreements — 2.5 f2's is settled by
verdict, not parked); **N16** the haggle (dead guest-facing at K3 per the mapping's node-death
ruling — every number in this corpus is held by the till, the menu or the book, and nobody
bids). These absences are the register telling the truth, not holes to patch.

## 7. Mints: zero — and the 17xx range goes unused

## 8. Honesty section

- **Authored, not transcribed.** No waiter said any line here; there is still no observed
  hospitality corpus. What changed today: the sector now has DIALOGUE — the thing the walk
  census named as the bottleneck — authored against the derived inventory, by one mind in one
  sitting. The strong-form test the mapping asked for (a corpus authored blind by someone who
  had not read §3) is NOT what happened: I read everything first, deliberately, because the
  brief was coverage. A blind-authored corpus would still be worth commissioning as a check.
- **The bar applied:** every line was read aloud against "would a real person say this?", and
  the six-turn arcs were written as conversations first, coverage second. Where a mapping
  line was used near-verbatim ("it's my licence, not my choice"), it was used because it
  already passed that bar, not to tick its box.
- **Constructed lines carried over:** several surfaces originate in the mapping's constructed
  examples. They were constructed there and remain constructed here; frequencies are still
  unclaimed.
- **What was not done:** no store or proposal file modified; no DB reads or writes; no audio
  and no audio-pass queued (canonical layer — there is nothing to render); no seed-set
  projection (the analogue of health's 85-turn general projection is the natural next job,
  and §6's counts are its input); no walk-JSON encoding (health's corpus lives unencoded too;
  encoding is the ingest job's decision, per the census's one-sentence fix); no test suite —
  this is prose, verified by reading it.
- **Scale note:** 55 flows against Aran's 73. The difference is deliberate breadth-first
  coverage of E1–E16 at 2–3 flows each rather than 3 everywhere; contexts that would take a
  third flow without new positions (1.2, 1.4, 1.8, 1.12–1.14, 2.0, 2.1) are the natural
  place to grow v2 if Tom wants weight, and growing them touches no structure.
