# The Welsh pair overlay — health walk, general role

**Authored 2026-09-01. EVERY WELSH LINE IN THIS DOCUMENT IS DRAFT-FOR-ARAN.** Under the standing
text-approval policy (`docs/pods/text-approval-policy-2026-08-16.md`), no worker signs off
target-language text: nothing here renders, ships, or enters a course until Aran has read it.
**No audio pass is queued by this document, and none may be queued until the draft flag clears.**

**What this is:** the first pair-specific stage of the health walk — the Welsh target side of the
canonical general-role seed set (`health-general-seed-set-2026-08-31.md`, 57 seeds, 159 new
LEGOs). This is **frame-mapping, not translation**: the English known side owns the frames; this
document is the mapping table for the eng→cym_n pair, with every mapping classified
**deterministic / split / inversion / erasure**, every re-cut reported, and the places where the
curriculum relocates for Welsh named.

**Dialect assumption, stated up front: `cym_n` — northern colloquial Welsh, Aran's own.** The
binding target is the base course **`cym_n_for_eng`** (668 seeds, human-voiced, live in
Supabase). Every line uses northern forms throughout: *dw i / dach chi / dan ni*, *rŵan*, *efo*,
*isio*, *gen i*, *medru*, *deud*, *dallt*, *adra*, *chydig*, *ylwch*, *tŷ bach*, *ara deg*.
Lines where the north/south choice is **load-bearing** (a southern course could not reuse the
form) carry the mark 🏔 and are collected in §4.3.

**ZUT, the only gate, one-directional:** one English intention → exactly one Welsh form,
document-wide. Convergence (several knowns landing on one Welsh form) is reception and is
welcome; divergence kills, and every place English wanted to fork is resolved in §3 by
re-cutting, never by blocking a word. The production ledger in §1 was swept end-to-end for
same-known→two-targets; the sweep is clean.

**Welsh is human-voice only** (standing ruling): when this draft clears, its audio is a
recording worklist for Aran/Catrin, never a TTS queue.

## 0. Standing decisions — the register and the frame ledgers

These are the overlay-wide calls every seed below obeys. Each is one Aran-look to overturn.

**R1 — chi throughout to the patient.** The tu-first law says informal unless context insists;
my read is this context insists: every seed is first-meeting, duty-of-care speech from a
professional to an elderly stranger, which is exactly where a real Welsh HCA uses *chi* — and
ZUT wants one you-form course-wide anyway. The patient may *ti* the worker in dialogue; the
worker's production side is *chi*, always. **This is the register call of the whole overlay —
Aran's one look decides it.**

**R2 — the deixis resolves.** The canonical set names languages deictically ("this isn't my
first language", "someone who speaks it") so it instantiates in any pair. In the pair overlay
the deixis lands: *Cymraeg*. The learner speaking Welsh says Welsh isn't their first language;
anyone declining proposes English — the learner's fallback — never their own preference, per the
deixis rule.

**R3 — the frame ledger** (one Welsh frame per English frame, applied without exception):

| English frame | Welsh frame | seeds |
|---|---|---|
| "shall we X?" / "shall I X?" | "wnawn ni X?" / "wna i X?" | HG11, HG42, HG46 |
| "let's X" | "dewch i ni X" | HG10, HG15, HG24, HG29, HG52, HG56 |
| "let me X" | "gadwch i mi X" | HG36, HG39, HG44 |
| "would you like…?" | "fasach chi'n licio…?" 🏔 | HG38, HG41 (HG17 *liciech chi*) |
| "that's what X is for" | "dyna i be mae X" | HG15, HG25, HG57 |
| "X it is" (the ratifier) | "X amdani" | HG42, HG45 — binds to scene 0's yield |
| "I'm just going to X" (announce-then-act) | "dw i jyst yn mynd i X" | HG30 |
| "all done" / "there we are" / "for now" | "wedi gorffen" / "dyna ni" / "am rŵan" | HG34, HG37 |
| declarative future "we'll X / I'll X" | "mi + short future" ("mi wnawn ni", "mi fydda i", "mi ddo i") | pervasive |
| "please" + imperative | imperative + "plîs" | HG01 — provisional, binds to core overlay |
| need-family ("I need to / you need / no need to") | "angen" ("dw i angen / dach chi angen / dim angen") 🏔 | HG19, HG22, HG31, HG51, HG56 |

| "just" elsewhere | "jyst" | HG19, HG24, HG30, HG36, HG51 |

**R4 — the pace family, three idioms for three intentions** (differentiated, not converged):
"slowly" → *yn ara bach*; "nice and slow" → *yn ara deg* 🏔; "extra slow" → *yn ara deg iawn*;
"take it steady" → *mynd gan bwyll* 🏔. Four knowns, four forms, no fork.

**R5 — numbers are modern decimal** (*cant tri deg pump*, not vigesimal), matching what
`cym_n_for_eng` teaches and what an NHS ward actually says. Blood-pressure readings are read
digit-group-wise: "135 dros 80".

**R6 — borrowings are register, not laziness.** *tsiecio, sortio, jobsys, sbwnj, cwstard, jwg,
cwff, plîs, jyst, lot, saff, neis, reit* are how the ward actually talks in the north, and the
corpus's own patients say "bach" and "Righto". Where a borrow and a book-word compete, the
overlay takes the one Aran's corpus register would take, and flags it.

**R7 — one lexeme per production family:** check/checking/checks → *tsiec-* everywhere; the
bell → *y gloch*; use → *defnyddio* (not iwsio — flagged, Aran may prefer the borrow); visiting
→ *yr oriau ymweld* as the one head (HG27/HG54 standardised — see §3).

**R8 — grammatical gender rulings the pronouns forced** (each one Aran look): *poen* is
**feminine** (*y boen*, "ydy hi'n waeth…"); the bell *cloch* feminine (*dyna i be mae hi*); the
reading/BP referent masculine *o* ("mae o'n 135 dros 80"); the third-party helper is **female**
(*iddi hi*, *chi'ch dwy*, *ni'n tair*) — pinned by the canonical cast (HG14's "she"), see split
S3.

**R9 — baskets are not rendered yet, by design.** Every BUILD/USE phrase in the canonical set
composes from the chunks mapped here, so the basket Welsh falls out of this ledger mechanically —
but rendering ~900 draft phrases against an **unratified** ledger would multiply Aran's review
burden ninefold for no information. The baskets render as a follow-on pass the moment Aran
ratifies §1, re-verifying tiling in target per the canonical set's own decision 9. (Volume
floors are untouched: this defers rendering, it cuts nothing.)

Every mapped row above — the 159 new LEGOs plus the owned/tiling chunks the seeds pass through,

column shows chunk size is what keeps it cheap.

## 3. The splits — every fork found, and how each was resolved

Per the ruling: where an English chunk would map two ways, the unit is re-cut, never the word
blocked.

- **S1 — "then" forks three ways.** Sequential → *wedyn* (HG32); in-that-case → tag *'ta*
  (HG16, and inside *reit 'ta*); alongside "so" → *felly*. Resolution: "then" is never a free
  unit in this pair — it is absorbed into its carrying chunk in every seed, so no production
  fork survives.
- **S2 — "miss" forks.** Miss-out-on → *colli* (HG54); fail-to-catch → *methu* (HG57). The
  canonical cut already keeps the two in separate chunks; the overlay pins one lexeme per chunk.
- **S3 — "both / three of us" fork by gender.** *dau/dwy*, *tri/tair*. Resolved by pinning the
  canonical cast: the third party is female (HG14's "she"), so *chi'ch dwy* and *ni'n tair*,
  course-wide. **Hazard flagged:** any future male-helper variant is a different known
  (different world), not a re-mapping of this one.
- **S4 — "you" (ti/chi).** Resolved course-wide to *chi* (R1). The one register call of the
  overlay.
- **S5 — "know" (gwybod/nabod)** never reaches production: the only knowing-chunk inverts to
  *gair diarth i chi* (HG04).
- **S6 — "use" (defnyddio/iwsio)** is a register fork, not a sense fork: standardised
  *defnyddio* in both chunks (HG04, HG36); Aran may swap to *iwsio* everywhere in one word.
- **S7 — "visiting"** would have taken two heads (*ymweld* / *oriau ymweld*) across HG27/HG54;
  standardised to *yr oriau ymweld* in both.
- **S9 — "anything" forks** (*rhywbeth* in existential offers — HG01, HG22, HG51; *unrhyw beth*
  in free-choice topics — HG25, HG23's *unrhyw un*). Resolved exactly as the granularity ruling
  predicts: "anything" is never a unit; each carrying phrase maps whole, so both forms live
  without a fork. This is the single best exhibit for conversation-granularity in the pair.
