# The retail themed walk — canonical dialogue, authored from nothing
## Complete linear flows: the shop worker and the people at the counter

**Authored 2026-09-01, against `docs/sector-pods/retail-metagraph-mapping-2026-08-30.md` (encounters
E1–E16) and the ratified store at origin/main.** There was no retail corpus before this document —
`proposed/retail-2026-08-30.json` says so in its own words — so nothing here is transcribed speech:
every line was written for its scene. **Canonical English known side only**: no target language, no
pair, no course code, no dialect, no named country, currency or scheme. Languages are named
deictically ("your language" / "mine"), per scene 0's ratified convention.

**The learner seat is the retail worker** (the mapping's ruling). **W** = the worker, the learner
seat. **C** = a customer. **R** = the regular (a customer with history). **M** = the manager (Dee).
**P** = Priya, who does the decorating counter. **K** = a colleague on shift. **J** = Jack, the new
starter. **⚠** marks the safety-critical lines a learner must catch first time — here they cluster
at the allergen counter, the age gate and the recall, which is where the sector's cost lives.

**Structure.** A scene is a branch point with its continuations attached: each flow is one six-turn
arc, a coherent path a real encounter could take, and the flows of a scene are the attested response
family at its branch point. Each scene admits at most **one response position beyond what the
learner already owns** (CORE's 18 shapes, scene 0, and the every-optional-walk floor); the
**Admits** line names it, and names the existing id it resolves to. **Zero-admission scenes are
deliberate** — they are where owned positions compose under the sector's own pressure, and the walk
is built so that its hardest scene, the security stop, admits nothing at all.

**Scene 0 is inherited, not re-authored.** The medium contract (N1201) and its branches are
`walks/core-scene-0.json` (W1201–W1204) and `walks/core-scene-0-notice.json` (W1401). Scene R0
below re-instantiates the contract in the shop's own terms, and every turn of it is resolved
against those walks in the companion report — defers named as defers, sector-new material flagged.
