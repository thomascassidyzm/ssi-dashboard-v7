## Production Economics

**Monthly Budget:** £1,000
**Capacity:** 25-30 standard courses (250 seeds) per month
**Audio Reuse:** 40% cost savings on language family variants

**Example:** Spanish family (10 courses)
- First course (`spa_for_eng`): Full cost (~$5 TTS)
- Variants (`spa_for_fra`, `spa_for_deu`, etc.): ~$2 each
- **Total family:** ~$23 TTS + 18% Claude capacity

**Strategy:**
- Launch with 250-seed courses (fast time-to-market)
- Upgrade to 668-seed "Premium" tier in Q3-Q4
- Leverage audio reuse for 40% cost reduction

## Pricing Model

> **Updated 2026-07-29 (founder ruling — supersedes any earlier tier table):** two products, nothing else.

| Plan | Price | What's Included |
|------|-------|----------------|
| **Learner** | £15/mo | One learner — ALL languages, all premium courses |
| **Family** | £25/mo | Up to 6 learners (group model, one payer/admin) — ALL languages, all premium courses |

- A family is a GROUP (tutor/freelance shape): the payer is group admin, sees members' access; each member is a full learner identity.
- Positioning: **"Bring the language home"** — intergenerational language transmission.
- Free tier: community courses, 300 seeds (deliberate boundary).
- There is no per-language pricing and no Standard/Polyglot/Complete tier — any doc or code referring to those is stale.

## Marketing Strategy

### Core Positioning

**"Learn any language like building with LEGOs - recombine what you know into infinite possibilities."**

### Key Differentiators

1. **LEGO Method:** Unique recombination pedagogy (proven with Welsh)
2. **Any-to-any languages:** Learn Spanish from French, German, Italian (not just English)
3. **AI-powered efficiency:** 668 pedagogically-ordered seeds for optimal retention
4. **Mission-driven:** Free courses for endangered languages

### Marketing Mix (Q1-Q4)

**Q1-Q2: Foundation**
- Content marketing (blog, YouTube, podcasts)
- Social media (Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit)
- Email marketing (Welsh community conversion)
- Influencer partnerships (language YouTubers)

**Q3-Q4: Scale**
- B2B expansion (corporate L&D, schools, government)
- Paid acquisition (Google Ads, Facebook, YouTube)
- PR & media (tech publications, mainstream media)
- Community activation (language activists, user-generated content)

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| **Technical:** Audio pipeline failure | Multi-provider TTS (Azure + ElevenLabs), human voice fallback, QA workflow |
| **Technical:** Infrastructure scaling | Monitor costs, CDN caching, volume pricing negotiation |

| **Financial:** Production costs exceed budget | Fixed £1k/month budget, audio reuse (40% savings), defer low-demand courses |
